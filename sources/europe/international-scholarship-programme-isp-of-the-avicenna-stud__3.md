### Programme Description
The International Scholarship Programme (ISP) of the Avicenna Studienwerk, in cooperation with the Islamkolleg Deutschland, supports international Muslim students and doctoral candidates with funds from the Federal Foreign Office (Auswärtiges Amt, AA). The aim of the programme is to create optimal conditions for academic qualification, social commitment and personal development through financial and non-material support. 

### Target Group
  * enrolment for a Master's degree or doctorate at a German higher education institutions
  * all fields of study and subjects

### Academic Requirements
enrolment (or at least acceptance) for a Master's degree or doctorate at a German higher education institution 

### Number of Scholarships
16 

### Duration
The scholarships are awarded for the entire duration of the Master's programme (standard period of study). An extension is possible. Doctoral studies are funded for a maximum period of 3 years (36 months). 

### Scholarship Value
Financial support:
  * monthly scholarship for students 844 EUR / for doctoral candidates 1,190 EUR
  * monthly healthcare allowance: 100 EUR
  * monthly travel allowance: 100 EUR
The non-material support consists of individual counselling and participation in the educational programme of the Avicenna Studienwerk. 

### Application Papers
Application documents (to be submitted in German or English): informal letter of motivation (max. 2 pages), CV in table form (max. 2 pages), certificate of higher education entrance qualification (Abitur, A-Levels, etc.), university certificate and if applicable job references, current certificate of enrolment, proof of social involvement, 2 academic letters of recommendation, a copy of your passport and current transcript of records. Additionally for doctoral candidates: a short outline of your dissertation project (max. 3 pages) with information about the topic, supervisor(s), research question, current status, expected results 

### Application Deadline
The scholarships are awarded annually. Current deadlines can be found on the programme website (https://www.avicenna-studienwerk.de/scholarship/#ISP-en). 

### Application Requirements
  * above average potential for academic/research achievement
  * social, cultural or political involvement
  * enrolment (or at least acceptance) for a Master's degree or doctorate at a German higher education institution
  * all fields of study and subjects
  * German language proficiency (B2 level or above). Exceptions are possible.

### Bangladesh-eligibility verification (2026-06-18)
A live WebFetch of the DAAD detail page (https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/?detail=10000633) was used to read the JS-rendered status/country dropdown that gates eligibility. Result: "the webpage includes a comprehensive dropdown list of countries. Bangladesh is explicitly listed among the eligible countries of origin." Eligible status options confirmed: "Graduates" and "Doctoral candidates/PhD students". Programme is open to international Muslim students/doctoral candidates enrolled (or at least accepted) at a German higher education institution; B2 German required (exceptions possible). A Bangladeshi Muslim graduate admitted to a German Master's therefore qualifies.
