## Our Scholarship S1 Program
The KAAD is an institution that awards scholarships to promising students and scholars from all over the world, especially from partner regions with developmental challenges, for a stay mostly in Germany. The scholarships – financed by German Catholic church taxes, diocesan funds, German government ministries, and numerous donors – are awarded at master and doctoral level, as well as for research stays by senior academics. They cover study and living costs comparable to those of other exchange organizations.

## Eligibility
We welcome applications from all disciplines, the natural sciences, life sciences, law, humanities etc. However, we do not offer grants to students of theology and theologians. The majority of our grant holders is Catholic, but each year we select a few candidates of no religious or other religious affiliation. With few exceptions, we also concentrate our grants on people coming from the following, currently six countries of major focus in our Asia program:
   1. Bangladesh
   2. Indonesia
   3. Myanmar
   4. Philippines
   5. Vietnam
   6. People's Republic of China

Please be aware, that we expect scholarship holders to return to their home countries after their stay in Germany. Otherwise the scholarship is turned into a loan.

## Study and Research Grants
1. Study Grants are provided as one-year scholarships, renewable yearly for a maximum of 3 years. They are meant to enable students to pursue a master or doctoral degree at a German university.
2. Research grants are provided for 2-6 months to senior faculty members or for post-graduate research.
Also short-term research grants connected to your M.A./Ph.D. program in your home country ("sandwich-model").

## S1: Applying from Your Home Country
The S1 scholarship program addresses all applications that reach the KAAD from abroad. The other program – S2 – is directed towards candidates studying in Germany already. The scholarship program S1 starts with filling in the application form on our website: https://www.kaad-application.de. After a positive initial evaluation, we will forward your application to our local partners in our cooperating countries, who will get in touch with you.

## Requirements
For a master degree program you need:
  a) a Bachelor degree (normally resulting from a four-year-program) or its equivalent from an institution of recognised standing;
  b) your overall-grade should be above average – we foster excellency;
  c) an acceptance letter from the university with which you would like to pursue your study.

For German-taught programs: proficient in German equivalent to level B2 of the Goethe Institute at the time of application. Grants can include language and culture training of four to six months.
For English-based programs: you will gain basic acquaintance with German prior to starting your studies. The scholarship includes 4 to 6 months of language and culture training in Bonn at the beginning of your stay.

Personal qualifications / documents:
1. Motivation letter (crucial for personal-qualifications decision).
2. The KAAD requests two recommendation letters from your academic supervisors or other academics in your field.
3. A reference from the country's Church, preferably from the local bishop or a cleric, reporting on the applicant's personal and religious convictions and active engagement in the Church and social life (in German or English translation). Candidates from other religions should be recommended by Catholic partners who ensure the candidate's commitment to interreligious dialogue.

## Application Deadlines
Partner committees around the world and Catholic chaplaincies of German universities send the applications to the KAAD in Bonn twice a year. The applications have to be received in Bonn by January 15 and June 30. The selection committee of the KAAD then decides twice a year (in March and September) about successful candidates. All candidates are contacted personally by email within about three months after the application deadline about the outcome of the selection process.

Documents needed for your application are listed at the end of the application form.
