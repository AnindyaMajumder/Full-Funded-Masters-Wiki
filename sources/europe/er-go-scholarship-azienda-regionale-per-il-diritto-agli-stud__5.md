## 2. Who can apply

"All students enrolled for the A.Y. 2025/2026 at the Universities, Higher Institutes for Arts and Music and all Higher-level Institutions in Emilia Romagna, regardless of citizenship or country of residence, as follows:
> Degree courses
> Second cycle Degree courses [= master's]
> Single cycle degree courses
> Specialisation Schools ...
> First, second cycle Diplomas ... (AFAM).
> Ph.D. Courses ..."

## Requirements (three)

- Educational achievement: "Students enrolled in the first year, unlike for subsequent years, can get access to scholarships only by their economic conditions. All students, in the first and subsequent years, must reach a certain level of achievement over the academic year, in order to maintain the Scholarship and not to pay back the installments received."
- Economic: ISEE/ISPE below thresholds (A.Y. 2025/26: ISEE EUR 25,000.00; ISPE EUR 50,000.00).
- Enrolment by the bando deadlines.

## 4.1 Documentation required to international students from Non-EU countries

"International students, coming from Non-EU countries, must provide the complete documentation about economic and financial conditions, issued by the competent authorities for the territory, in which the incomes were produced and where the assets are owned. The documents must be translated into Italian and legalized by the Italian diplomatic authorities competent for that territory or, where applicable, apostilled in accordance with the current regulations.

In case of countries where there are particular difficulties in issuing the certification attested by the local Italian Embassy, the documentation can be replaced by a certificate in Italian issued by the Embassy or by a Consulate in Italy of the country of origin, legalized by the Prefecture office competent for the territory."

Documentation must attest: (a) composition of family unit; (b)(c) working activity of each member in 2024; (d) income earned in 2024; (e) buildings owned on 31 Dec 2024; (f) residual mortgage at 31 Dec 2024; (g) assets owned at 31 Dec 2024; (h) any disability document. Values in local currency, converted to Euro at Bank of Italy 2024 average exchange rate.

"International students from Non-EU countries, which are 'particularly poor', (Table A, General Rules), must provide a certification of the Italian Representation in the country of origin, attesting that the student does not belong to a family, known as to be of high income and high social level."

## 4. How to apply (international student)

"You can fill in the benefits application form exclusively on line on the website www.er-go.it, INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS - HOW TO APPLY section, or at the CAF (Tax Assistance Centres) recognised by ER.GO."
Students without residency in Italy access via University credentials. SPID/CIE/CNS for those resident in Italy.
"ATTENTION! ER.GO automatically acquires the online application for total exemption from tuition fees for all the students who apply for scholarship."
"To apply for benefits there is no need to enroll in advance at University ... Students, to be eligible for benefits, must in any case enroll at University by the deadlines."

"These scholarship amounts also include any amount converted into catering service."

## 7.2 Increases

"A. In the case of ISEE up to € 12,500,00, the value of the scholarship is increased by 15%.
B. In the case of female students enrolled in S.T.E.M University courses ... the value of the scholarship is increased by 20%.
The possible increases are not cumulative with each other."

## Connected benefits (from Focus on Scholarship + Borsa di studio pages)

"If you are eligible for the scholarship, you are entitled to the total tuition fee exemption and the refund of the regional tax. You can also ask to convert part of your scholarship into catering vouchers."
