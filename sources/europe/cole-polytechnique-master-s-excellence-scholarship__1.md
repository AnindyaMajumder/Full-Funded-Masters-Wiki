# Scholarships and Financial Aid

##  Scholarships
Be aware that students who apply for the first and second rounds are prioritized for these financial aids.

[Excellence Scholarships sponsored by Ecole Polytechnique Foundation]
Ecole Polytechnique Foundation scholarships are based on several criteria (academic excellence, and diversity). The selection is the result of an overall evaluation of the candidate's application after the selection interview. Scholarships are deducted from tuition fees.
  * Eligibility: All candidates admitted to one of our master program.
  * Process: All admitted candidates automatically considered for Ecole Polytechnique Foundation scholarships. No additional document is required.
  * Deadline: Results from this scholarship are announced by email to all laureates after their admissions and before the confirmation deadline.
  * Amount: 8,000 EUR per year upon successfully validating first year of Master.

[Excellence and Solidarity: CMA CGM Excellence fund for Education]
As part of the CMA CGM Excellence Fund for Education, the CMA CGM Group finances more than 10 Ecole Polytechnique students each year, across all programs (Bachelor of Science, Polytechnic Engineering Cycle, Master of Science & Technology).
  * Eligibility: Half of the scholarships are reserved for students of Lebanese nationality, while the other half are awarded to students of all nationalities (international or French). The scholarships take social criteria into account, and applicants must inform Ecole Polytechnique of all financial resources...
  * Process: The admissions committee will make a preliminary selection based on criteria of excellence. If you are eligible for this scholarship, you will receive detailed instructions on how to apply, including how to provide proof of financial resources.
  * Deadline: The results will be communicated after the admission results have been announced and before the deadline for payment of the pre-registration application fee.
  * Amount: 7,500 EUR per year upon successfully validating first year of Master.

[Industrial Partnership Scholarships]
Each MSc&T Specialization may have Industrial partners who would sponsor scholarships to eligible students.
  * Eligibility: All candidates admitted to one of our master program. Scholarships are awarded based on academic excellence and diversity.
  * Process: All admitted candidates automatically considered. No additional document is required.
  * Deadline: Results from this scholarship are announced by email to all laureates after their admissions and before the confirmation deadline.
  * Amount: From 4,000 EUR to 15,000 EUR per year upon successfully validating first year of Master.

##  Need based Financial aid
[Other Financial Aid Opportunities]
Students are encouraged to find other types of financial aid in their own country or in France by themselves.
A complete list of scholarships and grants is available on CampusBourses listing.
**CROUS scholarships are not compatible with this degree**.
**the APL (Aide personnalisée au logement)** is a government housing benefit that helps french and european students with limited income pay part of their rent in France.

##  Loans
Most French banks require a guarantor with a French tax identification number. French or European students who meet residency requirements in France may be eligible for a state-guaranteed loan. Through partnerships with Ecole Polytechnique, enrolled students may also apply for a loan without a guarantor. These loans are subject to a maximum amount (which does not cover the full tuition fees) and require approval from the partner bank.

##  Lafayette Scholarship
Lafayette Fellowship a prestigious scholarship program launched by the Embassy of France in the United States and Villa Albertine.
Selected fellows receive: Full coverage of tuition fees; A monthly living allowance (€1,500); Round-trip airfare between the U.S. and France; Visa fee waiver and support.
**Eligibility:** U.S. citizens under 27 who have obtained a Bachelor's degree between 2023 and 2026 and are admitted to a partner French institution, including Ecole Polytechnique.

##  Brazil Scholarship
The Trajetórias-Polytechnique Funding Program. **Eligibility**: Brazilian citizens admitted to one of the eligible Master of Science and Technology (MSc&T) at Ecole Polytechnique.

##  Singapore Scholarship
Full coverage of tuition fees; Full coverage of round-trip airfare; 1,500 monthly stipend; ... Obligation to serve 4 to 6 years in the civil Singaporean Service immediately after graduation.

##  Colfuturo Scholarship
Colombian students offered a place for full-time graduate studies at Ecole Polytechnique and selected as beneficiaries of the Loan-Scholarship Program of COLFUTURO.
