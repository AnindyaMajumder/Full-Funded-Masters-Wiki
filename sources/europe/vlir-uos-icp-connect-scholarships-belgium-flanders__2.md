# ICP Connect Scholarships 2026-2027
We are excited to announce the launch of our ICP Connect scholarships call for students starting in September 2026. We are offering full scholarships for 20 programmes.

Duration: 12/24/36 months
Budget: Full Scholarship
Call Submission Deadline: 28 February 2026
Project Start Date: 01 September 2026

**The start and end dates of the application procedure can vary depending on the specific programme. Please check carefully.**

## ICP Connect Study Scholarships
ICP programmes equip students to become leaders and change agents in their fields. Students from 29 eligible countries in Africa, Asia, and Latin America are invited to apply for these scholarships (with Mali recently added to the list).

## FAQ — B. Admission & Application Requirements
1. English Language Requirement — All ICP Connect programmes are taught in English and therefore require a high level of English proficiency. Applicants must meet the specific English language requirements set by the organising institution. You can check the website of the programme you are interested in, or contact them, to find out about specific language requirements. ARES, another Belgian scholarship provider, offers French-language programmes.
3. Waiving English Test Requirement — If you studied in an English-speaking institution, some programmes may waive this requirement.
4. Dutch Language Requirement — No, you do not need to speak Dutch. All ICP Connect programmes are taught in English.
5. Applying Without Final Diploma — Many programmes may accept temporary certifications or official transcripts until the final certificate is available.
6. Reference Letter Requirements — No, you do not need separate reference letters. Reference letters are part of the admission process of the organising institution.
7. Document Legalisation — Applicants from certain countries may need to legalise documents through Belgian embassies or consulates for their application.

## FAQ — C. Financial Matters
1. Scholarship Coverage — The monthly scholarship allowance covers living expenses and tuition fees. Depending on the institution you pay for your own insurance, tuition fee, and/or accommodation out of the scholarship allowance OR the institution covers these costs directly and subtracts them from your monthly allowance.
2. Funds for Extra Activities — There are no additional funds provided by VLIRUOS for student activities like Global South immersions, but there might be programme-specific funds.
3. Application Fees — ICP Connect scholarship programmes do NOT require an application fee. Applicants to these programmes can submit their application without paying any fee.
4. Working While Studying — Generally, as a student, you can work a few hours per week to supplement your budget. Employment is not permitted during times when you are required to attend classes or other study-related activities.

## FAQ — D. Selection Process
1. How Applications Are Screened — Yes. The organising higher education institution reviews and ranks all eligible applications first. VLIRUOS then conducts a separate screening of the selection list it receives from the institution.
2. Receiving Your Application Result — VLIRUOS contacts selected candidates and those placed on the waiting list by email. Applicants who are not selected for a scholarship or the waiting list will be notified by the programme they applied to.
