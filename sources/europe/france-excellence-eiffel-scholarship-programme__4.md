## METICULOUS SELECTION OF EIFFEL PROGRAMME RECIPIENTS

### Graduate level (Master's)
Scholarship duration: Between 12 to 36 months based on the type of degree and the year of registration: master's degree or engineering diploma (see the regulations for the Eiffel scholarship)

Funding and support:
• Payment of the monthly scholarship (see regulations)
• Where applicable, no visa fees
• Coverage provided for:
  - international travel to France/return to home country
  - health insurance
  - housing allowance
• Support throughout the duration of your stay

Note: Tuition fees are not covered by the Eiffel programme. Students who are recipients of a French government scholarship are exempt from paying.

## Timeline
• Online call for applications: end of September
• Deadline for institutions to send applications to Campus France: 1st week of January
• Publication of results: March

## Selection criteria
• Candidate's track record of excellence.
• The institution's reputation abroad and its international student policy.
• Policy of cooperation and partnership with the French Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
• Guaranteeing excellence of the programme thanks to a selection commission composed of seven panels of experts (one per field of study).

## Eligibility criteria
The programme is reserved for international students. Candidates with dual nationality, one of which is French, are not eligible. Only programmes that confer a nationally accredited degree are allowed. French programmes located abroad will not be accepted. Programmes part of an apprenticeship contract are ineligible (contrat d'apprentissage, contrat de professionnalisation).

### Applications
Host French institutions commit to register awardees in the programme for which they were selected.

### Graduate level (Master's)
• Candidates currently studying in France are ineligible.
• Graduate level programmes, including engineering diplomas, are eligible.

### Post-graduate level (PhD)
• Students currently studying abroad are a priority over those who currently reside in France.
• The award of a 36-month scholarship is reserved for first-year PhD students registering for the first time in France.
Note: Students who are currently recipients of a French government scholarship are not eligible.
