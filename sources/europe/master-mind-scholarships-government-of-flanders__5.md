# Host institutions and deadlines
Below you will find a list of all Flemish higher education institutions that participate in the Master Mind scholarship.
## Important information
Carefully check the **scholarship application deadline** for the higher education institution you wish to study at. The deadlines are updated as soon as we receive confirmation from the higher education institution when the call for the scholarship is opened.
**If your intended Flemish higher education institution is not listed, you cannot apply for this scholarship.**
