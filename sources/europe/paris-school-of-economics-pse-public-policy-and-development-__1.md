## Your application
Applications for the Master Public Policy and Development are open to holders of a Bachelor’s degree in economics, mathematics or social sciences.
Applications can **only** be made on the [PSE Application platform](https://www.pse-application.eu/).
Applications must be submitted in English or French. An official transcript is required for all other languages.
- See the list of required documents: https://www.parisschoolofeconomics.eu/app/uploads/2024/04/required-documents-for-master-scaled.jpeg

Some candidates may be admitted directly to M2. During their previous studies, they must have acquired knowledge equivalent to that taught in M1 PPD (microeconomics, macroeconomics, econometrics, public policy evaluation). This request must be specified in the application form.

## Selection process
All applications are evaluated by our admission jury. The selection is made on the basis of your application file only – we will not organize interviews. The admission jury of PPD is independent from the jurys of the other master programs in PSE. Students can apply for more than one program, and some students may be admitted in more than one program.
Admission to the PPD program is conditional on obtaining the diploma that prepares you for a PSE master program. For us to be able to consider your application, it is important that you provide all the required documents and that you meet all admission requirements.

## Tuition fees
University fees are annual and set by the Ministry of Higher Education and Research in France.
Since the start of the 2019 academic year, the French government has announced an increase in registration fees up to €3,879 for Master’s students from outside the European Union who are entering a new cycle of studies. However, for the 2024-2025 academic year, our partner institutions have granted all students, regardless of their country of origin, to pay the same amount of €250 per year, plus around €100 in student life and campus contributions (CVEC), which students must pay to the CROUS.
Our partner institutions are willing to do the same again in 2025-26. We will update the website as soon as we get a confirmation (usually around March-April 2025).
