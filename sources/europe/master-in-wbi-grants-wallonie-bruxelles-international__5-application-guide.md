MASTER IN – WBI GRANTS 2026-2027
APPLICATION GUIDE
Wallonia-Brussels International (WBI) offers financial support to pursue a master’s degree or a specialised
master’s program of 60 or 120 credits for international students holding at least a bachelor’s or master’s
degree obtained from a foreign awarding institution recognized by the competent authorities of the
candidate’s country in the field of higher education. The master’s programme must be undertaken in
Wallonia or Brussels within a higher education institution recognized by the Wallonia-Brussels Federation.
For a 60-credit master’s programme, the grant is for a maximum duration of one year. For a 120-credit
master’s programme, the grant is awarded for a maximum duration of two years. Renewal of the grant for
the second year is subject to the validation by the higher education institution of a sufficient number of
credits allowing continuation of the programme.
In its current configuration, the programme excludes 180-credit master’s degrees as well as joint-degree
master’s programmes with foreign higher education institution.
Finally, as laureates, recipients of the MASTER IN grant will pay the same tuition fees as Belgian and
European students.

How to Submit Your Application
STEP 1 Verify whether the higher education institution of the Wallonia-Brussels Federation of your choice
participates in the MASTER IN – WBI grant programme. The grant may only be applied to a defined selection
of study programmes as determined by certain institutions. Higher education institutions reserve the right
not to process or respond to applications that are unrelated to the MASTER IN 2026–2027 call.
STEP 2 Submit an admission request to the targeted higher education institution, according to the
procedures specific to that institution. Your admission request for a master’s program at a university will
receive a reference number to be included on the form. In other cases (university colleges, schools of arts),
no number must be indicated.
STEP 3 Send your application form and the four annexes to the targeted institution. If it is a university,
indicate the reference number of your admission file.
STEP 4 The host institution preselects eligible applications.
STEP 5 Its ranking is transmitted to Wallonia-Brussels International, which makes the final selection based
on defined criteria, no later than April 20, 2026.

Documents to attach to the MASTER IN – WBI Grant Application Form
1. Copy of the international passport or national identity card;
2. A one-page curriculum vitae (including, where applicable, academic background, previous
scholarships, possible professional experiences related to the chosen master’s program, etc.);
3. A motivation letter of up to 2,500 words written in French or English, depending on the language
of the chosen master’s program, without the assistance of artificial intelligence (AI);
4. Copy of the degree(s) obtained and transcripts.
Note: If written in a language other than French or English, they must be accompanied by a certified
translation.

Eligibility Conditions for the MASTER IN – WBI Grant
-

-

The candidate must hold a bachelor’s or master’s degree awarded by an institution whose studies
or training are recognised by the competent authorities of the candidate’s country of origin in
higher education.
The last bachelor’s or master’s degree obtained in the country of origin must have been awarded
no later than five years before the start of the requested programme.
Moreover, the MASTER IN programme does not allow the funding of a full year (60 ECTS) within
the framework of a bridging pathway.
The candidate must hold a bachelor’s or master’s degree sufficient to access the targeted master’s
programme and meet its admission requirements. In addition, the higher education institution
remains free to add further conditions.
The candidate must be admitted to the master’s programme to apply for financial support and must
be enrolled in the programme to benefit from it.
Any candidate meeting the necessary and sufficient conditions for submitting an admission file to
the targeted higher education institution may apply.
Applications from candidates already engaged in higher education studies in Belgium will be
excluded. The aim is to support new mobility, not extensions of Erasmus+ stays or programmes
previously undertaken as free students in French-speaking Belgian institutions.
The candidate must indicate, in the application form, the reference number of the admission
file provided by the university. In the case of an application to a university college or a school
of arts, no reference number is required.
Note: This file number does not mean that admission is finalised.

Candidates who have studied in the following countries/regions are considered a priority by WBI
and higher education institutions: Brazil, Canada, Chile, Colombia, South Korea, Japan, Lebanon,
Morocco, Mexico, United Kingdom, Switzerland, Taiwan, Tunisia, USA.
All fields of study are considered in the selection process unless the institution decides to restrict
the programmes concerned by this call.
To avoid overlap with other international mobility schemes, candidates who have already
submitted an application within an ongoing academic programme or who have previously
benefited from a development cooperation scholarship from ARES are excluded
(https://www.ares-ac.be/en/international-training-scholarships-2026-2027).
The grant application form and required documents must be sent to the targeted higher education
institution no later than March 31, 2026, at midnight (GMT – Europe/Brussels) for the upcoming
academic year (2026–2027).
Incomplete applications will not be submitted to the WBI jury responsible for candidate selection.

Selection procedure
The selection process takes place in two stages:
First, the selection is carried out by the higher education institution based on its own criteria. Its ranking is
transmitted to Wallonia-Brussels International, which makes the final selection based on a predefined set
of criteria. The pre-established ranking of five applications per participating institution must reach WalloniaBrussels International no later than April 20, 2026.
Second, results are communicated by Wallonia-Brussels International to the targeted higher education
institution no later than May 15, 2026.
The candidate is informed of the outcome of their application by the international relations office or its
equivalent of the higher education institution. The laureate must contact the programme manager of
MASTER IN, Jeff NCUNGANYI (j.ncunganyi@wbi.be), on May 29, 2026.
All study stays in universities, university colleges, or schools of arts must begin at the start of the academic
year (September 2026). No deferral will be possible for any reason. If the student is not awarded the grant
and therefore decides not to pursue their admission request at the targeted institution, the application fees
incurred will not be reimbursed.

Financial arrangements
The grant will be disbursed according to the applicable rules and the following terms:
-

-

The grant amount of €10,020 is divided into 12 monthly payments deposited into a bank account
denominated in euros. This support does not cover the full costs of one academic year; therefore,
the laureate must supplement these funds with personal resources. Moreover, this funding is
incompatible with other support provided by WBI or ARES.
This amount meets the requirement of sufficient means of subsistence for a long-stay study visa
(Visa D).
The successful candidate shall benefit from an exemption from the administrative fee payable to
the Immigration Office.
Visa fees or other expenses must be advanced by the candidate. They will be indirectly reimbursed
through the monthly payments, with no advance disbursement before the start of the academic
year. Furthermore, Wallonia-Brussels International does not intervene in financial procedures
related to obtaining the visa.

Additionally, Wallonia-Brussels International covers health insurance (mutual subrogation), repatriation,
and personal liability insurance for the grant holder. Travel insurance remains the responsibility of the
candidate.
