# Applications — Université Côte d'Azur International MSc programs

Source: https://univ-cotedazur.eu/msc-programs/application-registration
Accessed: 2026-06-18

## Applying to our International MSc programs
**Applications** to our International MSc programs usually run from **January to June**. Students can apply online through our application portal, e-candidat (http://ecandidat-uca1.univ-cotedazur.fr). Note that guidelines in English are available on the first page of the application portal. Also you do not need to complete your application all at once, you can start and save for later.

## FAQs about applications

When can I expect to hear about my application?
  * Applications are reviewed from January until June.
  * You should receive a reply within two months after submitting your application

I am an international student, do I have to apply through Campus France?
  * International students from outside the EEU should apply through Campus France if their country is listed on the Campus France website.
  * However, applying on the university's platform (e-candidat) is also compulsory.
