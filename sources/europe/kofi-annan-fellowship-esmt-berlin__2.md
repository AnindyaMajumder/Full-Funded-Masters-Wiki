# ESMT Berlin — Full-time MBA Awards and Scholarships (crawled 2026-06-18)

Source: https://esmt.berlin/mba-programs/full-time-mba/tuition-financing/scholarships

NOTE (verifier): The Kofi Annan Fellowship was historically an ESMT MBA/MIM award. This
current official Full-time MBA scholarships page lists every MBA award/scholarship in
detail. There is NO "Kofi Annan Fellowship" among them. The MBA scholarships are
tuition-reduction awards only ("Scholarships and awards can be combined and apply solely to
tuition fees.").

Scholarships present (none named "Kofi Annan"):
- Awards: Early application; Test score excellence award; German Language.
- Diversity scholarships: Gender pay gap (€8,000 x10); Rainbow (€5,000 x2); Regional
  (Africa/ME up to €25,000; LatAm up to €10,000; Asia up to €8,000; Europe up to €6,000;
  North America up to €2,500).
- Impact scholarships: Dean's Excellence (€15,000 x2); Vali Berlin entrepreneurship
  (€5,000 x4); Impact scholarship (€5,000 x2).
- Panzer scholarship (€20,000 x1; African citizens/residents <=30 who founded a
  business/social enterprise).
- BMW Group Change Maker Fellowship (€45,000 x2; specific country list, NOT incl.
  Bangladesh).
- Network scholarships: e-Fellows (€10,000); Bundeswehr and former military (€15,000).

Verbatim framing: "Scholarships and awards make your Full-time MBA more affordable by
lowering the tuition you need to pay." and "Scholarships and awards can be combined and
apply solely to tuition fees." — i.e. tuition reduction only, no living stipend.
