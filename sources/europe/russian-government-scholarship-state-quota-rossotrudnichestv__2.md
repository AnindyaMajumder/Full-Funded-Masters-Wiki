# 124 Bangladeshi students get Russian govt scholarships
26 September 2023

The Russian government today announced 124 scholarships for Bangladeshi students on bachelor, masters and PhD courses for the academic year 2024-2025.

Counsellor of the Embassy of the Russian Federation Pavel Dvoychenkov announced this in a press conference ... at the Russian House in Dhaka.

The counsellor said that the application for the scholarship can be done online at https://education-in-russia.com from September 1, 2023 till October 31, 2023. However, before applying all educational certificates and mark sheets/transcripts, original and photocopy, must be attested by the Bangladesh Ministry of Foreign Affairs and passport must be valid for more than 18 months from September 2024.

After completing the online application, its copy along with attested copies of all educational certificates and transcripts, passport copy, medical certificate (Hepatitis A.B.C, Tuberculosis, and HIV absence report), consent letter of the candidate to personal info processing and one passport size color photograph should be submitted to the Education Section of Russian House in Dhaka.

Application submission time at Russian House in Dhaka is 10:00am to 5:00pm from Sunday to Thursday.

## Additional confirmation (WebSearch, accessed 2026-06-20)
- For the 2026-2027 academic year, the Russian government has allocated 200 scholarships for Bangladeshi students (up from 124), at 400+ universities. Application via education-in-russia.com.
- Selection of candidates for the scholarships is carried out by the Russian House in Dhaka alongside the Russian Embassy in Bangladesh and representatives of leading Russian universities.
- Bangladeshi news (The Financial Express / Daily Star, 2025): "Students under this scholarship are exempt from tuition fees, but they must cover their living expenses, estimated at Tk 1-2 lakh per year." (Note: a monthly state stipend IS paid per official university quota pages, but it is small and may not fully cover living costs.)
- Applicants must ensure academic certificates/transcripts are authenticated by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Bangladesh; passport valid at least 18 months.
- Government stipend amount ranges roughly RUB 1,500-3,000/month depending on university/level (third-party/university figures); not a single official fixed figure published centrally.
