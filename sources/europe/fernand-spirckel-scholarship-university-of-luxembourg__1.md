#  Fernand Spirckel Scholarship

####  Who is eligible?
Les candidats à un master en : • Master en Sciences de l’ingénieur (MEEE) • Master in Civil Engineering Sciences (MCES) • Master in Sustainable Product Creation (MSPC)

####  When?
Apply for a Master’s degree before 31 May 2026

####  About
Scholarship based on excellent academic performance

  * This scholarship is made possible through the generosity of Ms Anne‑Marie Spirckel‑Albig. It is established in honour of her late husband, Fernand Spirckel, a Luxembourgish engineer who spent the majority of his professional life abroad and was committed to supporting future generations in the engineering profession.
  * Master’s students
  *     * [Master en Sciences de l’ingénieur – Efficacité énergétique et économique](https://www.uni.lu/fstm-en/study-programs/master-en-sciences-de-l-ingenieur-efficacite-energetique-et-economique/)
    * [Master in Civil Engineering Sciences – Megastructure Engineering with Sustainable Resources](https://www.uni.lu/fstm-en/study-programs/master-in-civil-engineering-sciences-megastructure-engineering-with-sustainable-resources/)
    * [Master in Sustainable Product Creation](https://www.uni.lu/fstm-en/study-programs/master-in-sustainable-product-creation/)
  * All countries are eligible.
  *     * Only available for Master’s programmes that run their full two-year programme in Luxembourg
    * Excellent academic performance during undergraduate studies
    * Full-time students only
    * Applicants must not be beneficiaries of Luxembourg’s State Financial Aid for Higher Education (formerly CED)
    * [Moving to Luxembourg to study?](https://www.uni.lu/life-en/moving-to-luxembourg/to-study/)Information about residence permit, health insurance, etc.
  * Maximum 2 students
  * 2 years (standard duration of the Master’s degree)
  * **€11 875** per student, per academic year, for up to two years.
  * Student housing is not by default guaranteed for scholarships recipients.
Students are required to apply for accommodation in a timely manner and carefully follow the provided instructions and deadlines in the accommodation section.
    * [Accommodation Uni.lu](https://www.uni.lu/life-en/accommodation/)
  * Application deadline for the Fernand Spirckel Scholarship: 31 May 2026

**1. Apply for the Master’s programme:**
First, we invite you to consult the admission requirements specified on the webpage of the Master programme you are interested in to ensure that your application is complete. (to be completed before 31 May)
You should then submit your Master’s application and all the required documents via our online admissions portal.
    * [Applying for admission](https://www.uni.lu/en/admissions/bachelor-master/)

**2. Evaluation of your Master’s application:**
Once your application is complete, it will be forwarded to the programme director of the selected Master’s programme.
If the Programme Director considers that your application meets the required criteria of excellence, you will receive a notification inviting you to apply for the excellence scholarship separately. The link to submit your scholarship application will be included in this notification.

**3. Scholarship application:**
For your online scholarship application you will need to:
    * Complete the dedicated online form;
    * Attach a personal statement outlining your reasons for applying and presenting your strengths
Once you have submitted your scholarship application, it will be evaluated.
**We encourage you to follow these steps carefully to ensure that your application is managed efficiently.**

  * **Selection criteria:**
    * Quality of academic performance
    * Originality of your personal statement and strengths of your candidacy.
The selection process involves an internal evaluation procedure.
Finalists should be notified by **June 2026** and they will need to confirm acceptance of the scholarship within three working days.
All applicants will receive a final response.
Upon arrival at the University of Luxembourg in September 2026, scholarship recipients will sign a Scholarship Agreement, including a sworn declaration accepting the terms and conditions of the Ferdinand Spirckel Scholarship.
