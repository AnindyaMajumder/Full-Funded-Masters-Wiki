## Note on tuition / regional-tax exemption
The DiSCo English call page lists benefits as: Scholarships (cash), Accommodation,
"Easy access to the canteen ... with meals at reduced prices", and international-mobility grants.
Under the Italian national Right-to-Study (Diritto allo Studio / DSU) framework, scholarship
winners and 'idonei' are statutorily exempt from the regional study tax and university tuition
contribution — this is the same companion benefit documented for EDISU Piemonte and DSU Toscana.
DiSCo's own public pages do not restate the tuition-exemption text in English, so it is flagged
in mustKnow rather than asserted from a DiSCo verbatim quote.
