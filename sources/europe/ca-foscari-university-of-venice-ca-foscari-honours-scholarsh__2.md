# Non-EU students Tuition fees for BA and MA degrees
Source: https://www.unive.it/pag/44400/
Accessed: 2026-06-20
NOTE: This page describes non-EU tuition fees (a.y. 2022/2023 example). It shows that Ca' Foscari charges non-EU students a flat tax; students from Low Human Development Countries (annually approved by MUR) pay a reduced flat fee of 700 EUR. The Honours Scholarship (10,000 EUR/year) is a cash grant, not a tuition waiver.

If you are a student with non-EU citizenship, who enrolled to a bachelor’s or master’s degree are subject to the payment of a flat tax.

#### OECD Countries
If you are a student with citizenship to one of the following OECD countries (excluding EU countries): Australia, Canada, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, South Korea, Japan, Iceland, Israel, Mexico, New Zealand, United Kingdom, United States and Turkey, you are subject to a flat tax of:
  * 1,900 EUR Bachelor's degree course;
  * 2,100 EUR Master's Degree course.

#### Students from countries with low human development identified annually by MUR
If you are a student enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree course, with citizenship in one of the Low Human Development Countries (list annually approved by the Ministry), you are subject to a flat fee of 700 EUR for the academic year.

#### Students not belonging to the first two categories
If you are a student enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree programme with citizenship to a non-EU country not belonging to the first two categories, you are subject to a flat tax of 1,200 EUR for the academic year.
