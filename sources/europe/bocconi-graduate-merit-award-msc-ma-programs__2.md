#  Admissions

#### **International Applicants**

Selection rounds and timeline

For the 2026/27 academic year, **four selection rounds** are available for international candidates.

| Round of selection  | Application Period  | Results release date and beginning of the enrollment period*  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I round  | 9 October - 5 November 2025  | 15 December 2025  |
| II round  | 6 November 2025 - 22 January 2026   | 26 February 2026  |
| III round  | 23 January - 9 March 2026  | 9 April 2026  |
| IV round  | 10 March - 29 April 2026  | 9 June 2026  |

Each round closes at 11:59 p.m. (Italian time) on the date indicated.

Slots for the most sought-after programs and the majority of scholarships are more likely to be allocated in the earlier rounds.

You cannot apply to two rounds at the same time. ... A new application within the same academic year is allowed only if you were not admitted in the previous round. Candidates who have received an offer of admission are not eligible to reapply in the same academic year.

Elements of selection

In the four selection rounds, international candidates are evaluated through a comprehensive process that considers all aspects of their profile, with a specific focus on **three compulsory evaluation elements**. **Applications will not be evaluated if any of these three elements are missing**:
  1. **Results of the GMAT/GRE test or the online Bocconi test** ... Please note that the PPA and GLOBE programs do not require a selection test.
  2. **Grade point average of exams passed in the Undergraduate program** The undergraduate GPA and transcripts indicate the applicant's previous academic performance. No particular scores are required to participate in the selections.
  3. **Evaluation of the Dossier and Motivation** ... The Dossier/Motivation supporting the candidate's application must be **in English.**

You can also record a **Video presentation** (optional) in English as an additional element to your application.

Requirements: valid bachelor qualification

Applicants can enter the selection process if they acquire/have acquired a **valid and recognized 1st cycle qualification (Undergraduate level)**.
  * The qualification must be acquired by and no later than **October 2026** or the first graduation session available after the summer break...

Requirements: English language

For selection purposes, English certifications are not required, BUT, in case of admission, a valid English language requirement MUST be provided by the enrollment deadline... We invite candidates to check the English language requirements needed in case of admission.

How to apply

To participate in the selection, it is necessary to:
  * Submit an online application through the **Apply2B portal**
  * Pay the **application fee** of €100

(Note: The Bocconi Graduate Merit Award requires NO separate application — it is awarded automatically based on the admission application above; results communicated together with admission results.)
