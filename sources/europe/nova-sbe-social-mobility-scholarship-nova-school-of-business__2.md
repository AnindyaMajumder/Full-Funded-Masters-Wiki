# Nova SBE — Master's Admissions deadlines (2026/27 cycle), anchors scholarship timeline

Source: https://www.novasbe.unl.pt/en/programs/apply/masters/admission
Accessed: 2026-06-20
Method: WebFetch (crawl4ai unavailable on this JS-heavy site).

The Social Mobility Scholarship is applied for AT THE TIME of the master's application,
so the scholarship deadline = the master's program application deadline.

## 2026/27 (Fall 2026) cycle — verbatim dates
Application period: 13/11/2025 through 30/04/2026 (regular programs).
Earlier deadline 23/03/2026 for: IMF, IMM, CEMS MIM, Double Degrees and Workstreams.

Five sequential rounds (each closes 5.00 pm Lisbon time):
- Round 1: 13/11/2025 – 27/11/2025 (decision within 4 weeks)
- Round 2: 28/11/2025 – 06/01/2026 (decision within 8 weeks)
- Round 3: 06/01/2026 – 12/02/2026 (decision within 8 weeks)
- Round 4: 12/02/2026 – 23/03/2026 (decision within 8 weeks)
- Round 5: 23/03/2026 – 30/04/2026 (decision within 8 weeks; regular Master's only)

Note: As of 2026-06, the 2026/27 admission cycle's last round (30/04/2026) has already
closed; these are the most recent known/completed cycle dates. A Spring intake also exists.
Rolling scholarship budget means early rounds are strongly advised.
