### Excellence Scholarships of the University of St.Gallen for Bachelor students
Excellence scholarships are awarded to students enrolled in a Bachelor's degree at the University of St.Gallen on the basis of merit. The scholarship amount corresponds to the current tuition fees at the Bachelor level for a total of 6 semesters.

Currently, the University of St.Gallen, the "St.Galler Stiftung für Internationale Studien" (SSIS) and the "Monique Dornonville de la Cour - Stiftung" award scholarships for excellence at the Bachelor level in cooperation with the Advice Center for Study Funding.

Enrolled students of the University of St.Gallen with an outstanding academic performance in the HSG Assessment Year are identified by the Advice Center for Study Funding and invited to apply for an Excellence Scholarship. An application without an invitation from the University of St.Gallen is not possible.

### Excellence Scholarships of the University of St.Gallen for Master students
A further excellence scholarship is the HSG Best Talents Programme (HSG Best Talents | unisg.ch). This scholarship programme is for outstanding and engaged bachelor's graduates (EU/EFTA nationals or holders of the Swiss C permit) who wish to pursue a master's at HSG. Those who are accepted into the programme benefit from financial support as well as a personal development programme alongside their studies. ...

Other Excellence Scholarships may be offered by Master's programmes. Please reach out to your respective Master's programme representative.

### Loan and Scholarship Fund (need + merit, all levels incl. Master)
This fund supports enrolled Assessment Year students and students at Bachelor's and Master's levels with scholarships and loans at favourable rates of interest. ... payments ... are granted from the second regular degree course semester after initial enrolment or re-enrolment. Application forms ... on Compass. Deadlines: Autumn semester 1 October; Spring semester 1 March.
