# Tuition & Financial Support

**Tuition** The Master in Economic Research has two versions:
- No tuition fee in the '0311TA050010' version
- EUR 8.000 per academic year in the '0311TA050011' version

**CERGE-EI Foundation Scholarships for Master in Economic Research**

CERGE-EI Foundation awards a number of competitive scholarships for students applying for the CERGE-EI Master in Economic Research (0311A050011) study program. The scholarships cover the tuition fees of the program. CERGE-EI Foundation selects the scholarship recipients from among the admitted applicants to the program upon the recommendation of the CERGE-EI Director. Each year five full and five partial scholarships are awarded. A full scholarship covers the entire tuition fees and a partial scholarship covers 50% of the tuition fees. A scholarship covers both years of the program, either fully or partially, with the continuation in the second year conditional on successful academic performance in the first year. Applicants for the Master in Economic Research (0311A050011) study program need to fill out the following [form](https://forms.gle/jCc6M6qbKmtHCAwz5) to be considered for the scholarship. The deadline for the scholarship application is the same as the deadline for the study program application. Applicants are notified about admission to the program and the award of the scholarship at the same time.
