Master of Science & Technology
# Application Deadlines & Procedure

**APPLICATIONS for the September 2026 intake will open on September 17th, 2025!**

##  Application Deadlines
As seen below, this year we have four application rounds:
The applicants selected by the admissibility jury will be invited to an interview conducted by academic directors and admission staff.

| Application Deadline | Admissibility Jury | Interview Period | Admission decision sent on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ROUND 1** | October 13, 2025 (noon Paris time) | 3rd - 6th November, 2025 | 21st - 28th November, 2025 | Early December |
| **ROUND 2** | January 12, 2026 (noon Paris time) | 4th - 9th February, 2026 | 2nd March - 6th March, 2026 | Mid - March |
| **ROUND 3** | March 9, 2026 (noon Paris time) | 2nd - 8th April, 2026 | 28th April 2026 - 6th May 2026 | Mid - May |
| **ROUND 4** | April 22, 2026 (noon Paris time) | Mid May, 2026 | 26th May - 1st June 2026 | Early June |

When the applications website is open, you can initiate your application at any time. However, you will only be able to finalize it within the above deadlines. **Please note that only students who apply in the first and second rounds of admissions can apply for a scholarship.**

Kindly note that you can only apply for each program once per year. Please note that we do not offer spring term admission.

##  Procedure
  1. Create an account on our application website (https://candidatures.polytechnique.fr/candidatures-mutualisees/). Please use the same email address for your application and any inquiries to us.
  2. Fill in the online application form.
  3. Upload all the required documents. You do not need to send us any paper copies.

[Download the Application Checklist] https://gargantua.polytechnique.fr/siatel-web/app/linkto/...

##  Application fee
The application processing fee for Masters of Science and Technology is **90 euros.**
You can pay by credit card or through an electronic bank transfer. Please note that this fee is non-refundable, even if an application is denied.
