       I.            Presentation of the program
       1. Objectives
As part of the internationalization strategy of its master’s programs, Université Paris-Saclay offers
scholarships to outstanding international students to facilitate their access to its courses. They are
particularly aimed at, but not limited to, those wishing to pursue a PhD.

       2. Eligibility criteria
Students of foreign nationality meeting the following cumulative criteria are eligible to apply:
    • Admission in a master’s degree of Université Paris-Saclay operated by one of the following
       institutions: AgroParisTech, CentraleSupélec, ENS Paris-Saclay, INSTN-CEA, IOGS, Université
       Évry Paris-Saclay, Université Paris-Saclay, UVSQ

    • Under 30 years of age during the year of application
Students who have been or are enrolled in a French higher education institution during their studies

Students who are receiving any other funding above 600 euros per month will be declared ineligible.
The international master’s scholarship cannot be cumulated with France Excellence Eiffel or France
Excellence Europa scholarships, neither with Erasmus Mundus scholarships.

    3. Eligible programs
All academic fields are eligible.
All master’s programs of Université Paris-Saclay are eligible except vocational programs (« formations
en apprentissage »).

    4. Level of studies
Both M1 (first year of master’s) and M2 (second year of master’s) are eligible.

    5. Duration of the scholarship
The scholarship is awarded for the academic year corresponding the year of the call for applications.
No deferment is allowed.
The scholarship will automatically renew for a second year for any laureate admitted in M1 who, upon
completion of all required credit, is admitted in an M2 program of Université Paris-Saclay.

    6. Benefits
The international master’s scholarship is 10 000 euros per year. It is paid monthly by Université Paris-
Saclay for the entire duration of the academic year, from September to June. Its amount is reduced
during months spent abroad (compulsory international mobility, delocalized master’s programs) or
during internships undertaken outside of Université Paris-Saclay affiliated labs.
A yearly flat fee of up to 900 euros supporting travel and visa costs is paid to each laureate. Its amount
depends on the country of origin of the candidate.
Laureates will receive their scholarship as well as travel expenses only upon arrival in France and once
they have registered in the program to which they have been admitted. No advance payment can be
made.
Laureates are supported in their administrative procedures and for the search of accommodation.
However, the reservation of accommodation cannot be guaranteed and it is of laureates’ responsibility
to anticipate the search for housing.

    II.     Regulations of the international master’s scholarship program
Selected among numerous candidates in the framework of the international master’s scholarship
program, you have been awarded a merit-based scholarship by Université Paris-Saclay. Laureates are
invited to refer to the practical modalities outlined in this leaflet.

    1. Duration of the scholarship
The scholarship is paid for one year to any laureate admitted in M2 (second year of master’s).
The scholarship is paid for two years to any laureate admitted in M1 (first year of master’s) provided
they obtain all required credit and are admitted in the second year of master’s at Université Paris-
Saclay.
In case a laureate needs to repeat the year, the scholarship will be stopped.

    2. National and international mobility
The scholarship will be suspended, without any deferment, during any period of time the laureate
attends courses in an institution outside of Université Paris-Saclay (in France or abroad).
But it will be maintained at a reduced rate during compulsory international mobility programs required
by some master’s or academic exchange programs between Université Paris-Saclay and its
international partners (double degrees with compulsory mobility, Erasmus Mundus Joint Master
Degrees…). This compulsory mobility cannot be carried out in the student’s country or institution of
origin.
Payment of the scholarship will be suspended for any non-compulsory mobility.
For delocalized master’s programs, laureates will receive a reduced scholarship for the semesters
spent abroad and a full scholarship for the semesters spent on one of Université Paris-Saclay campuses
in France.

    3. Gap year
Gap years are allowed but not funded.

    4. Internships
Internships in research laboratories must preferably be undertaken within Université Paris-Saclay
research labs. Otherwise, the scholarship will either be reduced or suspended.
Internships in France, aside those within research laboratories of Université Paris-Saclay, which
stipends exceed 1000 euros per month, will lead to a suspension of the scholarship.
Paid or unpaid internships abroad will lead to a suspension of the scholarship.

    5. Benefits
         5.1 Living grant
The international master’s scholarship is 10 000 euros per year.
Its amount is reduced during months spent abroad (compulsory international mobility, delocalized
master’s programs) or during internships undertaken outside of Université Paris-Saclay affiliated labs.

         5.2 Travel and visa flat fee
A yearly flat fee of up to 900 euros supporting travel and visa costs is paid to each laureate. Its amount
depends on the country of origin of the candidate.

        5.3 Scholarship payment modalities
The scholarship is paid in 10 monthly instalments from September to June by bank transfer to an
account in euros opened in the name of the laureate.
Laureates will receive their scholarship as well as travel expenses only upon arrival in France and once
they have registered in the program to which they have been admitted. No advance payment can be
made.

         5.4 Registration and tuition fees
Registration and tuition fees are not covered by the international master’s scholarship of Université
Paris-Saclay. It is of laureates’ responsibility to pay for these fees. A request for partial or full discount
can be made to the enrolment institution but it remains at the sole discretion of this institution.

         5.5 Accommodation
Laureates will be supported in their search for accommodation. However, the reservation of
accommodation cannot be guaranteed and it is of laureates’ responsibility to anticipate the search for
housing.

         5.6 Support
Laureates will be supported in their administrative procedures, particularly for their visa and residence
permits. They will also benefit from all the services offered to international students at Université Paris-
Saclay for their arrival and integration. More information on https://www.universite-paris-
saclay.fr/en/campus-life/international-welcome-desk.
Laureates will be supported with regards to pursuing their studies up to PhD level through information
sessions.

    III.    Obligations of international master’s scholarship laureates
Laureates of the international master’s scholarship programs have rights but they also have
obligations.

    1. Academic program
The international master’s scholarship is granted for a predetermined program for the academic year
corresponding to the call for applications. No change of program nor deferment of the scholarship is
allowed.

    2. Attendance
Laureates must follow the program for which their scholarship was awarded in one of the faculties,
component-institutions and associated universities of Université Paris-Saclay.
The scholarship can be suspended in case of repeated absence without due justification.

    3. Communication
Laureates commit to inform the administration of the international master’s scholarship program of
any change in their situation.

Université Paris-Saclay reserves the right to refuse or cancel the scholarship awarded if the information
provided by the candidate proves to be false or in the event that they do not respect the program
regulations.


The administration of the international master’s scholarship program is available to help you
throughout your stay. Do not hesitate to contact us.

We wish you success in your studies and a wonderful stay at Université Paris-Saclay!
