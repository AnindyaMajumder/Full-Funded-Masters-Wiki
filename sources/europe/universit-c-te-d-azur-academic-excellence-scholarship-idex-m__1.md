# Academic Excellence Scholarships
###  **IDEX SCHOLARSHIPS FOR INTERNATIONAL MSc PROGRAMS**

Source: https://univ-cotedazur.eu/msc-programs/tuition-fees/are-you-an-international-student/academic-excellence-scholarships
Accessed: 2026-06-18

Each year Université Côte d'Azur awards **10 IdEx scholarships to the best MSc candidates**. The recipients of these grants of excellence will be exempt from tuition fees and will receive a stipend of €5,000, paid in two installments for **one year only.** The disbursement of the scholarship is conditional upon the student's regular attendance in classes and examinations, as well as exemplary conduct throughout the academic year.

Eligibility

To be eligible, students will have to first apply to one of Université Côte d'Azur's international programs listed below:
  * European MSc in Molecular Pathology
  * MSc Biobanks and Complex Data Management
  * MSc Data Science and Artificial Intelligence
  * MSc Modeling of Neuronal and Cognitive Systems
  * MSc Biocontrol Solutions for Plant Health
  * MSc Environmental Hazards and Risks Management
  * MSc MARRES, Ocean Science, Conservation and Innovation (Science & Society)
  * MSc Smart-EdTech, Co-creativity and Technology Enhanced Learning
  * MSc Creative Music and Sound Design for Visual Media Scoring
  * MSc Management of the Flavor and Fragrance Industry
  * MSc TV Content Creation and Innovation

The main criterion of selection is academic excellence, so candidates of any age or nationality can apply as long as their results match expectations. Note that students who have already been registered at Université Côte d'Azur are **NOT** eligible. Also, this scholarship cannot be combined with any other.

Dates & Deadlines

**Applications are currently closed.** Application Period: April to May annually. _* Applications received after this date will be automatically rejected. * Results will be announced by email mid-June, favorable or not._

How to Apply

Students should apply for the MSc program of their choice **BEFORE** applying for the IdEx Scholarship. Applications must be submitted via the form provided here and upload the requested documents. The form will be available once applications reopen, from April annually. _*Candidates will be assessed based on their provided results. Applications submitted without an eCandidat number will not be considered_
**Applications are Closed**

### **FRANCE EXCELLENCE EUROPA SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM**
International students from 26 European countries may be awarded the **France Excellence Europa scholarship** to pursue a **Master's degree** at Université Côte d'Azur for a maximum period of 12 months (M2) and 24 months (M1).
  * For more information, please visit [Campus France's webpage](https://www.campusfrance.org/en/france-excellence-europa-scholarship-program)

### **EIFFEL SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM OF EXCELLENCE**
  * The Eiffel scholarship program is a tool developed by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to enable French higher education institutions to attract the best foreign students to graduate programs at the master's and doctoral levels.
  * More information [here](https://www.campusfrance.org/en/eiffel-scholarship-program-of-excellence)
