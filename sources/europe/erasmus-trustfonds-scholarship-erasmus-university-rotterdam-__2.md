ETS in combination with TSH Changemaker Scholarship
The Erasmus Trustfonds-TSH Changemaker Scholarship supports underrepresented students to pursue a master programme at Erasmus University Rotterdam. The Erasmus Trustfonds scholarship covers the statutory tuition fee and a monthly living allowance of € 600. The **TSH Changemaker Scholarship** provides a one year housing in a Social Hub Residence. Non-EU/EEA citizens who qualify for the Institutional tuition fee will receive a partial tuition fee waiver from the school (if applicable). By combining tuition fee coverage, housing support, and access to a vibrant changemaker community, this scholarship empowers talented individuals to thrive academically, socially, and professionally. Awardees benefit from mentorship, training, and a lifelong network of changemakers committed to shaping inclusive and sustainable cities.

## Requirements
  * Unconditionally admitted to the master at the start of the program
  * Belonging to one or more disadvantaged or underrepresented groups, including but not limited to:
    * Limited financial, educational, social, or health resources
    * Refugee or newcomer status
    * Ethnic/religious marginalisation
    * Disabilities (physical or mental)
    * First-generation student
    * LGBTQI+ identity with potential safety risks
    * Reduced social capital or sense of security

Demonstrated community involvement, social contribution, and commitment to engaging with the Changemaker alumni network

**Applicable to:**
  * Nationalities varies per school
  * Participating faculties:
    * Erasmus School of Law (ESL)
    * Erasmus School of Social and Behavioural Sciences (ESSB)
    * Erasmus School of Health Policy & Management (ESHPM)
    * Rotterdam School of Management (RSM)
    * Erasmus School of Philosophy (ESPhil)
  * Level of study: Master

Scholarship/Grant in € 9.895 for costs of living and statutory fee; one year accommodation in a residence of the Social Hub, partial tuition fee waiver if applicable.
