# Aalborg University — Apply for a Master's programme (deadlines)
Source: https://www.en.aau.dk/education/apply/master
Accessed: 2026-06-18

## Important dates — September intake
Please check the programme website to see if the specific programme has a September intake.
- Application portal opens: Expected medio December
- Application deadline (for all applicants): 1 March 23:59 CET
- Answer to your application: No later than 10 June
- Accept/decline study place: No later than 15 June
- Available study places announced: 20 June

## Important dates — February intake
Please check the programme website to see if the specific programme has a February intake.
- Application portal opens: Medio August
- Application deadline (for all applicants): 15 October 23:59 CET
- Answer to your application: No later than 10 November
- Accept/decline study place: No later than 15 November
- Available study places announced: 18 November
