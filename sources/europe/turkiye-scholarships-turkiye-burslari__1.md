# Why Türkiye Scholarships?

#### Apply now to get a fully funded and, high-quality education in Türkiye!

### Türkiye Scholarships;
* Provides **university and department placements** unlike other higher education scholarship programs.
* **Covers** tuition fees.
* Allows you to learn the language of the country by studying **a 1-year Turkish language course.**
* Provides **accommodation** for students arriving in a new country.
* Covers your **once-off flight ticket.**
* Allows you to take full advantage of widespread health services with **general health insurance.**
* Also provides **monthly stipend.** 4.500 TL at Associate’s and Bachelor’s levels, 6.500 TL at Master's level and 9.000 TL at PhD level per month.

#### Monthly Stipend
A monthly stipend of 4.500 TL at undergraduate level, 6.500 TL at master’s level, and 9.000 TL at doctorate level is deposited regularly each month into the bank account you will open upon your arrival in Türkiye. Academically qualified students will be rewarded with monthly stipend 2 times higher than the regular payment as part of Merit Scholarship Program.

#### Accommodation
Students at master's and doctoral levels are given the right to stay in the dormitory in the first year of their education. In the following years, it is possible for our graduate students to rent a house with a housing allowance of 6.000 TL for students studying in Istanbul and Ankara and 5.000 TL for those studying in other cities.

#### University and Department Placement
Candidates are given the opportunity to select between 12 choices in online applications through the Türkiye Scholarships Application System (TBBS).

#### One Time Flight Ticket
As Türkiye Scholarships, we provide once-off fight tickets.

#### Turkish Language Course (1 Year)
We offer 1 year of free Turkish language education to all our students.

#### University Fees
While studying at the most prestigious universities at world standards, your university tuition is covered by Türkiye Scholarships.
