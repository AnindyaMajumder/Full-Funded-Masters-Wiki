# Erasmus Trust Fund Scholarship (ESHPM track)
Source: https://www.eur.nl/en/eshpm/education/grants/erasmus-trust-fund-scholarship
Accessed: 2026-06-18

CRITICAL NOTE: The ESHPM faculty version of the Erasmus Trust Fund Scholarship is restricted
to EEA/EU nationals ONLY ("You are an EEA/EU national"). This is the OPPOSITE of the ESE
track (source __1), which is explicitly open to non-EEA applicants. Eligibility varies per
faculty/school. A Bangladeshi (non-EEA) applicant must apply via a faculty whose track admits
non-EEA students — the Erasmus School of Economics (ESE) track does.

Application deadline: 1 March 2026, 23:59 CET

## Eligibility
You can apply for this scholarship if you meet all the points below:
  * You are an EEA/EU national.
  * You are eligible for the statutory tuition fee.
  * You have never studied at a Dutch institution.
  * You have been admitted to one of these ESHPM master's programmes: Health Care Management (HCM) / Health Economics, Policy & Law (HEPL)
  * Enrolled by 1 September 2026.

## Terms
  * The scholarship is valid for academic year 2026-2027.
  * Meeting eligibility does not guarantee selection due to limited scholarships.
  * Non-compliance with terms may result in withdrawal of the scholarship.

## Required documents
Upload all documents as PDF files.
  * Motivation statement (max. two A4 pages)
  * CV
  * Confirmation of application HEPL or HCM

## How to apply
Step 1: Apply for one of the master's programmes listed above.
Step 2: Complete the application form and upload all required documents before the deadline.
Only complete applications will be reviewed.
For more details about this scholarship, please see the university's website:
https://www.eur.nl/en/scholarship/erasmus-trustfonds-scholarship
