# Find out if you need to pay fees (University Admissions Sweden)

## What are the criteria?
In general, individuals who are citizens of countries that are not part of the European Union (EU), European Economic Area (EEA, includes Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway) or Switzerland are required to pay application and tuition fees. There are some exceptions to this general rule.

### Exemptions from paying fees
You are **NOT** required to pay application and tuition fees if you:
  * have Swedish citizenship
  * have been granted a permanent Swedish residence permit
  * have been granted a temporary Swedish residence permit for reasons other than studies
  * have applied for an extension of your expired temporary Swedish permit which was granted for reasons other than studies
  * have citizenship in an EU or EEA country or Switzerland
  * are a family member of a citizen of an EU or EEA country and have temporary or permanent right of residence in Sweden
  * are a family member of a citizen of Switzerland and have been granted a Swedish residence permit
  * have long-term resident status in Sweden
  * have long-term resident status in another country in the European Union and have a Swedish residence permit for reasons other than studies
  * are a British citizen who started living in Sweden on or before 31 December 2020
  * are already registered for studies at a Swedish university (exempt from the application fee, but will still need to pay tuition fees)
  * are applying for or are registered for third cycle (doctoral) studies
  * are registered at a university outside of Sweden and will be studying in Sweden on a temporary basis as part of an official study abroad programme as an exchange student

(In general, citizens of countries not part of the EU/EEA or Switzerland are required to pay application and tuition fees — i.e., they are "fee-paying students". Bangladesh is not in the EU/EEA/Switzerland, so a Bangladeshi national is a fee-paying student and thus the eligible group for the scholarship.)
