# KU Leuven International Scholarship Fund — Sponsor / funding source
Source: https://giving.kuleuven.be/funds/international-scholarship-fund
Accessed: 2026-06-18

At KU Leuven, we believe in the boundless power of young talent. Students from around the world bring not only their dreams but also unique perspectives and skills.
The KU Leuven International Scholarship Fund gives young talent from all corners of the world the opportunity to pursue a master's degree at KU Leuven, offering them a springboard to a bright future.

The fund awards scholarships every year. How many? That depends on your support. You can donate for one or more of these scholarships:
  * Inspiring the Outstanding Scholarship — for students from around the world
  * KU Leuven Scholarship for Africa — for students from Africa
  * KU Leuven ASEAN Scholarship — for students from Southeast Asia
  * KU Leuven Scholarship for Central and South America — for students from Central and South America

Meet the first scholarship recipients from the International Scholarship Fund who began their studies in September 2025 (testimonials: Maria Abaurre, Thao Le, Eslam Soliman, Constanza Wageman).

Professor Tom Van Puyenbroeck, Vice Rector of Student Affairs and Diversity Policy, is manager of the fund.
