# Scholarships
Each year, we award around 20 excellence scholarships to the top Master’s students (M1 and M2) enrolled in core AI integration programs.
**Eligibility Criteria**
These scholarships are open to all students, regardless of nationality.
Applicants for an M1 scholarship must have obtained a Bachelor’s degree or equivalent.
Applicants for an M2 scholarship must have successfully completed the first year of a Master’s program (M1).
These scholarships cannot be combined with other funding sources such as apprenticeship or professional training contracts. However, they can be combined with need-based social grants
**Application Procedure**
You must submit as many scholarship applications as there are candidacies for ANITI labeled Masters (listed in the table below).
**Application deadline: May 2, 2026**
**Application File Requirements for a M1 scholarship:**
  * Submit a motivation letter for AI studies and PhD research by clicking the following link: <https://enquete.univ-toulouse.fr/index.php/356317?lang=fr>


**Application File Requirements for a M2 scholarship (for current M1 students):**
  * Motivation letter for AI studies and PhD research,
  * M1 application file,
  * M1 academic transcripts. 


These documents must be combined into **a single PDF file (under 10MB)** and uploaded via the following link: [https://enquete.univ-toulouse.fr/index.php/465874?lang=en](https://enquete.univ-toulouse.fr/index.php/465874?lang=fr)
**Application for a Master 2 (M2) Scholarship (for engineering students in a double-degree program):**
  * Motivation letter for AI studies and PhD research,
  * Academic transcripts for semesters 5 to 7.


These documents must be combined into **a single PDF file (under 10MB)** and uploaded via the following link: [https://enquete.univ-toulouse.fr/index.php/465874?lang=en](https://enquete.univ-toulouse.fr/index.php/465874?lang=fr)
**Selection Process:**
Complete applications received before the deadline will be reviewed by the ANITI **Education Committee** , following consultation with the **Master’s program directors**.
The primary selection criteria are **academic excellence** and **motivation to join a research project within an ANITI chair** , starting with the M2 internship (for more information, visit [www.aniti.univ-toulouse.fr](https://aniti.univ-toulouse.fr/en/)).
**Scholarship Duration and Amount:**
The scholarship is awarded for a duration of **one year**. The total amount is **€10,000** , distributed in **10 monthly payments of €1,000** (with the first payment in November 2026).
