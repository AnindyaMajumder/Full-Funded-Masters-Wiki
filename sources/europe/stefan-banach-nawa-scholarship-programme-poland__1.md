#  Banach NAWA Scholarship Programme 
Banach NAWA programme offers scholarships for full-time second-cycle studies conducted in Polish or English at universities supervised by the Minister of Science and Higher Education. 
Our main goal is to support the socio-economic development of developing countries by improving the level of education and professional qualifications of their citizens.
**What does the programme offer?**
  * monthly scholarship of PLN 2,500
  * exemption from tuition fees at public universities
  * preparatory course funded by NAWA (Polish-track)
  * travel allowance (PLN 2,500)
  * studies in Polish or English
  * Polish language and culture course (English-track)


**Who can apply?**
The programme is open to citizens of **36 countries covered by Polish Aid** from Eastern Europe, Asia, Africa and Latin America.
The full list of eligible countries is available in the [call announcement](https://nawa.gov.pl/en/students/foreign-students/the-banach-scholarship-programme/call-for-proposals).
The Programme is a joint initiative of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Polish National Agency for Academic Exchange in the form of Polish development aid.
