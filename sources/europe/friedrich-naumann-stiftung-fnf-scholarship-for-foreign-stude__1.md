#  Scholarships for foreign students from non-EU countries 
We support committed and liberal-minded students with very good grades in full study at German universities. As a foreign student, you can apply for a scholarship to study for a master's or main study stage of the state examination. To be funded you must still have two remaining semesters before you complete your studies. Our scholarships to foreign students are funded by and in accordance with the **[requirements of the Federal Foreign Office](https://www.bva.bund.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/Aufgaben/ZMV/Zuwendungen_national/aa_stiftungen_richtlinien_bnbest.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3)** (link in German).
The scholarships do not have to be repaid. Funding is initially granted for one year. You may apply for an extension for another year until you complete your studies.
Unfortunately, we **cannot** fund bachelor's programmes, study and research stays or internships abroad.
