##  2. Application fee
They are fixed at 90 €*.
*Students enrolled in a training cycle at École Polytechnique are exempt from application fees.

##  3. Deposit payment
Students are required to pay one non-refundable deposit of €3,200, according to the information and timetable provided upon admission to the programme. The deposits will be deducted from their total tuition fees.
