# Financial Aid Packages
## GSSE Financial Aid Options
There exists a wide spectrum of financial aid and scholarship opportunities and packages for graduate students at the Graduate School of Sciences and Engineering. These include "KU Graduate Fellowship" funded by Koç University and "Project-based Graduate Scholarship" co-funded by external sources such as TÜBİTAK, EU, industry through projects, and Koç University.

### Koç University Presidential Fellowship
Eligible candidates are offered: **(effective October 2026)**

**Presidential PhD Fellowship – for students with outstanding research and leadership potential.**
  * Full Tuition Coverage (~$25K per year)
  * New Students: Granted for 2 years (can apply for extension up to 4 years)
  * Existing Students: Granted for 1 year (can apply for extension up to 4 years)
  * Stipend: 83.000 TL ** supported fully from KU funds or as a top-up for grants
  * Free Dormitory Room or Housing Allowance (10.000 TL/month)
  * Private health insurance (limited coverage)
  * Meal Allowance
  * Free HGS (Fast Transit Pass) car stickers for campus entrance
  * Travel Grants for Conferences

**Presidential MS Fellowship – for students with outstanding research and leadership potential.**
  * Full Tuition Coverage (~$25K per year)
  * New Students: Granted for 2 years
  * Existing Students: Granted for 1 year
  * Stipend: 57.500 TL ** supported fully from KU funds or as a top-up for grants
  * Free Dormitory Room or Housing Allowance (6.700 TL/month)
  * Private health insurance (limited coverage)
  * Meal Allowance
  * Travel Grants for Conferences

### KU Graduate Fellowship
KU Graduate Fellowship is a competitive MS and Ph.D. scholarship opportunity. Applicants having an excellent academic record and/or having high impact scientific publications are considered for KU Graduate Fellowship.
KU Graduate Fellowship includes the following stipend & benefits: **(effective October 2026)**

**PhD Students:**
  * Tuition waiver
  * Monthly stipend of 60.000 TL
  * Free Dormitory Room or Housing Allowance (10.000 TL/month)
  * Private health insurance (limited coverage)
  * Meal Card
  * Free HGS (Fast Transit Pass) car stickers for campus entrance
  * Travel support (once a year, at a yearly determined fixed amount) to attend scientific events

**MS Students:**
  * Tuition waiver
  * Monthly stipend of 39.000 TL
  * Dormitory room may be offered to a limited number of MS students depending on the availability of resources or Housing Allowance (6.700 TL/month)
  * Private health insurance (limited coverage)
  * Meal Card
  * Travel support (once a year, at a yearly determined fixed amount) to attend scientific events

### Project-based Graduate Scholarship
MS and Ph.D. students are accepted to participate in specific research projects funded by external sources such as TÜBİTAK, EU, or industry. The stipend varies depending on the project. Koç University provides the same benefits for all Project-based Graduate Scholarships.
  * Tuition waiver
  * Monthly stipend (depending on the project source; MS or Ph.D. study)
  * Dormitory room (limited number of MS students depending on availability)
  * Private health insurance (limited coverage)
  * Meal Card
  * Free HGS car stickers (for Ph.D. students only)
  * Travel support

### Tuition Waiver Only Scholarships
MS and Ph.D. students accepted with "Tuition Waiver" will be exempt from the yearly tuition fee. The tuition waiver scholarship does not include any side benefits.
