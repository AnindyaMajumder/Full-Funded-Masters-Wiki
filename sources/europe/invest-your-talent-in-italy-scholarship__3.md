#  History And Mission 
“A tree: the slow explosion of a seed.” 
BRUNO MUNARI
The Invest Your Talent in Italy program was launched in 2005 with the aim of promoting intercultural dialogue and fostering understanding and integration among the younger generations. 
Over the years, more universities have embraced the program's mission, offering a rich and up-to-date range of courses to provide foreign students with rewarding and successful opportunities. Similarly, more countries interested in building bridges between their most promising students and Italy have joined the program. Finally, companies and trade associations return every year to welcome Invest Your Talent students and train them for work through internships in line with the nature of the program's training and vocation. 
Knowledge and professionalism are and will continue to be the main assets of Invest Your Talent in Italy, which currently includes 23 countries in Asia, Africa, and Latin America, more than 20 universities, and contact with numerous Italian businesses. 
Discover the milestones that have characterized this wonderful 20-year project on the timeline on the Home Page! 
Just as a lush plant needs good seeds to flourish and grow, talent and skills need someone to help them blossom.
