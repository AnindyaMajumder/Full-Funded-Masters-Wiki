## CATÓLICA TOP+ SCHOLARSHIP
The **Católica TOP+** scholarship is a **merit-based** award offered by Católica-Lisbon SBE, designed to support outstanding Master’s candidates, which can cover between **10-90%** of your **Tuition Fees** (Exchange Semesters and Double Degrees are not covered by the Scholarship). We invite all exceptional candidates to take advantage of this opportunity!
This scholarship is **only available through your Master’s Application,** which means that scholarship applications will not be accepted after the submission of the application. 
Please note that scholarships are awarded on a **rolling basis** , meaning funds may be allocated before the end of the Application Rounds. Given the high demand and limited availability, we strongly encourage you to **apply early** to increase your chances.
