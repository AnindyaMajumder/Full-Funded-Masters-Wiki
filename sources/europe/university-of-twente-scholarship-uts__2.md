The tuition fee you will need to pay depends on two factors: your nationality and the type of programme you have chosen to follow.

## Tuition fees for non-EU/EEA students
If you are a national of a non-EU/EEA country, you will need to pay the so-called 'institutional fee'. This fee is higher than the statutory fee because Dutch universities do not receive government funding for non-EU/EEA students.
