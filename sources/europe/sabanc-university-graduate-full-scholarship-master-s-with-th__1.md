#  Graduate Scholarships
**Almost all students** admitted to Sabancı University’s academic graduate programs (except professional graduate programs)**receive full exemption from annual tuition fees.**
In addition to tuition waiver, **majority of Sabancı University graduate students** receive further support, which includes monthly stipend and free dorm room. 
Details of the scholarships available for the **2026-2027 Academic year, which is effective as of September 2026 for continuing and new graduate students,** can be found below. 
Admission and scholarship decisions, which are based on academic merit, for applicants are made during the evaluation process. Scholarship recommendations are made by the Admission Jury and then finalized by the Board of the relevant Graduate School. 

## DISTINGUISHED SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY SCHOLARSHIP 
Sabancı University’s "**Distinguished Science and Technology Scholarship** " is a scholarship awarded to exceptionally successful students, who apply to or have passed the qualification stage in the _PhD programs of the Graduate School of Engineering and Natural Sciences._
PhD candidates with exceptional credentials may be eligible for Distinguished Science & Technology Scholarships based on a separate evaluation by the scholarship committee.

## PROJECT SCHOLARSHIP 
These are scholarships provided by research grants received from TÜBİTAK, Industry, NGO’s, etc.
_TÜBİTAK Research Projects Stipends:_ **47.850 TL/month for MA/MS; 74.250 TL/month for PhD; 82.500 TL/month for PhD-after qualifying exams.**

## TUITION WAIVER SCHOLARSHIP 
Graduate students may be admitted with a scholarship that provides full or partial exemption from the annual tuition fee.

## TUBITAK BIDEB SCHOLARSHIP 
Sabancı University encourages students to apply to the 2210 - National Graduate Scholarship Program or 2211 - National Doctorate Scholarship Program, which is run by TÜBİTAK-BİDEB.
