# Scholarships
### Scholarships at Koç University for international students
Koç University prides itself in offering scholarship packages for high-caliber international and domestic undergraduate and graduate students that meet our selective admissions criteria.

### Undergraduate International Scholarships
Koç University awards merit-based tuition scholarships of 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100% to outstanding candidates each year (except for Medicine). There is no separate application period or scholarship form. All candidates will be informed if they have been awarded a scholarship or not with their admission offer. To be considered for a merit-based scholarship please keep the following information in mind before submitting your application:
  * Submitting an application without one of the required exam scores (SAT/ACT, IB, A-Levels, or others) significantly diminishes your admission chances and therefore your scholarship chances.
  * All admitted students are first evaluated for a merit-based scholarship by the Admissions Committee on the basis of academic merit only (need-blind). These scholarships can be awarded for 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100% of the annual tuition for the duration of the program. Merit-based scholarships do not include any other benefits (housing, living expenses, books, etc).
  * The School of Medicine does NOT award any type of merit or need-based scholarships for admitted international students.
  * Scholarship results are communicated to candidates with the admission offer letter from our Registrar's Office via email.
  * For specific scholarships based on citizenship please visit the pages under "Scholarships" on this website or email us at study@ku.edu.tr for details.

### Graduate International Scholarships (Master with Thesis/Research Masters)
To be considered for a scholarship:
  * Complete and submit the online application form for your Master with Thesis program of interest with all the required documents (this includes a valid GRE/GMAT and TOEFL test score). There are NO SEPARATE SCHOLARSHIP FORMS. Graduate program applications are available [here](https://koc.dreamapply.com/enter/22).
  * Submitting an application without the required exam scores (GRE/GMAT, TOEFL) significantly diminishes your admission chances and therefore your scholarship chances.
  * All admitted Master with Thesis students are awarded 100% merit-based scholarships by the relevant Graduate School (except for the Graduate School of Health Sciences). Merit-based scholarships may also include other benefits (living expenses, health insurance) depending on the Graduate School. Please note that the LLM with Thesis program is not included in this category.
  * Scholarship results are communicated to candidates with the admission offer letter from the relevant Graduate School via email on the dates indicated on their respective websites under "Admission".
  * For specific Master scholarships based on citizenship please visit the pages under "Scholarships" on this website or email us at study@ku.edu.tr for details.

### Graduate International Scholarships (Master without Thesis/Taught Masters)
Professional master's programs (Master without Thesis) at Koç University in the fields of Data Science, Cybersecurity, Computer Engineering, LLM, Public Policy, Global Health, MBA, EMBA, Finance, and others, are offered on an affordable tuition-paying basis to both domestic and international students. There are no tuition scholarships for these programs.
If you need the scholarship to pursue your graduate studies we recommend that you prepare and submit an application to our Master with Thesis programs instead.
The Turkish Government's Turkish Scholarships (Türkiye Bursları) program at Koç University does NOT include support for Master Non-Thesis programs.

### Graduate International Scholarships (Ph.D./Doctoral)
Koç University offers all Ph.D. domestic and international admitted students a full scholarship for the duration of their studies. Admission to our Ph.D. programs is very competitive due to the very large number of qualified applicants.
  * Complete and submit the online application form for your Ph.D. program of interest with all the required documents. There are NO SEPARATE SCHOLARSHIP FORMS. Graduate program applications are available [here](https://koc.dreamapply.com/enter/22).
  * Submitting an application without the required exam scores (GRE/GMAT, TOEFL) significantly diminishes your admission chances and therefore your scholarship chances.
  * All admitted Ph.D. students are awarded 100% merit-based scholarships by the relevant Graduate School (except the Graduate School of Health Sciences). Merit-based scholarships may also include other benefits (housing, living expenses, health insurance) depending on the Graduate School.
