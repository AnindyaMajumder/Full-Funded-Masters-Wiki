## Key verbatim section (resolves the "Starr" confusion)

One of the purposes of the Starr International Foundation is to promote education in Switzerland and abroad. The HSG Starr International Foundation Fund supports excellence scholarships of the Best Talents Programme.

In this very spirit, the St. Gallen Symposium partners with the University of St. Gallen, to enable one annual excellence scholarship of one Bachelor student.

## CONCLUSION
The Starr International Foundation Fund funds the Best Talents Programme (EU/EFTA / Swiss C-permit only). There is therefore NO general, all-nationality, self-applicable master's "Excellence Scholarship" track at HSG that a Bangladeshi national could apply for. The only all-nationality merit Excellence Scholarships are explicitly for Bachelor students. Bangladesh gate FAILS for the master's program.
