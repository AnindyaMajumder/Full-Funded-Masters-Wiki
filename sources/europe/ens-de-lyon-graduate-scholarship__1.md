#  Application for Master's degrees and scholarships
Applicant's profile
Degree/Program
[Application for a Master's degree](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/en/taxonomy/term/595)
ENS de Lyon and its partners offer scholarships (Ampère, GRADUATE +) for excellent international students to enroll in Master's programs.
For thorough guidance on the choice of Master's degree, please read carefully the[ Master's programs description](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/en/studies/academic-programs/masters-2022-2026). The International Student Office will not be able to assist you in this regard. 
For ENS de Lyon, [Master’s programs description are available here ](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/en/studies/academic-programs/masters-2022-2026)For Graduate + Scholarship, [Master’s programs description are available here](https://graduate-plus.fr/bourses-dexcellence/)
## Conditions
**To apply for the first year of a Master's program (M1),** applicants should justify holding a L3 degree (Bachelor - 180 ECTS) or, for applicants who have studied abroad, an equivalent level.
**To apply for the second year of a Master's program (M2)** , applicants should justify holding a M1 degree (240 ECTS) or, for applicants who have studied abroad, an equivalent level.
Applications are approved by the admission committee.

### Ampère Merit Scholarships
  * Master's programs eligible: Master’s programs in Exact Sciences, the Arts, and Human and Social Sciences (except FEADéP Master's programs and a few Master's programs). Consult the Ampère scholarship rules (document on the right)
  * Amount: 1,000€ per month for 12 months 


**Specific partnerships with some countries** - US students enrolled in a US University applying for a Master's program at the ENS de Lyon can also apply for[ the Lafayette Fellowship from the French Embassy in the USA](https://lafayette.villa-albertine.org/).- Canadian students enrolled in a Canadian university applying for a Master’s program at ENS de Lyon can also apply for [an Embassy of France in Canada scholarship](https://www.canada.campusfrance.org/fr/programmes-de-bourse)- Indian students enrolled in an Indian university applying for a Master’s program at ENS de Lyon can also apply for [an Embassy of France in India scholarship](https://www.inde.campusfrance.org/co-financedscholarships)Applications possible for all ENS de Lyon Masters opened to international students
### GRADUATE + Scholarship
Graduate+ scholarships are awarded to the best international students selected for a Master’s degree in the following Graduate initiatives and Master’s programmes, either in M1 or M2.
Those training programmes are part of the Graduate+ project involving four French universities on the Lyon - Saint-Etienne site: Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1, Ecole Normale Supérieure de Lyon, Université Jean Moulin and Université Jean Monnet. 
  * CORE (Conflits and Recompositions in a globalized World) :[ European studies](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/en/studies/academic-programs/masters-2022-2026/european-studies), [Advanced Economics](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/en/studies/academic-programs/masters-2022-2026/advanced-economics)
  * DynLifeSys (Multiscale dynamics of living systems) :[ Biosciences](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/en/studies/academic-programs/masters-2022-2026/biosciences),[Biosciences for modeling of complex systems](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/en/studies/academic-programs/masters-2022-2026/biosciences-and-modeling-complex-systems)
  * MathInfi (Mathematics, foundations of computer sciences and interactions) : Advanced mathematics master (M2) , Computer science: concepts and applications (M1&M2), Maths in action (M2), Master Mathématiques appliquées, statistiques (M1), Master Mathématiques Générales (M1), Master Machine Learning and Data Mining (M1&M2)[Detailed information here](https://graduate-plus.fr/mathematics-foundations-of-computer-science-and-interactions/2025-master-bourses-dexcellence-mathinfi/)
  * PACE (Physics, Astro, Chemistry, Earth) - M2 only :Physics & chemistry, Life, earth and universe sciences, Sciences of earth, planets and environment, Synthèse Organique et Chimie des Molécules Bioactives, Ocean, Atmospheric and Climate Sciences, Q-light, Cosmology[Detailed information here. ](https://graduate-plus.fr/en/physics-astrophysics-chemistry-earth/ "Page PACE")Any question regarding PACE initiative, please send an email to : graduateplus.pace@ens-lyon.fr
  * Amount: 1,000€ per month for 10 months
  * Scholarships funded within the framework of the French government’s SFRI programme.
  * _Ce travail a bénéficié d’une aide de l’État gérée par l’Agence Nationale de la Recherche au titre de France 2030 portant la référence « ANR-21-SFRI-0001 »._


## Application process
**Call for application 2026-2027 is now closed.**
_**Please note** : before beginning your application: carefully read all the information and choose your program_
For Ampere Scholarship, [Master’s programs description are available here ](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/en/studies/academic-programs/masters-2022-2026)For Graduate + Scholarship, [Master’s programs description are available here](https://graduate-plus.fr/bourses-dexcellence/)
**NON-EUROPEAN students are students:** - who do not have the nationality of one of the countries of the European Economic Area (the 27 countries of the European Union, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein), or of Switzerland, Monaco and Andorra.and- who have obtained a higher education degree in a foreign country.**EUROPEAN students are students:** who have the nationality of one of the countries of the European Economic Area (the 27 countries of the European Union, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein).

_**Please note: these forms allow you to apply for a Master's program and for a scholarship of excellence.**_
**Important information: by clicking on the following link, you will have to register to access the form.**
_Application on the wrong form will not be considered._

  * As a European student - Apply here – Online Form - CLOSED
  * As a non European student with a higher education degree in a foreign country - Apply here – Online Form - CLOSED

## Documents requested
Please note: the International Student Office will not pre-validate the application. It is the responsibility of the student to check that all the requested documents have been uploaded. **The uploaded documents will be examined after the validation of the online application.**
See below the documents that the candidates have to upload in the application form -**in French or English only** :
  * **All candidates:**
    * Official identity document
    * Motivation Letter
    * Curriculum Vitae
    * Certificate of diplomas and degrees already obtained (ex: Bachelor degree) - documents are accepted only in French or English. If the document is in another language, you have to provide a translated document in French or English, officially certified
    * University academic transcript of records - documents are accepted only in French or English. If the document is in another language, you have to provide a translated document in French or English, officially certified
    * For the current year: proof of enrollment - documents are accepted only in French or English 
  * **Other documents**
    * Additional documents are requested depending on the chosen program. Those documents could be about research projects, language certificates, letters of recommendation and any other document useful for the academic advisors. If you do not have a document, an explanation is expected in pdf format.


All the information about language proficiency requirements (French and/or English) and documents to provide [are available on this page](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/en/studies/student-information/applicants-what-are-language-requirements) - exceptions accepted are indicated at the end of this specific page.
