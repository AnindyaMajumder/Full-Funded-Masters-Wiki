# Bachelor's and Master's Scholarships
Source: https://www.studyinfinland.fi/funding-your-studies/bachelors-and-masters-scholarships
Accessed: 2026-06-20
Updated on page: 04.06.2026

Scholarships can help you manage tuition costs in Finland, but they are competitive and do not cover any living costs. Understanding what scholarship options exist will help you plan wisely.

## How to Apply for a scholarship?
Scholarships and tuition fee waivers in Finland are offered by the individual universities - there are no governmental scholarships available. If the university of your choice offers scholarships, remember that they are competitive and will typically only cover your tuition fees partially. You must independently cover your own living costs. With some universities, you can apply for a scholarship at the same time as you submit your admission application — making it easy to plan your studies and funding together. In other cases, scholarships may become available after you've started your studies. These can for example reward your progress, academic success, or even your growing Finnish language skills.

### Find Your Programme
Start by picking the degree programme that interests you the most. You can find a suitable programme either on the universities' own websites or at Studyinfo.fi.

### Explore Scholarships
Once you have found a suitable study programme, check if the programme offers scholarship, tuition fee waiver or "early bird" tuition fee options for non-EU/EEA students. You can typically find instructions for applying for a scholarship alongside the application information.
If you have questions or need a bit more guidance, check out the scholarship information on the universities' own websites.

Scholarships for studying in Finland - facts
You may have seen posts and ads promoting "Fully funded Finland government scholarships" that would cover everything from university tuition fees to travel and living costs in Finland. Be cautious - those claims are misleading. Posts and ads like that contain a lot of false information.
