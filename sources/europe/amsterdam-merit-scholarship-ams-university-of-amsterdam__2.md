# Scholarships (Faculty of Science)
The Faculty of Science aims to attract talented students to its international classrooms. It offers a limited number of scholarships designed to offer non-EU/EER excellent and motivated students the opportunity to pursue a Master of Science degree at the University of Amsterdam.
## Amsterdam Merit Scholarship (AMS)
For: An outstanding student from outside the European Union / European Economic Area (EEA)
Amount: for the exact amount please check the link here below
Application deadline: 15 January 2026
[ Read more about AMS requirements and application ](https://gss.uva.nl/scholarships/amsterdam-merit-scholarship/ams-faculty-of-science.html)
