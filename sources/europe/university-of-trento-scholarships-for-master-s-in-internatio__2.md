# University of Trento — Scholarships for Master's Degrees in International Studies (a.y. 2026/2027)

Source: Consulate General of Italy in Houston (republishing the official University of Trento call)
URL: https://conshouston.esteri.it/en/news/dal_consolato/2026/02/universita-di-trento-borse-di-studio-per-lauree-magistrali-in-studi-internazionali-a-a-2026-2027/
Accessed: 2026-06-18 (note: unitn.it main + international.unitn.it are behind Cloudflare/redirect; consulate page + corsi.unitn.it admission pages + official call PDF used as ground truth)

## Programs Offered (2026/2027, taught entirely in English)
- Master's Degree in International Security Studies (MISS) — in collaboration with Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna di Pisa
- Master's Degree in European and International Studies (MEIS)

## Scholarship Details for Non-European Candidates (a.y. 2026/2027)
- Application Deadline: March 4, 2026
- Eligibility includes individuals who:
  - Hold non-EU citizenship and don't reside in Italy
  - Hold non-EU citizenship with Italian study residence permits (not resident in Italy)
  - Hold non-EU citizenship with temporary protection status (not resident in Italy, no Italian secondary diploma)
- Financial Support:
  - 2 scholarships available for MISS program
  - 2 scholarships available for MEIS program
  - Each provides €7,200 per year (partial contribution to living/mobility expenses)
  - Additional tuition waivers may be granted based on admission ranking
- Additional Opportunity: Descendants of Trentino emigrants may access separate scholarship funding.

## European/Equivalent Candidates
A second application period runs April 1 – June 30, 2026, for EU citizens, residents of Norway/Iceland/Liechtenstein/Switzerland/San Marino/Vatican City, and non-EU residents of Italy.

## Ground truth from the official Call for Admission PDF (MEIS, a.y. 2025/2026)
URL: https://corsi.unitn.it/sites/cds/files/2025-01/call-for-admission-ms-meis-en-25-26.pdf
(saved full text in __1.md)

Art. 1: "Entry is restricted to 50 places; this figure includes 10 places reserved for non-EU nationals living abroad."

Art. 2 Categories: "Non-Europeans: Citizens of countries outside the European Union living outside Italy"

Art. 2 Admission requirements:
i. "a bachelor's or higher degree ... at least 60 credits from at least two of the following disciplinary areas: History and Philosophy, Economics, Law, Political Science, Sociology or Foreign Language."
iii. "good knowledge of the English language, at least B2 level ... IELTS no skill lower than 5.5, TOEFL internet-based ... no skill lower than 21, Cambridge B2 first: no skill lower than 165. Candidates who have gained their degree (Bachelor's or Master's) from a university where tuition is entirely in English are not required to submit an English Language certificate"
iv. "basic knowledge of a second foreign language, other than Italian, from among the following: Arabic, Chinese, French, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, German"

Art. 3.4 Required documents to upload:
a. copy of passport (personal info pages) or identity card
b. copy of bachelor's degree certificate or registration certificate attesting expected graduation date
c. transcript including a list of all courses, exams and marks (official translation if not in IT/EN), with weighted average and min–max range
d. transcript of marks for any post-graduate (Master's) degree
e. letter of reference (preferably written by a university lecturer)
f. curriculum vitae in English following the Europass model
g. English Language qualification
h. declaration attesting at least basic knowledge of a second foreign language
i. statement of purpose — brief personal statement in English (max. 4000 characters, spaces incl.)

Art. 3.5: "Candidates are required to pay an application fee of €30.00 ... Failing to pay the application fee ... will automatically lead to the exclusion of the applicant."

Art. 4: Selection by qualifications (and possibly interview). "Applicants might be invited to interview (in person or conference call), only if the selection Committee requires to collect further information." Score 0–100; ≥50 suitable. Tie-break: "preference will be given to the youngest."

Art. 5: Enrollment confirmation fee €100.00 (non-refundable). "Non-Europeans who apply and pay the Enrollment confirmation fee must pre-enroll at the Italian diplomatic representative in their own country ... Pre-enrolment is compulsory in order to obtain the Visa."

Art. 6 Tuition fees for non-Europeans: max €10,000/year. Reduced by application score:
- Tuition waiver: score 90–100
- €1,000: score 70–89.9
- €3,000: score 60–69.9
- €6,500: score 50–59.9

Art. 7 Scholarships for non-Europeans: "University of Trento will grant scholarships to top scored non Europeans applicants provided that they get a score of at least 90/100. The number of available scholarships and their allocation will be announced with the ranking list. Students who benefit from the UniTrento scholarship are waived from tuition fees." "The scholarship is intended as a partial contribution to mobility expenses and will be paid only after arrival in Italy."

Note 1 (Non-European category): "citizens of any country in the world that is not part of the European Union, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Switzerland, the Republic of San Marino, or Vatican City State, and who are not permanently residing in Italy" — no country list excludes Bangladesh.
