# Financial aid for high-achieving international students
Source: https://www.unive.it/pag/19545
Accessed: 2026-06-20

There are various forms of financial aid available to high-achieving students who have obtained their education outside the Italian education system and are interested in enrolling in a bachelor’s or master’s degree at Ca’ Foscari. This merit-based financial support is made available both by Ca’ Foscari itself and by other Italian governmental organizations.

## Merit-based scholarships for high-achieving students offered by Ca' Foscari
Given Ca' Foscari's commitment to welcoming international students and fostering the creation of an ever more global community, the university provides targeted financial aid for meritorious international students enrolling in a bachelor's or master's degree taught in either Italian or English.

**For high-achieving students enrolling in a degree programme in the 2026/2027 academic year, Ca' Foscari is offering renewable yearly scholarships** that are designed to support them over the entire duration of their degree:
  * For new **bachelor's students** : **8** Ca' Foscari Honours Scholarships of **10,000 euro per year for three years**
  * For new **master's students** : **20** Ca' Foscari Honours Scholarships of **10,000 euro per year for two years**

Applicants are assigned points based on the following elements of evaluation in order to be situated in the relevant set of rankings:
  * **Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)** : this must be equivalent to or higher than **85%** at the time the scholarship application is submitted.
  * **Motivational video** : this must be prepared according to the indications provided in the call for applications.
  * **Reference letter(s)** : these must be prepared by teachers or professors familiar with the applicant and his/her work. Applicants to bachelor's degrees are permitted to provide one reference letter for evaluation; master's degree applicants must provide at least one letter but are permitted to submit up to two.
  * **Language certification** : the applicant's knowledge of the language of instruction of his/her chosen programme of study can be demonstrated by providing one of the language certifications accepted by the university.

## Ca' Foscari's International College
The International College is an honours school that allows its students to pair a Ca’ Foscari bachelor’s or master’s degrees with an additional, parallel course of study involving interdisciplinary courses and activities. Each year, the College offers admission to 12 new bachelor’s degree and 8 new master’s degree students, each of whom receives financial assistance in the form of a voucher, or payment exemption.

## "Invest Your Talent in Italy" scholarships
The Invest Your Talent in Italy (IYT) scholarship programme is for new master’s degree students enrolling in an English-taught programme in the fields of Economics, Management, Engineering or High Technology. Historically, these renewable scholarships have offered 1,000 euro per month for 9 months to high-achieving students from a specific range of countries with which Italy is actively cultivating economic ties. Scholarship beneficiaries also receive a tuition fee waiver.

## MAECI scholarships
Each year the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation (MAECI) offers grants to foreign nationals and Italian citizens living abroad (IRE) wishing to enrol in a 2-year master’s degree at an Italian higher education institution. Historically, these renewable scholarships have offered 9,000 euro per year to students from a wide range of countries all over the world. Scholarship beneficiaries also receive a tuition fee waiver.
