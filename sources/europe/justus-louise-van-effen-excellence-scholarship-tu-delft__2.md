### Degree
- Graduated: An original or certified copy of your bachelor's degree.
- Not graduated yet: You may apply for a MSc programme if you are in the process of completing your studies. Provide a certified statement from your university confirming the type of degree you will obtain.
- If the degree/statement is in a language other than English or Dutch, a translation is also required (certified translation from your university, or a sworn translation).

### Transcript
- Graduated: An official final academic transcript(s) issued by the educational institution.
- Not graduated yet: The most recent official academic transcript. Your CGPA must meet the exact minimum requirements at the time of application.
- Your CGPA must be clearly mentioned on your official documents, or included as a certified statement. Make sure to include your university's grading system.

### English Language Certificate
The following English language certificates are accepted*:
- TOEFL iBT
- IELTS (academic version)
- Cambridge Assessment English
  - C1 Advanced (Certificate of Advanced English)
  - C2 Proficiency (Certificate of Proficiency in English)

The IELTS Indicator test and The IELTS Online (Academic) test cannot be accepted.
* Certificates that are older than two years on the day you have uploaded the document and completed your application are not accepted.

"For EU/EFTA nationals … You may upload the English language certificate until 1 July, provided that all other required application documents are uploaded before the application deadline of 1 April. Do note, additional time is not granted for scholarship applications. If you are applying for a scholarship, your English language certificate must be included when you submit your application."

(For Aerospace Engineering the listed scores are strict minimum requirements; for other programmes no official minimum scores are set, but applicants below the score levels may be rejected. The page lists accepted certificate types but does not publish a single university-wide IELTS/TOEFL band here — minimums are programme-specific.)

### Motivation letter
A clear and relevant essay in English (1,000 – 1,500 words) addressing motivation for the MSc programme, interest in TU Delft, and (if applicable) preferred specialisation.

### Curriculum vitae
An extensive CV (resume) written in English.

### Reference Letters (if applicable)
"Only required for applicants applying for a scholarship listed on our website. Two reference letters in either English or Dutch. These letters must be
  * from either a previous professor or assistant professor. If you have work experience, one of these letters may be from your employer.
  * preferably written on the official letterhead /have the official stamp of the university or company.
  * signed by the referee and include your complete name."

### Portfolio (if applicable) — required for Architecture / Building Technology / Landscape Architecture / Management in the Built Environment / Urbanism.

### GRE (if applicable) — required for certain MSc programmes (GRE General Test).

Final upload checklist includes: Degree, Transcript, Proof of English language proficiency, Passport/European ID card, Residence permit, Motivation letter, Curriculum vitae, Reference letters, Portfolio, GRE.
