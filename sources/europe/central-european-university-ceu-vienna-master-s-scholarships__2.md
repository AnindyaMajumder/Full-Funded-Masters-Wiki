# Scholarships Funded by CEU Supporters
Thanks to generous donations many students are supported by external donations from Alumni and other individuals.

## Available Scholarships
- Yehuda Elkana Doctoral Fellowship in Higher Education Policy (doctoral).
- Talent & Outstanding Merit Scholarships — awarded to a very limited number of top-ranked students who meet the eligibility criteria for the Analytics Talent Scholarship or Finance Talent Scholarship.

## Other Scholarships
In addition, we have the following donor funded scholarships to CEU students. At this point it is NOT possible to apply for these scholarships. In case of availability, students will be informed of the awards by Admissions or the Financial Aid Office generally as part of the acceptance offer.

- Dr. Elemer Hantos Scholarship: Awarded to students from Central Europe or Latin America.
- George Soros Leadership Fund Scholarship.
- Hiflylabs CEU Business Analytics Scholarship: Covers tuition expenses for one student attending CEU's 1 year Business Analytics MS program.
- Peter Hangartner Fellowship: Economics MA degree student with the highest GPA.
- Peter A. Nadosy Scholarship: awarded annually to a Hungarian student.
- Postgraduate Opportunity Scholarship: open to students pursuing a Master's at CEU and who are citizens of any of the 30 eligible countries, primarily located within Central & Eastern Europe and the former Soviet Union. Up to nine awards will be available annually.
  Eligible countries include: Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Estonia, Georgia, Hungary, Kazakhstan, Kosovo, Kyrgyzstan, Latvia, Lithuania, Macedonia, Moldova, Mongolia, Montenegro, Poland, Romania, Russian Federation, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Ukraine, and Uzbekistan.
  [NOTE: Bangladesh is NOT on this list — so the donor "Postgraduate Opportunity Scholarship" is NOT open to Bangladeshis. But the general CEU Master's Financial Aid (Tuition Award + Stipend Award) on the main financial-aid page IS open to "candidates from any country".]
