IdEx, A LABEL FOR EXCELLENCE
The IdEx 'Université Grenoble Alpes: University of innovation' project was awarded the 'Initiative d'Excellence' label by the French Government in 2016 as part of the National investment program for the future (PIA). This distinction is limited to 9 university sites in France.

### ENGLISH VERSION

I. Application

**Am I eligible to the IdEx scholarship?**
The IdEx Formation Master's Scholarship is intended for the following students:
  - currently enrolled in a university outside France, or who have obtained their last degree in a foreign university
  - who obtained their baccalaureate abroad

The selection is based on high academic potential. The scholarship may be allocated to students entering a first or second year of a Master's degree, or its equivalent in the engineering field (2A equivalent to M1, 3A equivalent to M2).

**How to apply?**
In order to apply to the IdEx scholarship, you shall take the following steps:
1. Apply to one or several Master's degrees
The first step is to apply for a Master's degree at one of the 4 institutions composing UGA:
  - École nationale supérieure d'architecture de Grenoble (ENSAG),
  - Institut polytechnique de Grenoble (Grenoble INP),
  - Sciences Po Grenoble – School of Political Studies (IEP)
  - Université Grenoble Alpes (UGA)

The list of Master's degrees taught in English is available at https://www.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/education/programs/;
The complete list of UGA degrees is available at https://formations.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/fr/index.html

2. Indicate your wish to apply to the scholarship
When applying for a Master's degree, you have the possibility to indicate your wish to apply to the IdEx Formation Master's scholarship.
This must be done in accordance with the calendar for each curriculum and:
  - either online, when applying to the Master;
  - if not possible, by writing to the School Registrar and/or the Head of the curriculum.

Your application to the IdEx scholarship is reviewed by the Master's academic team and sent to the IdEx team if selected.

3. The decision
  - If you are selected for the scholarship, you will receive a formal notification from the IdEx Formation staff, and be informed of the multi-step procedure to follow to confirm your approval of it;
  - If you don't receive any answer, or if you wish to have more information about the progress of your application, we invite you to contact the Master's team directly (either the academic team or the school office). It is possible you are selected to the Master's but not to the scholarship.

**How much can I receive from the scholarship?**
The amount of the IdEx scholarship depends on your study year. The maximum amount is set per academic year, i.e. 10 months.
  - for the first year of a Master's degree / 2A year, the maximum amount is 9 000 € netto and tax-free income
  - for the second year of a Master's degree / 3A year, the maximum amount is 5 500 € netto and tax-free income
Caution!
If you receive another scholarship, you are requested to report to the IdEx Formation team. The full amount of your scholarships cannot exceed the amount allocated by the IdEx Formation. The amount of your other scholarship will be deducted from the amount awarded by the IdEx Formation.

**Calendar**
Since applications for the IdEx scholarship are linked to applications for a degree, you must refer to the application calendar of the degree(s) you are applying to.
Applications for the IdEx scholarship as such are open from March to July and will be dealt with on the basis of the best files plus in the chronological order the applications have been received.

II. Awarding

**Does obtaining my scholarship exempt me from paying tuition fees?**
The waiving of tuition fees rests with the Masters.
Obtaining an IdEx scholarship does not exempt you from paying tuition fees. For more information, please contact the Registrar (=scolarité) of the Master's degree.

**When will I receive the scholarship?**
The payment of the IdEx scholarship is made in two separate payments of 50%.
For arrivals in September, the schedule is as follows:
  - a first payment mid-October at the earliest after confirmation by the Master's supervisors of your registration and attendance at the Master's lessons (meaning : after you have paid due tuition fees);
  - a second payment in February after confirmation by the Master's supervisors of your attendance at the exams and classes.
In the case of an arrival on semester 2, payment is made after arrival, upon confirmation of attendance at the Master's lessons.
These payments are conditional on the opening of a French bank account or a SEPA-area bank account in your own name. You will have to save your Bank Identity Statement ('RIB' or 'IBAN') on the IdEx platform (you will receive the link together with the award notification of the scholarship).

**Is the scholarship valid for the two years of my academic program?**
No. The IdEx Formation Master's scholarship is only granted for one academic year. If you are awarded the IdEx scholarship for the first year of your Master's degree, and would like to continue to benefit from the scholarship during the second year of your Master's degree, you must apply again.

III. Help finding accommodation
As an IdEx scholarship holder, you have the possibility to be supported by a student accommodation service, subject to conditions. No accommodation is guaranteed.
