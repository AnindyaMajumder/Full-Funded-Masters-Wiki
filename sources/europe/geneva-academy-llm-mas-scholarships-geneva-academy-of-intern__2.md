# How to Apply
Instructions on the steps required to apply for our LLM-MAS Programmes.
Applications are currently open for the 2026-27 cohorts of our in-person LLM-MAS programmes:
**16th January 2026** – applications close for those requesting scholarship. Note that non-scholarship applications received before this date will be treated with priority in the review process
**16th March 2026** – applications close for those applying without scholarship.
Application Process
Before applying to our [LLM – MAS programmes](https://geneva-academy.ch/masters-of-advanced-studies/), please **download and carefully read the application guidelines** :
[LLM-MAS in International Crimes, Justice and Human Rights – Application Guidelines](https://www.unige.ch/formcont/download_file/1927/0)
[LLM – Master of Advanced Studies International Humanitarian Law and Human Rights – Application Guidelines](https://www.unige.ch/formcont/application/files/5417/6364/3525/LLM-MADH-Application-Procedure.pdf)
These documents provide explanations on the required documents, deadlines, and important information you will need to provide on the admission portal.
Please note that the guidelines include **interactive documents** related to your career plan, awards, grants, and prizes. These documents must be downloaded, completed, and uploaded to the admission portal. If these documents are not provided, you **will not be able to submit your application**.
We therefore strongly advise that, as a first step, you **download, read, and refer to the guidelines throughout the application process**
Required level of English
A **sound command of English (level C1)**. You must be able to show, via a [recognized test](https://geneva-academy.ch/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Language-Requirements-2025.pdf), that your English is of a high enough standard to successfully engage with and complete your course at the Geneva Academy. This requirement does not apply if (1) your mother tongue is English; (2) you have taken an English-taught bachelor’s or master’s degree; (3) you have at least two years’ professional or academic experience in an English-speaking environment
Tuition Fee
The tuition fee for the LLM-MAS programmes is 18,000 Swiss Francs.
If you are applying with scholarship:
Scholarships
We offer a limited number of full scholarships for the programme. 
These scholarships are allocated through a highly competitive process based on criteria established by scholarships donors, and which notably include academic merit, extracurricular achievements, country of study, country of residence and the candidate’s financial needs.
These full scholarships cover tuition fees and living expenses in Geneva for 10 months. They are only available for citizens of non-western countries (full list of eligible countries available [here](https://www.oecd.org/dac/financing-sustainable-development/development-finance-standards/daclist.htm)). 
**If you are applying with scholarship, you will have to download and fill in the related document on scholarship from the guidelines.**
Admission Fee
In order for their applications to be reviewed, all applicants must submit within the week following the submission of their application a non-refundable admission fee of CHF 100 via [this link](https://payment.flywire.com/pay?recipient=BGM&payment_purpose=MTJ%20MAS%202026-2027:P0324). On the Flywire platform, you will have to indicate your first and family name as well as a student ID. 
**For the student ID, please indicate the name of the programme you are applying for: LLM – MAS in IHL and HR or LLM – MAS in Inter crimes, justice and HR**. This is very important in order to verify that payment has been received.
