## Description
Universidad Carlos III de Madrid intends, through this call, to provide graduate students with the necessary resources so that they can carry out their studies in research-oriented Master's degree programs, providing them with funding linked to the fulfillment of specific objectives.
For all these reasons, UC3M announces, for the academic year 2026/2027, scholarships for studies in the University Master's Degree programs listed in Annex I of this Resolution.
## Number of scholarships available
**9 full scholarships**
  * 7 scholarships for the Master's Degree in Business and Finance
  * 2 scholarships for the Master's Degree in Social Sciences

## Amount of aid
The aid will consist of a scholarship that **will cover the amount of the prices corresponding to the ECTS of the first enrollment of the complete course** , and an additional financial aid for study for a total amount of **€10,000** that will be paid proportionally throughout the first and second semester of the 2026-27 academic year.
## Length
One academic year (2026/27). In those Master's Degree programs whose duration is longer than one academic year (1 additional semester for 90 ECTS and 2 for 120 ECTS), the scholarships may be renewed for the academic year 2027-28
## Requirements
Those persons who have **[applied for admission](http://www.uc3m.es/postgraduate/admission)** for the 2026-27 academic year to one of the university master's degrees indicated in ANNEX I may apply for the study scholarships regulated in this call for applications, as long as there is no resolution denying such admission. 
**Annex I: List of programs:**
  * Master in Business and Finance (MRes)
  * Master in Social Sciences

## Necessary paperwork
The required documentation attached to the application is as follows: 
  * a) Curriculum vitae in Spanish or English.
  * b) When applicable, proof of the degree of disability equal to or greater than 33%.

Only documentation submitted in Spanish or English will be accepted.
Non-submission of the application through the aforementioned **[university application](https://aplicaciones.uc3m.es/formulario/ayudas_completas_uc3m_postgrado)** within the required deadlines will result in the applicant's rejection from this call for applications.
## Selection criteria
For the evaluation and subsequent preparation of the corresponding award proposal, the Committee will take into account the following criteria and scale:
  1. Score obtained in the admission process to the corresponding Master's program: up to 9 points
  2. Curriculum Vitae of the applicant: up to 1 point
  3. Proven disability of 33% or more: 1 additional point

## Award decision
The resolution granting the aid, with the definitive list of the selected candidates and, if applicable, the prioritized list of candidates in reserve, will be published in the **BOEL** and on this website.
  * **Final decision. AEM_UC3M-26/27 (8/6/2026)**

## Application and forms
A single regular application period has been established: **until 30 April 2026**
The application and the required documentation must be submitted through the **university's computer application** enabled for this purpose, selecting the reference call: **AEM_UC3M-26/27**.
