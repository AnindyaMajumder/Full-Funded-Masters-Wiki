#  Research Master Scholarship
**At Tilburg University, we highly value our Research Master's programs. These programs serve as an important initial stride toward a rewarding research career, potentially leading to a PhD position within Tilburg University or elsewhere. Beyond academia, graduates of our Research Master's programs often find themselves excelling in research-intensive organizations. To stimulate students to pursue our Research Master's programs, Tilburg University provides an attractive waiver.**
Outstanding students admitted to any of Tilburg University's Research Master's programs are eligible for the Research Masters Scholarship. This partial tuition fee waiver covers the entire two-year duration of the Research Master.
## Scholarship value
Admitted students to a Research Master's program awarded with this scholarship will be charged the statutory fee of €2,695(*) for the academic year 2026-2027 instead of the higher non-EEA institutional fee of €19,900.
_(*) Please note that this is the preliminary value, still subject to confirmation or changes._
## Tilburg School of Social and Behavioral Sciences (TSB)
Research Master in Social and Behavioral Sciences 
### **Who is eligible?**
Non-EEA students who pay the institutional fee for the [Research Master in Social and Behavioral Sciences](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/education/masters-programs/social-and-behavioral-sciences), including those who obtained their Bachelor’s diploma in the Netherlands.
### **Number of waivers available**
Each program offers up to three tuition fee waivers for first-year students.
### **Duration**
The tuition fee waiver covers the full two-year duration of the program _._ Waivers for the second year are available to students who received one in the first year, provided they have met the study progress criteria. Extensions may be granted in exceptional cases.
### **How to apply**
By applying in Studielink, you also automatically register for the waiver selection process of [the Research Master Scholarship.](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/education/masters-programs/tuition-fees-scholarships/research-master-scholarship "Research Master Scholarship")
### **Selection process**
  * A maximum of three waivers are awarded per program.
  * Only students who are conditionally or definitively admitted to the program by the Admissions Board are eligible for the waiver.
  * Candidates will be assessed based on several criteria, including: Prior education, Research experience, and Study progress.


If more than three students meet the eligibility criteria, a draw will determine the final recipients.
**Note:** If one or more applicants decline the tuition fee waiver or choose not to enroll, a ranking recap will be conducted. The waiver will then be offered to the next eligible candidates on the list.
**Timeline**
  * **Application deadline:** April 1st
  * **Selection and notification:** By May/June
  * **Confirmation:** Students awarded the waiver must confirm acceptance within two weeks. If one or more applicants decline the tuition fee waiver or decide not to enroll, a ranking recap will take place, and the waiver will be offered to the next eligible candidate.
  * **Ranking recap:** By June

## Tilburg School of Humanities and Digital Sciences (TSHD)
Research Master in Communication, Cognition and Digital Culture 
**Who is eligible?**
For the [Research Master in Communication, Cognition and Digital Culture](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/education/masters-programs/research-master-linguistics-and-communication-sciences), a limited number of scholarships are available for exceptional students in the following categories:
  * Outstanding non-EEA students pursuing their first master's degree in the Netherlands.
  * Exceptional students with a previous master's degree from the Netherlands, including Dutch, EEA, and non-EEA citizens.


**Please note:** Only students with a start date in September are eligible for this waiver. Students who start the program in February are not eligible.
**Number of waivers available**
A maximum of three tuition fee waivers are available per cohort.
**Duration**
The tuition fee waiver covers the full two-year duration of the program _._ Please note that if students experience study delay after the standard two-year period, they can no longer claim the waiver for the remaining time.
**How to apply**
To be considered for the tuition fee waiver, students must:
  * Meet the admission requirements, which include prior education and English language proficiency.
  * Submit a complete application for the program at Tilburg University before **April 1st, 23:59 CET**.
  * Ensure their scholarship application is complete before the program’s application deadline.
  * Take into account that it may take at least six weeks to receive a (conditional) Admission Letter.


For more information on how to apply and an overview of the admission requirements, please refer to the program's [application and admission website](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/education/masters-programs/research-master-linguistics-and-communication-sciences/application)
**Selection process**
Eligible candidates will be selected based on the following criteria:
  * Meeting the general eligibility requirements for the Research Master's program.
  * Academic quality and potential in the proposed field of study.
  * Fit with Tilburg University's Research Master’s program.
  * Academic and personal motivation, as expressed in the motivation letter.


**Selection steps:**
  1. **Interview Invitation:** Qualifying candidates will be invited for an online interview in the first half of May.
  2. **Interviews:** Interviews will take place in the first half of June.
  3. **Final Decision:** All applicants will be notified of the results via email in the third week of June.
  4. **Acceptance:** Students awarded the waiver must sign and return the award letter by July 1st.


If one or more applicants decline the tuition fee waiver or decide not to enroll, a ranking recap will take place, and the waiver will be offered to the next eligible candidate.
