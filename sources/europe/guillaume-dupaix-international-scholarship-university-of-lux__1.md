#  The Guillaume Dupaix International Excellence Scholarship
##  At a glance
  * Target audience:**Prospective students**
  * Study level:**Master**
  * Deadline:**24 March 2026**
  * Faculty:**All faculties**
  * Funding type:**Academic excellence**
  * Student type:**Domestic, International EU, International outside of EU**


  * Talent support stipend aimed at attracting high-achieving international students who wish to pursue a Master’s program at the University of Luxembourg. **No registration/tuition fees wavier possible**.
  * Full-time successful applicants aiming to pursue a Master’s degree at the University of Luxembourg, regardless of the field of study. _**Not eligible** : Master’s programs requiring a mandatory semester or year abroad and applicants/recipients of the Luxembourg’s State Financial Aid for Higher Education (formerly CEDIES)._
    * [Moving to Luxembourg to study?](https://www.uni.lu/life-en/moving-to-luxembourg/to-study/)Information about residence permit, health insurance, etc.
  * **30 students per annum** (10 per faculty) **with the following distribution:**
| Regular students **regardless of their country of origin**  |  **15** students  |
| --- | --- |
| Students from **target countries**  |  **15** students from the Western Balkans, Ibero-American countries, Thailand, Vietnam, Belarus, Egypt, India, Moldova, Russia, the United Kingdom, Turkey, Ukraine  |
  * Up to 2 years = standard duration of a Master’s degree
  * **5.763€** per semester=**11.526€** per annum, covering living expenses + accommodation, **not** tuition fees.
    * [Accommodation Uni.lu](https://www.uni.lu/life-en/accommodation/)
  * The scholarship application is part of the application to the University of Luxembourg Master’s programmes and cannot be made via email. Apply here: [**_Bachelor & Master – University of Luxembourg_**](https://www.uni.lu/en/admissions/bachelor-master/)
**Step 1: Master’s application** by **_24 March 2026_** Submission of Master’s applications via the online admissions portal and assessment of academic excellence by the Directors of each Master’s program.
**Step 2: Scholarship application** by _**24 March 2026**_ Email invitations to apply for the Guillaume Dupaix scholarship are sent to the students whose applications are deemed excellent by the Master Study Programmes.
**Step 3: Missing documents** by 31 **_March 2026_** Last call for submitting any missing documents for the scholarship applications.
  * **Step 4: Outcome** by **_15 May 2026_** Successful and unsuccessful applicants are informed via email.
**Step 5: Acceptance** within **_3 working days_** Successful applicants are invited to confirm the acceptance of the scholarship offer.
**Step 6: Agreement** by** _fall 2026_** Scholarship recipients are invited to sign the scholarship agreement.
