# KU Leuven Inspiring the Outstanding Scholarship — Main page
Source: https://www.kuleuven.be/scholarships/year/2026-2027/inspiring-the-outstanding-scholarship
Accessed: 2026-06-18

## Scholarship summary
KU Leuven is committed to making higher education accessible to students from all backgrounds, regardless of their financial circumstances. Therefore, the University has established a fund for donations from the global KU Leuven community to award scholarships to outstanding students who wish to pursue one of our programmes.
The scholarship is based on the University's baseline 'inspiring the outstanding', which reflects KU Leuven's mission and ambition and underlines its commitment to fostering excellence and innovation.

## Sponsor
This scholarship is financed by the KU Leuven International Scholarship Fund (https://giving.kuleuven.be/funds/international-scholarship-fund).

## Type, coverage and duration
| Academic year | 2026-2027 |
| Scholarship type | Full fee waiver; Allowance |
| Tuition fee waiver | The Inspiring the Outstanding Scholarship holders receive a full tuition fee waiver and pay only an annual administration fee of circa €140. |
| Scholarship coverage | Incoming students will receive a scholarship of € 13,200 per academic year, for a maximum of two academic years. |
| Duration | The duration of the scholarship is one academic year for a 60 ECTS master's programme and two academic years for a 120 ECTS master's programme. |
| Number of available scholarships | 1 scholarship |

## Eligibility
If you are eligible for an Inspiring the Outstanding Scholarship, you are also eligible for the following master's scholarships: Master Mind Scholarship; KU Leuven ASEAN Scholarship; KU Leuven Scholarship for Africa; KU Leuven Scholarship for Central and South America. Through one application process, you will automatically be considered for all of these scholarships.

| Study level | Master's programmes |
| Eligible programme(s) | View eligible master's programmes here. Students enrolled in the following programmes for the 2026-2027 academic year will not be eligible for the Master Mind Scholarships: preparatory programmes, bridging programmes and distance learning programmes. |
| Eligible nationalities | All nationalities are eligible. |
| Academic requirements |
  * The student has a high level of academic performance and potential. Excellent study results are reflected in a high Grade Point Average (GPA), as shown on the transcript of records.
  * Previous degree(s) should have been obtained at a higher education institution outside Flanders. International students who have completed a credit mobility programme at a host Flemish higher education institution are still eligible for an Inspiring the Outstanding Scholarship.
|
| Language requirements | Regardless of the minimum score required by the programme, applicants whose first language is not English must demonstrate a good knowledge and command of English. These are the accepted English language tests:
  * Online TOEFL: minimum overall score of 94
  * IELTS: minimum overall score of 7
  * Cambridge CPE: minimum level C2
The language test must be taken before the application deadline of 15 January. Language test results can be added after the deadline. Students who have previously completed an English-taught programme in Australia, English-speaking Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, the United Kingdom or the United States of America do not need to submit a certificate of English language proficiency. |
| Disqualifying conditions |
  * Current enrolment: Students who are already enrolled at a Flemish higher education institution are not eligible. The only exception to this is for international students who, when they apply for a scholarship in the spring of 2026, are enrolled in a specific preparatory programme to start their master's programme in September 2026.
  * Other scholarships: Students may not combine the Inspiring the Outstanding Scholarship with any other scholarship.
|

## How to apply
One application procedure gives you access to several master's scholarships offered via KU Leuven. When you apply for an Inspiring the Outstanding Scholarship, you automatically apply for the following scholarships: Master Mind Scholarship; KU Leuven ASEAN Scholarship; KU Leuven Scholarship for Africa; KU Leuven Scholarship for Central and South America. It is not possible to apply for these scholarships separately.

| Applications open | 15 October 2025 |
| Application deadline | 15 January 2026, 23.59 CET |
| Application procedure |
  * Step 1: Submit a complete application file for an eligible master's programme before 15 January 2026, 23.59 CET, including the KU Leuven Scholarships & Master Mind Candidacy Form as an annexe. This annexe should be uploaded in the application tool under document type "Annexe". Download the Candidacy Form here.
  * Step 2: Register your scholarship candidacy via the online registration tool before 15 January 2026, 23.59 CET.
  * After nomination: If you have been nominated as a candidate, you will be informed by 10 March 2026. You will then be asked to submit a one-minute motivational video for the next selection phase by 18 March 2026.
|

## Selection
| Procedure |
  * Nomination: Following the application deadline of 15 January, KU Leuven's master's programmes will nominate eligible students who have expressed interest in the scholarship.
  * All nominated students will be informed about their nomination by 10 March 2026.
  * All nominated students will be asked to submit a one-minute motivational video for the KU Leuven selection.
  * Selection: The central KU Leuven Selection Committee will make the final selection. The results will be announced at the end of March or at the beginning of April.
|
| Selection criteria | The following elements will be taken into account when applications for the master's programme are assessed: academic performance, academic awards, motivation, relevant experience, demonstrated leadership abilities, sense of commitment, top applicant profile. |
