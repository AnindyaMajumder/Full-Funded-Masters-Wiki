## Nova SBE Merit Scholarship — verbatim quotes

(1) Tuition coverage:
"This scholarship can cover 20 % or 50 % of the tuition fee value of the Master's program."

(2) Eligibility:
"All candidates can apply for this scholarship, regardless of their country of origin."

(3) Application timing:
"Applications are open for the Fall Intake and cannot be considered after the application
to the Master's program has been submitted"

(4) How to express interest / documents:
"Candidates must show interest in a merit scholarship during their application to the
Master's program by filling in the designated area in the application form for this matter."

(5) Cap when combined with other Nova SBE scholarships:
"When a student is granted different scholarships within Nova SBE, the funding cannot
exceed 70 % of the total amount of the tuition fee for the Master's program, except in
cases where the funding comes entirely from a 100 % social mobility scholarship."

(6) Rolling basis / early-apply warning:
"Scholarships are awarded on a rolling basis to applicants, which means that the budget
available for scholarships may be used before the end of the Master's admission cycle.
Due to the high volume of deserving candidates and the limited funding available for
scholarships, we strongly recommend that you apply as early as possible."
