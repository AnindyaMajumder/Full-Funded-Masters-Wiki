# Application for Master's degrees and scholarships
Source: https://www.ens-lyon.fr/en/studies/admissions/application-masters-degrees-and-scholarships
Accessed: 2026-06-18

ENS de Lyon and its partners offer scholarships (Ampère, GRADUATE +) for excellent international students to enroll in Master's programs.

## Conditions
**To apply for the first year of a Master's program (M1),** applicants should justify holding a L3 degree (Bachelor - 180 ECTS) or, for applicants who have studied abroad, an equivalent level.
**To apply for the second year of a Master's program (M2)**, applicants should justify holding a M1 degree (240 ECTS) or, for applicants who have studied abroad, an equivalent level.
Applications are approved by the admission committee.

### Ampère Merit Scholarships
  * Master's programs eligible: Master’s programs in Exact Sciences, the Arts, and Human and Social Sciences (except FEADéP Master's programs and a few Master's programs). Consult the Ampère scholarship rules (document on the right)
  * Amount: 1,000€ per month for 12 months

**Specific partnerships with some countries** - US students enrolled in a US University applying for a Master's program at the ENS de Lyon can also apply for the Lafayette Fellowship from the French Embassy in the USA. - Canadian students enrolled in a Canadian university applying for a Master’s program at ENS de Lyon can also apply for an Embassy of France in Canada scholarship - Indian students enrolled in an Indian university applying for a Master’s program at ENS de Lyon can also apply for an Embassy of France in India scholarship. Applications possible for all ENS de Lyon Masters opened to international students

### GRADUATE + Scholarship
Graduate+ scholarships are awarded to the best international students selected for a Master’s degree in the following Graduate initiatives and Master’s programmes, either in M1 or M2. (CORE, DynLifeSys, MathInfi, PACE)
  * Amount: 1,000€ per month for 10 months
  * Scholarships funded within the framework of the French government’s SFRI programme.
  * Ce travail a bénéficié d’une aide de l’État gérée par l’Agence Nationale de la Recherche au titre de France 2030 portant la référence « ANR-21-SFRI-0001 ».

## Application process
**Call for application 2026-2027 is now closed.**

**NON-EUROPEAN students are students:** - who do not have the nationality of one of the countries of the European Economic Area (the 27 countries of the European Union, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein), or of Switzerland, Monaco and Andorra. and - who have obtained a higher education degree in a foreign country.
**EUROPEAN students are students:** who have the nationality of one of the countries of the European Economic Area (the 27 countries of the European Union, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein).

### 1. Complete and submit the online application form.
These forms allow you to apply for a Master's program and for a scholarship of excellence.
**If you apply to ENS de Lyon**
  * As a European student - Apply here – Online Form - CLOSED
  * As a non European student with a higher education degree in a foreign country - Apply here – Online Form - CLOSED
  * As a non European student with a French higher education degree - Apply here – Online Form - CLOSED

Applications submitted after the deadline will not be considered. Applications submitted via email will not be considered. Incomplete applications will not be considered.

### 2. Application review
All applications are reviewed by the admission committee of ENS de Lyon. No results will be given by phone.

### 3. Admissions and enrolment
If your application is accepted in a program from ENS de Lyon, you will receive a letter of pre-enrolment.

## Documents requested
See below the documents that the candidates have to upload in the application form - **in French or English only**:
  * **All candidates:**
    * Official identity document
    * Motivation Letter
    * Curriculum Vitae
    * Certificate of diplomas and degrees already obtained (ex: Bachelor degree) - documents are accepted only in French or English. If the document is in another language, you have to provide a translated document in French or English, officially certified
    * University academic transcript of records - documents are accepted only in French or English. If the document is in another language, you have to provide a translated document in French or English, officially certified
    * For the current year: proof of enrollment - documents are accepted only in French or English
  * **Other documents**
    * Additional documents are requested depending on the chosen program. Those documents could be about research projects, language certificates, letters of recommendation and any other document useful for the academic advisors. If you do not have a document, an explanation is expected in pdf format.

PLEASE NOTE : if not available now, the required diploma for Master's admission will have to be transmitted before the beginning of the academic year (compulsory)
