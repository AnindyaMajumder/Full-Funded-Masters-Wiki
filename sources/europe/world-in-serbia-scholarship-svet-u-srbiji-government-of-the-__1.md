# Стипендије Свет у Србији / World in Serbia Scholarship

### Важна обавештења (Important notices)
- Продужен рок за пријаву за кандидата за стипендије „Свет у Србији” — 10.06.2026. (Extended application deadline notice)

## Project overview

Пројекат „Свет у Србији” представља иницијативу Владе Републике Србије од националног значаја и односи се на доделу стипендија Владе Републике Србије кандидатима из 127 држава Африке, Азије, Централне и Јужне Америке, које су чланица и државе посматрачи Покрета несврстаних.

(The "World in Serbia" project is an initiative of the Government of the Republic of Serbia of national importance and refers to the awarding of scholarships by the Government of the Republic of Serbia to candidates from 127 countries of Africa, Asia, Central and South America, which are member and observer states of the Non-Aligned Movement.)

Стипендија која се додељује у оквиру Пројекта омогућује остваривање права на бесплатно школовање у Републици Србији у складу са одабиром кандидата, смештај и исхрану у систему студентског стандарда Републике Србије, месечни новчани износ стипендије, као и здравствено осигурање.

Као предуслов за школовање на српском језику, студенти стипендисти по доласку у Републику Србију, а пре уписа жељених студија на високошколским установама, похађају бесплатне курсеве интензивне наставе српског језика.

Министарство просвете сваке године расписује конкурс за доделу стипендија „Свет у Србији".

## What does the project scholarship include?

The scholarship within the project **includes:**
- Free preparatory Serbian language instruction (intensive Serbian language course upon arrival in the Republic of Serbia). This does not apply to scholarship recipients who have already studied in Serbia and attended Serbian language classes;
- Free tuition within one level of studies, including undergraduate academic, integrated academic, and vocational studies, specialist vocational studies, master academic and vocational studies, specialist academic or doctoral studies;
- A monthly scholarship in the amount of RSD 18,000.00;
- Free accommodation and meals at student standard institutions founded by Republic of Serbia or the Autonomous Province of Vojvodina;
- Voluntary healthcare insurance for scholarship recipients studying the Serbian language. Scholarship recipients are strongly advised to secure a health insurance policy independently;
- Mandatory healthcare insurance for scholarship recipients under 26 years of age, and voluntary healthcare insurance for scholarship recipients over 26 years of age. Scholarship recipients over the age of 26 are strongly advised to secure a health insurance policy independently;
- Free recognition of secondary school diplomas;
- Free recognition of higher education diplomas.

The scholarship within the Project **does not include:**
- Travel expenses for arrival and departure to and from the Republic of Serbia;
- Public city transportation costs within the Republic of Serbia;
- Costs for textbooks and study materials during the studies;
- Cost of exam registration fees and other administrative charges levied by higher education institutions founded by the Republic of Serbia (e.g. issuing fees for graduation certificates, diplomas and similar), as well as charges established by the student standard institutions (e.g. the cost of meal fob cards) in line with the law;
- Scholarship holders pay administrative fees for procedures under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Internal Affairs in connection with temporary residence approval and the issue of a biometric residence card – tariff no. 1: application/requests RSD 420, tariff no. 37 issue of temporary residence permit, unique permit RSD 2,120;
- A fee of RSD 1,788 is payable for the issue of the biometric identity card.

## Conditions for receiving a scholarship

A candidate may be eligible for a scholarship if they meet the following conditions:
- They are a citizen of a member state or observer state of the Non-Aligned Movement (NAM).
- They have been proposed by the competent authority of the participating state in the Project.
- They are younger than:
  - 21 years old for enrollment in first-level studies – undergraduate academic studies, integrated academic studies, undergraduate vocational studies;
  - 25 years old for enrollment in first-level specialist vocational studies and second-level studies – master's academic studies, master's vocational studies, specialist academic studies;
  - 35 years old for enrollment in third-level studies – doctoral academic studies.
- They possess the appropriate educational qualifications:
  - For enrolment in master's academic studies:
    - With 60 ECTS credits – they must have an appropriate diploma from a higher education institution obtained from undergraduate academic studies with at least 240 ECTS credits, or an appropriate diploma from a higher education institution of four years' duration;
    - With 120 ECTS credits – they must have an appropriate diploma from a higher education institution obtained from undergraduate academic studies with at least 180 ECTS credits;
- Knowledge of English at least at A1 level, or knowledge of Serbian at least at A1 level upon arrival.

## Applying procedure and requested documentation

The above steps must be followed, otherwise the application will not be taken into consideration.

Step 1. The candidate applies via the Google Form: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSevQItl0KUaeWDHfofPfa539kcV_T2Gb0fuoUiCOC1KkPbt5A/viewform

Step 2. The candidate shall submit scanned original documentation as well as translations into Serbian or English certified by an authorized person to the email address worldinserbia@prosveta.gov.rs (include name, surname and country of application in the subject line):
- Curriculum Vitae with a motivational letter;
- Copy of the travel document (passport) – not translated; it is necessary that the travel document be valid for at least another 6 years at the date of submission for a visa;
- Proof of acquired education – transcripts of educational qualifications certified by an authorized person (secondary school certificates, higher education diplomas – for enrolment in master's studies, and transcripts for both study levels and diploma supplements);
- Certificate of the average grade from the previous level of studies for enrolment in doctoral studies;
- Proof of proficiency in English or Serbian at a conversational level (certificate, attestation, confirmation, etc.);
- Health documentation, comprising (including medical form):
  1) Proof of immunisation (vaccination): tuberculosis, hepatitis B, poliomyelitis, diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis (whooping cough), measles, mumps, rubella, Streptococcus pneumoniae, and Haemophilus influenzae.
  2) A medical certificate addressing: general state of health; chronic conditions; current treatment regimen; and a psychological / psychiatric assessment.
  3) Results of laboratory testing for the following communicable diseases: HIV; Hepatitis B; Hepatitis C; Tuberculosis (QUANTIFERON-TB test or chest X-ray); and Malaria.

All health documentation must bear the signature and official stamp of a physician from a recognised health institution in the candidate's country of origin. All diagnoses must be recorded in Latin and coded per the International Classification of Diseases (ICD).

Note: Upon arrival in the Republic of Serbia, the Student Health Care Institute of Belgrade will verify the candidate's health documentation and, where necessary, conduct a general medical examination. The Ministry of Education reserves the right to decline a candidate's application where the health certification cannot be verified or where the candidate's health state is incompatible with residency in shared accommodation.

Step 3. The candidate submits documents to the competent institution of his/her country that announced the application for scholarships within the "World in Serbia" project.

NOTE:
- Documentation of a candidate who has not applied via the link and has not submitted complete documentation translated into Serbian or English to the email worldinserbia@prosveta.gov.rs will not be taken into consideration;
- After passing the entrance exam and being admitted to a specific study program, the scholarship holder does not have the right to change the study program.

## Application Deadline

Рок за пријаву кандидата путем линка је **од 10. јуна 26. јуна 2026. године**, а достављање Листе са предложеним кандидатима од стране надлежног дипломатско-конзуларног представништва Републике Србије Министарству спољних послова Републике Србије је најкасније до **3. јула 2026. године.**

Deadline for candidate application via the link **is no later than June 26th 2026**, and the submission of the List of proposed candidates by the competent diplomatic and consular mission of the Republic of Serbia to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Serbia is no later than **July 3rd, 2026.**

Selected scholarship holders are required to bring upon arrival in the Republic of Serbia:
- original documents (secondary school certificates, higher education diplomas, master's diplomas, transcripts of grades and diploma supplements), translated into Serbian/English;
- certificate of average grade at the last level of studies;
- original health certificate with the required test results, in Serbian/English, not older than six months.
