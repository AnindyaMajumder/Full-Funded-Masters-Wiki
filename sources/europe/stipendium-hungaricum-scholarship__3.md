# Stipendium Hungaricum — News: Provisions covered for the 2024/2025 academic year (corroborating benefits)
Source: https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/news/provisions-covered-by-the-stipendium-hungaricum-scholarship-for-the-2024-2025-academic-year/
Crawled: 2026-06-06 (crawl4ai markdown-fit)
Published: November 3rd, 2023

The programme provides many opportunities for international students such as tuition-free education, monthly stipend, accommodation contribution, and even medical insurance.

Tuition-free education
... as a scholarship holder you can focus on your academic advancement, as all your tuition fees are covered during your university studies.

Monthly stipend
If you apply for non-degree, bachelor’s, master’s, one-tier master’s levels or postgraduate specialist training courses, you can get a monthly amount of HUF 43,700 (cca EUR 110) contribution to the living expenses in Hungary, for 12 months a year, until the completion of your studies.
If you apply for doctoral level, the monthly amount of scholarship is HUF 140,000 (cca EUR 365) for the first phase of your education (for 4 semesters) and HUF 180,000 (cca EUR 470) for the second phase (4 semesters) – for 12 months a year, until the completion of your studies.

Accommodation contribution
In all study levels, the scholarship provides free dormitory place or a contribution of HUF 40,000 / month to the accommodation costs for the whole duration of the scholarship period.

Medical insurance
Finally, the scholarship includes health care services according to the relevant Hungarian legislation (Act LXXX of 1997, national health insurance card) and supplementary medical insurance for up to HUF 65,000 (cca EUR 170) a year/person.

Bear in mind that these provisions are only a contribution to the living expenses of the Scholarship Holders, which also means that they do not fully cover all the costs of living. If you become a scholarship holder, you need to add your own financial resources to cover all living expenses in Hungary.
