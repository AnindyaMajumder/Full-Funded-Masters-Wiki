================================================================

ELIGIBILITY
- "International students who have completed their first degree abroad can apply
  prior to starting a Master's degree program in Germany" and international PhD
  students with completed graduate degrees.
- "Priority is given to applicants from DAC countries who have not yet taken up
  residence in Germany at the time of their application."

APPLICATION DEADLINES (two annual cycles)
- Fall 2026: July 15 – September 1, 2026
- Spring 2027: January 15 – March 1, 2027

NON-FINANCIAL BENEFITS
- Event program and support
- Idealistic / mentoring support for scholars
- Alumni network and program access
- Study start and academic support resources

FUNDING SOURCE
- "Funded by the Federal Ministry of Education and Research (BMBF) and the
  Federal Foreign Office (AA)."

CONTACT
- Scholarship Department: studienwerk@boell.de | Phone: +49 (0) 30 285 34-400

================================================================
SOURCE: https://www.boell.de/en/application  (Scholarship application)
================================================================

APPLICATION DEADLINES
- "Applications are accepted twice a year. The application deadlines are March 1
  and September 1."

ELIGIBILITY
- The foundation grants scholarships to graduates and doctoral students from
  outside Germany. "An application for a scholarship for an undergraduate degree
  is not possible."

GERMAN LANGUAGE REQUIREMENT
- "Good or very good knowledge of the German language is required (at least B2
  Level or DSH1)."

APPLICATION PROCESS
1. "Gather information on the application process and the requested documents"
2. Register for the online application portal
3. "Provide the requested information in the Online Application Portal"
4. "After uploading all of your documents, submit your application before the end
   of the application deadline"
- "We accept only online applications"
- "The application portal will be opened about 6 weeks before the application deadline."

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS (student scholarships)
- Info Sheet A1-1b (checklist)
- Application Form A1-2
- Form Expert report A1-3b
- Form Reference on Social Commitment A1-4b
(For doctoral scholarships: A2-1b checklist, A2-2 application form, A2-3b expert
report, A1-4b reference on social commitment.)
- All forms should be completed using Adobe Acrobat Reader.

MASTER'S STUDENTS (Non-EU / international)
- Base scholarship: "a base scholarship in the amount of 992 € / month"
- Health insurance: "a health insurance allowance of up to 100 €"
- Tuition reimbursement: "up to 10,000 € / year"
- Language courses: Tuition fees for German language courses covered
- Fixed allowance: "fixed fringe benefits in the amount of 38 € / month"
- Family support: "family allowance and child allowances (per child)"
- Study abroad: "no additional funding" provided if degree components completed internationally

PHD STUDENTS (Non-EU / international)
- Base scholarship: "a base scholarship in the amount of 1,500 € / month"
- Health insurance: "up to 200 € / month"
- Tuition reimbursement: "up to 10,000 € / year"
- Language courses: Tuition fees covered
- Fixed allowance: "fixed fringe benefits in the amount of 38 € / month"
- Family support: "family allowance and child allowances (per child)"

INCOME RESTRICTIONS
- "Any additional income must be offset against the scholarship payment" based on
  marginal employment earnings limits.
