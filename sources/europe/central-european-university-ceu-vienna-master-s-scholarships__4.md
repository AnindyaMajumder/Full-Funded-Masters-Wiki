KEY IMPLICATION: Bangladesh IS a visa-sensitive country. Per the master's admissions page,
"candidates from visa-sensitive countries ... can apply in the first round only." Financial aid
is available in Rounds 1 and 2, but a Bangladeshi can only apply in Round 1, so the effective
financial-aid deadline for a Bangladeshi is the Round 1 deadline: October 15, 2025 (cycle 2026/27).
