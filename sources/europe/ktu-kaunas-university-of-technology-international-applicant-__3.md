# Welcome to the admissions platform for international students
Kaunas University of Technology offers a diverse portfolio of study programmes developed with a strong focus on innovation and the evolving demands of global industries. Admission is available at three academic levels: Bachelor’s, Master’s, and Doctoral studies.

# Tuition fee waivers and scholarships
We offer partial and full tuition fee waivers to high-achieving students based on academic merit. During their studies, our students can also apply for scholarships awarded for excellent academic results, achievements in extracurricular activities and more. We also provide various forms of financial aid that help support their studies and living expenses.
