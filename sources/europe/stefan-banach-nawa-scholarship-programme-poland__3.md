I.        SCOPE OF RECRUITMENT

     Pursuant to Article 19 section 1 of the Act on the Polish National Agency for Academic Exchange of 7
     July 2017, the Director of the Polish National Agency for Academic Exchange announces the call for
     applications for participation in the Prof. Stefan Banach Programme (hereinafter referred to as the
     “Programme”) – scholarships for second-cycle studies or preparatory course from the winter
     semester 2026/27.

II.       PROGRAMME DESCRIPTION

2.1. Programme Objective
     The programme aims at supporting the socio-economic development of developing countries by
     raising the level of education and professional qualifications of citizens of countries eligible to
     participate in the programme by offering scholarships for undertaking second-cycle studies in Poland.
     The offer is addressed to citizens of the following countries: Albania, Angola, Argentina, Armenia,
     Azerbaijan, Belarus, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Brazil, Georgia, India, Indonesia, Iraq, Iran, Jordan,
     Kazakhstan, Kenya, Kosovo, Lebanon, Mexico, Moldova, Mongolia, Montenegro, Nigeria, North
     Macedonia, Palestine, Papua New Guinea, Peru, Philippines, Rwanda, Senegal, Serbia, Tanzania,
     Tunisia, Ukraine, Uzbekistan, Vietnam.
     The programme is a joint initiative of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Polish National Agency for
     Academic Exchange, implemented as part of the Polish development cooperation programme.

2.3. Thematic scope of activities carried out under the Programme
    The programme enables receiving a scholarship for full-time second-cycle studies in Polish or English,
    conducted at universities supervised by the Minister of Science and Higher Education that have
    concluded a framework agreement with NAWA.
    In addition, in public universities, the Programme enables free education. Studies in Polish may be
    preceded by a one-year NAWA preparatory course carried out on scholarship terms.
    At the stage of applying for financial resources in the Programme, the Applicant selects one of the
    scholarship paths:
          1)     Polish language path (for studies in Polish):
                 a)     preparatory course and second cycle studies or
                 b)     second-cycle studies in Polish (without a preparatory course).
                 The NAWA preparatory course preceding studies in Poland lasts one academic year and
                 its aim is to deepen knowledge of the Polish language and knowledge of subjects related
                 to the planned field of study. Applicants qualified to participate in the programme
                 complete courses at a centre chosen by NAWA. Assignment to centres implementing
                 the preparatory course is determined in particular by the field of planned studies and
                 the availability of places in the centres. Applicants who have chosen the “preparatory
                 course and studies” scholarship path will participate in the admissions process for the
                 2027/28 academic year while attending the course, on dates specified by the selected
                 university.
          2)     English path (for studies in English) - second-cycle studies in English and a course in
                 Polish language and culture.

                                   Rules for substantive assessment

The result of the substantive assessment is determined as the total of the products of the individual
criteria. The maximum number of points is 110.

Points for the arithmetic average of grades from first-cycle studies on the first-cycle diploma or on the
certificate of average grade will be awarded after calculating the average based on the following
formula:

            number of points = average grade obtained x 70 / maximum average grade on a given
            scale

The number of points will be rounded to two decimal places.

In the case of a grade point average that cannot be converted according to the above formula (e.g.
reverse grade point average, alphabetical grade point average, etc.), a separate method of converting
the grade point average into points will be established.

The condition for obtaining a positive substantive assessment by the Applicant is to obtain at least 70
points, including:

          a.    minimum 50 points in criterion 1;

          b.    minimum 20 points in criterion 2.

Applicants whose applications do not meet the above conditions will receive a negative substantive
assessment.

If the number of points awarded under criterion 1 is lower than the specified minimum, the
application will not be evaluated under criterion 2 and will automatically receive a negative
substantive evaluation.

2.8. Project budget
The scholarship of the Director of the Polish National Agency for Academic Exchange includes:
1) monthly scholarship 3 during the regular education period,
2) exemption from fees for education at NAWA preparatory courses or at public universities 4
3) lump sum for international travel costs 5 related to coming to Poland to undertake education.

The monthly scholarship amounts to PLN 2,500 per month and is paid for a maximum of 12 months in
the academic year (maximum of 5 months during the winter semester and 7 months during the summer
semester), starting from the first month of classes at studies and during the NAWA preparatory course.
For scholarship holders participating in an on-site Polish language and culture course prior to the start
of their studies, the first scholarship is payable from September 2026.
Financial conditions of education at private universities supervised by the Minister of Science and
Higher Education are determined by the universities. In such cases, the issue of payment for studies
should be agreed individually between the Scholarship Holder and the private university hosting them.
The matter of exemption from education fees remains within the competence of the private university
accepting them.

The lump sum for the cost of international travel related to coming to Poland for the purpose of
undertaking studies (“second-cycle studies” path) or a NAWA preparatory course preceding studies
(“preparatory course and second-cycle studies” path) is paid once together with the first monthly
scholarship of PLN 2,500.

The NAWA Director's scholarship is paid through the unit conducting the preparatory course or the
university where the Scholarship Holder is studying.
