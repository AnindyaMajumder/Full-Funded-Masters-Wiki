# SI Scholarships on master's level — FAQ
Source: https://si.se/en/apply/faq-scholarships-on-masters-level/
Crawled: 2026-06-18 (crawl4ai, grep-filtered relevant Q&A)

* **The result of my English test was sent to University Admissions too late. What should I do?**
You do not need to inform us about it. Instead you should contact University Admissions or the Swedish universities you are applying to. If you cannot be admitted to any eligible master’s programme by 26 March 2026 due to this late submission, your scholarship application will not be considered. We cannot make any exceptions.

* **What requirements do you have for English proficiency and GPA?**
SI does not have any requirements for language proficiency and GPA for applicants on master’s level, as these assessments are done by the universities and University Admissions.
Find more information regarding language proficiency and potential GPA requirements here (universityadmissions.se) or on the specific university’s website.

* **I have applied for other scholarships. Can I still apply for SI scholarships?**
Yes. The competition for SI scholarships is high. We recommend that you also look for and apply for other scholarships.

* **Why do applicants have to be liable to pay tuition fees?**
To be eligible for the scholarship, you must be liable to pay tuition fees to Swedish universities. This does not mean that you need to pay tuition fees by your own means if you are awarded one of our scholarships. It only means you need to be required to pay when you apply. In general, citizens of countries that are not part of the European Union (EU), European Economic Area (EEA) or Switzerland are required to pay tuition fees. Our scholarships cover both tuition fees and living expenses, which means EU citizens (for example) are not the target group of our scholarships.
**The tuition fee will be paid by SI directly to the university in case you are awarded one of our scholarships.**

* **Can my referee fill out the form in my native language and then translate it?**
We accept translations as long as they are performed by a notarised translator with the notary office’s official stamp. It is not acceptable for individual applicants to translate the documents on their own. The original document and the translation should be uploaded as one PDF.
