# Call for Ordinary Scholarship

In accordance with the provisions set by current regulations, the competition for the awarding of Scholarships to students enrolled at the University of Pavia is announced annually. The application for admission to the competition must be submitted **ONLY ONLINE** in the section "STUDENT'S ONLINE SERVICES" (https://edisupv-sol.dirittoallostudio.it/apps/V3.1/sol/public/index.php).

With **ONE** online APPLICATION, students apply to EDiSU for ALL BENEFITS THEY ARE INTERESTED IN for the current academic year (multiple applications cannot be submitted by the same student). It is understood that the application for benefits must be submitted by the earliest deadline specified in the notice for which the student is applying. (For example, if a student is applying for both a scholarship and a college spot, he will submit ONE online application to apply for BOTH benefits and the deadline will be that of the college application).
