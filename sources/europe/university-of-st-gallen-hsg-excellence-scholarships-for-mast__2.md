# HSG Best Talents Programme — University of St.Gallen (crawled 2026-06-18)

Source URL: https://www.unisg.ch/en/studying/why-hsg/hsg-best-talents/

## Key verbatim sections

The University of St.Gallen (HSG) offers a scholarship programme for outstanding and engaged bachelor's graduates (EU/EFTA nationals or holders of the Swiss C permit) who wish to pursue a master's at HSG. Those who are accepted into the programme benefit from financial support as well as a personal development programme alongside their studies.

This programme is being launched in Autumn Semester 2026 with 39 talents and, with support from our donors, is set to expand to 45 talents per cohort (each autumn).

### Who are the HSG Best Talents?
In general, all students from EU/EFTA countries as well as holders of a Swiss C-permit who are admitted to a Master's programme at HSG (criteria) can apply. To have a good chance of securing one of these prestigous slots, you need excellent grades and a demonstrated engagement beyond your studies. ...

### How does one become a HSG Best Talent?
Once your admission to HSG and a specific Master programme has been confirmed, please select on this page the option "Apply now!" and submit the additional application documents. ... Awards are made on an ongoing basis.

Applications for the 2026 cohort were accepted until June 12, 2026. Selection decisions and refusals will be communicated by July 12, 2026 at the latest. The application portal will reopen in November 2026 for the 2027 cohort application cycle.

### What are the advantages of HSG Best Talents?
By offering the Best Talents programme, we support talented and committed students from Switzerland and Europe (EU/EFTA nationals or holders of a Swiss C-permit), who have completed a Bachelor's degree...
- Financial support: Coverage of tuition fees and contributions to living expenses
- Personal development: Accompanying support programme as well as access to the HSG community...

## CONCLUSION
The master's-level "Excellence Scholarship" at HSG is the Best Talents Programme, restricted to EU/EFTA nationals or Swiss C-permit holders. Bangladesh nationals are NOT eligible.
