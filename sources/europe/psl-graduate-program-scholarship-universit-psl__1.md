#  Scholarships and grants for graduate students

Whether you are a French or international student and whatever your study program is (master's, PhD tracks etc.), financial support is available for you.
Here you'll find useful information about three types of financial aids designed for different kinds of study projects and students from low-income families:
  * Scholarships
  * Mobility grants
  * Social criteria grants

SCHOLARSHIPS
MOBILITY GRANTS
SOCIAL CRITERIA GRANTS

Scholarships for graduate students vary depending on one's school, degree programs, and specific enrollment status. Listed below are five types of scholarships provided or co-funded by Université PSL and its member schools and fellowships offered by the French government. They are open for the academic year 2024-2025. Please refer to the link of each scholarship for updated details and contact information.

### 1) Université PSL Graduate Programs Excellence Grants
Several PSL Graduate Programs award scholarships to **M1 or/and M2 students** based on their excellent academic performance, motivation and (in some cases) financial situation.
**Some scholarships are open for specific tracks and levels of study.** Please refer to the graduate program's web page or contact the head of the program for detailed information

Excellence Grants in Chemistry
**Eligibility:** All nationalities
**Applicable PSL degree programs:** Master's of Chemistry
**Funded by:** PSL Graduate Program in Chemistry (https://psl.eu/en/graduate-programs/graduate-program-chemistry)

Excellence Grants in Engineering (ISAI)
**Eligibility:** International students
**Applicable PSL degree programs:** Master's programs in: Energy; Materials Science and Engineering; BioMedical Engineering Paris; Nuclear Energy
**Funded by:** PSL Graduate Program in Engineering (ISAI)

Excellence Grants in Computer Science
**Eligibility:** All nationalities
**Applicable PSL degree programs:** Master's in Computer Science
**Funded by:** PSL Graduate Program in Computer Science (https://psl.eu/en/graduate-programs/graduate-program-computer-science)
See detailed information about the excellence grants in Computer Science: https://pginfo.ens.psl.eu/last/scholarship_grants_en.html

Excellence Grants in Mathematics
**Eligibility:** All nationalities
**Applicable PSL degree programs:** Master's in Mathematics and Applications
**Funded by:** PSL Graduate Program in Mathematics and Application

Excellence Grants in Finance
**Eligibility:** All nationalities
**Applicable PSL degree programs:** Master's in Finance (Research in Finance track)
**Funded by:** PSL Graduate Program in Finance
See detailed information: https://dauphine.psl.eu/en/training/scholarships-and-social-assistance#c78306

Excellence Grants in Earth and Biodiversity Sciences
**Eligibility:** All nationalities
**Applicable PSL degree programs:** Master's in Earth and Planetary Science, Environment
**Funded by:** PSL Graduate Program in Earth and Biodiversity Sciences

Excellence Grants in Life Sciences
**Eligibility:** All nationalities
**Applicable PSL degree programs:** Master's in Life Sciences (only students enrolled in IMaGHE- Integrative PhysioPathology curriculum who carry out an internship in a PSL laboratory can apply for the excellence grant)

Excellence Grants in Religious Studies

**Applicable PSL degree programs:** Master's programs in: Civilizations, Culture and Society; Asian Studies; Philosophy; Religious Sciences and Societies

Excellence Scholarship in Physics

### 2) Université PSL PhD Tracks grants
Université PSL offers 5-year fully funded programs from Master's to Doctorate level covering seven research fields.
PhD Tracks
**Eligibility:** All nationalities
**Fields of study:** Chemistry; Life Sciences; Engineering; Computer sciences; Astrophysics; Earth sciences & biodiversity; Physics; Cognitive sciences
**Application Process:** Awarded at Admission
