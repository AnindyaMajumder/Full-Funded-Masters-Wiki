# Financial Aid

Funding Your Studies at CEU
Studying at CEU comes with both educational and living costs.
Please refer to the following guideline: The Essential Financial Guide (https://www.ceu.edu/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-03/brochurefinancialaid2026-03v04rzdoublepages.pdf)

## Financial Aid by Degree
Financial aid is granted on the basis of academic merit and is available for most of our programs (only full-time programs). Please make sure to check your future program's website before you start your application.

Candidates from any country can apply for CEU financial aid. Applicants who want to be considered for these awards must apply by the appropriate deadline.

To be considered, indicate your interest for financial aid in the CEU Application Form ('Funding' section).

We offer different types of financial support for each degree level.

### Financial Aid for Master’s Programs
Financial Aid for Master’s student is awarded only by academic merit. It can include:
  * Master’s Tuition Award (may cover 50% - up to 80% of the tuition fee) — Full Tuition Award is possible only in exceptional cases.
  * Master’s Stipend Award (300 EUR/month up to 750 EUR/month)

### Financial Aid for Bachelor’s Programs
A small number of Partial Tuition Awards is available for outstanding applicants to our undergraduate programs.
This is a merit-based award, which may cover up to 50% of the tuition fee (up to €4,000 per academic year).

### Financial Aid for Doctoral Programs
CEU Doctoral Scholarship: Candidates who apply by the financial aid deadline are automatically considered for the CEU Doctoral Scholarship, covering the full cost of tuition, complete with a monthly stipend.
The Doctoral Stipend is 2,002 EUR/gross/month, awarded for a period of 48 months in total.

## Financial Aid FAQ (selected)
- Where and when can I receive my first stipend? You must complete the Onboarding and Online Matriculation Form so you become officially enrolled at CEU and you need to physically arrive at the Vienna Campus.
- When is the stipend paid? The monthly stipend is transferred to the student’s bank account by the 4th day of each month.
- Do I need to open an Austrian bank account to receive my stipends? Your bank account needs to be from a SEPA country or a SEPA-eligible online bank account (Wise, Revolut, N26) and it has to be a EUR currency account.
- If I withdraw from the program in the middle of the academic year, do I have to pay back the scholarship? No.
