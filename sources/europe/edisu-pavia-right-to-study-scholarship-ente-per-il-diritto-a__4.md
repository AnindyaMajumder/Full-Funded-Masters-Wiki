The scholarships to be awarded for the 2025/2026 academic year are financed by the
European Union - Next Generation EU (PNRR), the Ministry of University and
Research (MUR) and the Lombardy Region and other public or private bodies.


Prior enrollment at the Pavia University is NOT a mandatory requirement for the
application although enrolment procedures must be completed within the deadlines
indicated in art. 4 (except for students enrolled in the “filter semester”).




                   CONFIRMATION of the online application must be made by no later
                          than Monday, September 15, 2025, at 12 pm
            GENERAL COMPETITION REQUIREMENTS

Be duly enrolled at the University of Pavia for the 2025/2026 academic year to the date of 14 of November 2025:

✓ Enrolment, for the first time, in the first year of a bachelor’s degree, master’s degree or a single cycle master’s degree programme (Students
   enrolled in the 1st year of a master’s degree, if they have not already obtained a bachelor’s degree, can be registered as “enrolled under
   condition” but they have to complete enrolment procedures in compliance with the terms and conditions prescribed).

✓ Enrolment in the “filter semester” of Medicine and Surgery course or Dentistry and Dental Prosthodontics course having chosen the University
   of Pavia as first location to continue the course of study.

✓ Enrolment in an extra year (i.e., one year beyond the course’s prescribed length) of a bachelor’s degree, master’s degree or a single cycle
   master’s degree programme (1° year «fuori corso»);

✓ Enrolment in a specialization course (with the exception of those pertaining to the field of medicine as defined by Legislative Decree 368/99);
✓ Enrolment in a PhD programme (if not already a recipient of a scholarship)
The competition is also open to students with disabilities – students with recognized disabilities (as defined by the law n. 104/1993, or whose level
of disability is 66% or higher) who are enrolled for a further 2 years beyond the regular duration of their course of any bachelor’s degree, master’s
degree or a single cycle master’s degree programme.



Meet the merit-based requirements, including steadiness in academic progress, and the income-based requirements listed later.
                            WHO’S NOT CONSIDERED ELIGIBLE?
   Students who hold a degree of the same level as, or superior to, the one for which the scholarship is being requested for the
   2025/2026 academic year;

Example: In 2023/2024 I achieved a Bachelor Degree in Philosohpy, in 2025/2026 i dedice to enrol in another Bachelor Degree
course, for example Economics -> in this case i cannot be eligible for the college placement since i already hold a degree of the same
level.

   The recipient, in the same academic year, of a scholarship awarded by another public or private institution;

   Those who enrol at the University of Pavia after having, in previous years, withdrawn (at the University of Pavia or another
   Italian/foreign university) from a course that requested the same admission qualifications as the course in which they intend to
   enrol in 2025/2026 a.y.

Example: is excluded the student who in the 2025/2026 a.y. enrol in the 1° year of a bachelor’s degree or single-cycle master’s
degree course but, in previous years, has withdrawn from a bachelor’s degree or master’s degree course at any university.

   Those who are enrolled and repeating an intermediate year of their course;

Example: is excluded the student enrolled in the 2° year, as repeating student, of the Medicine and Surgery course.

   Those who are transferring to a different degree course or from another university and have to repeat the same year of the
   course in which they were previously enrolled (at Pavia University or another University);


                                                  BUT THERE’S AN EXCEPTION
Is eligible the student who has repeated a year of course, even after renewing the enrolment following formal withdrawal from a course, but has anyway
completed the number of CFU required to access the ranking list according to attachment A (CFU recognized for the course of study in which the student
intends to enrol for the a.y. 2025-2026).
The calculation of the minimum number of CFU, required for eligibility, is calculated starting from the year of absolute enrolment.
This waiver DOES NOT APPLY to students enrolled in the 1st year of any degree course or to those enrolled in any year of a PhD programme or specialisation
course.
EX: in 2023/2024 the student attends the 1° year of a three-year bachelor’s course in Biological Sciences. In 2024/2025, after withdrawing from the
Biological Sciences course, he enrolls in the 1° year of the Geological Sciences course. If this student requests, in 2025/2026, the scholarship as a student
intending to enroll in the 2° year of Geological Sciences bachelor’s degree, he will be eligible for the assignment of the benefit only if he has completed the
number of CFU required for students who are enrolling in the 3° year of Geological Sciences, by August 10, 2025.




            W H AT ’ S T H E Y E A R O F A B S O L U T E E N RO L M E N T ?
                     The phrase “absolute enrolment” refers to any previous enrolment in any Bachelor or Single Cycle Master Degree Course course (even
                     one different from the one attended in the current academic year) at any Italian or Foreign University, that has required the same
                     qualifications as those required for enrolment in the course attended in the current academic year in Pavia, regardless of the results of
                     previous study (withdrawal from the course, university transfer, graduation, etc.) and the number of years that have elapsed since.

                     Example: in 2024/2025 the student attends the first year of a bachelor’s course at Milan University. In 2025/2026, after withdrawing
                     from Milan, the student enrolls in the regular first year of a bachelor’s course at Pavia University. If this student requests, in
                     2025/2026, EDiSU benefits he needs to indicate as ABSOLUTE ENROLLMENT the a.y. 2024/2025.

                     Example: in 2023/2024 the student starts the first year of a bachelor’s course a Foreign University. In 2025/2026, after withdrawing
                     from the foreign university, the student enrolls in the regular first year of Medicine course at Pavia University. If this student requests, in
                     2025/2026, EDiSU benefits he needs to indicate as ABSOLUTE ENROLLMENT the a.y. 2023/2024.

       STUDENTS WITH INCOME AND ASSETS OVERSEAS
    For those countries where it is particularly difficult to have documents certified by a local Italian Embassy, as an alternative an original
    certification of income and assets owned can be submitted, in the Italian language, by the diplomatic or consular representation in Italy of the
    foreign country. The certification issued by the diplomatic or consular representation in Italy must be LEGALISED BY A PREFECTURE and must
    expressly refer to the documents originally issued in the country of origin.

    For foreign students coming from particularly POOR COUNTRIES the evaluation of their financial status is based on a statement issued by an
    Italian consular agent in the country of origin, or by Diplomatic Authorities of the country of origin in Italy, certifying that the student does not
    belong to a family with known high income and social status.

    Students holding the status of stateless person or political refugee or holders of subsidiary or special protection are treated as Italian citizens
    and, for the purpose of evaluating their financial condition, are exempt from submitting statements issued by Embassies and Consulates,
    because the only elements that will be considered are any income and assets possessed in Italy, which must be certified in the same manner
    as for Italian students (ISEE UNI statement). The stateless person/political refugee/holder of subsidiary or special protection status must be
    substantiated by evidence provided by applicants through official documents, in date and valid. These documents must be attached
    (uploaded) in electronic document format to the online application and later handed in at the EDiSU help desk.


             NB. For the submission of foreign documents it is only possible to access the EDiSU counters by APPOINTMENT
                                              (by opening a new “ticket” in your personal area of EDiSU)


In the event that the original foreign documentation, submitted by September 15, 2025, prove to be incomplete/incorrect, the student shall be excluded
from the provisional ranking. Applicants may, however and within 10 days from the publication of the provisional ranking list, apply to revise their
application and to present additional original certifications, in addition to the previous documentation, to replace incomplete/missing documents.

                                                                TRANSFERALS
     If the student transfers to another university, after the start of the a.y., the scholarship application submitted to EDiSU Pavia will be sent, upon the
     written request of the student, by EDiSU to the University to which the student has requested to be transferred. In this case the student will lose any
     benefit/funding which he might have been awarded by EDiSU (and in time will have to return any amounts that have been disbursed).

     If the student transfers from another university to the University of Pavia, after the start of the a.y., he will have to request the transmission of his
     application from the previous institution supporting the right to university study to EDiSU Pavia, provided that it is submitted before the deadline given
     in the Call for Applications. Regularization of the student transfer process must be completed by November 14, 2025.
