### Students from outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland
In accordance with Norwegian legislation, tuition fees for full-time students from outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland are **30% higher** than those for students from within the EU/EEA and Switzerland.
This does not apply to exchange students from partner institutions. Some groups may apply for an exemption.
International citizens residing in Norway may qualify for domestic tuition fees. [Read more](https://www.bi.no/en/programmes-and-individual-courses/admissions/tuition-fees/tuition-fees-outside-eu/) to determine eligibility and how to apply.

Master programmes last two academic years. Tuition fees listed are for one academic year and include lectures and standard examination fees. There is also a fee per semester for membership in the student organisation at your place of study.
**Fees do not cover:**
  * re-sit examinations
  * course reading
  * potential course excursions


Prices are adjusted annually in accordance with price inflation.
BI Norwegian Business School reserves the right to adjust tuition fees.
Internal applicants are applicants with a minimum of 120 credits (ECTS) from BI Norwegian Business School.
