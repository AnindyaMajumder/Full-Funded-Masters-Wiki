#  Erhard Busek Grant 
© Pexels
**This information refers to the 2025/26 application round. At present, no further call for applications for the Busek Scholarship is planned.**
The Erhard Busek Grant aims to enable highly qualified students from non-European developing countries to complete a full two-year Master’s programme in Austria. The excellence scholarship supports outstanding students, particularly in STEM or English-taught Master’s programmes.
**Target group:** Graduates of a Bachelor’s degree who wish to pursue a two-year Master’s programme in Austria and whose degree was obtained at a higher education institution in a non-European developing country; scholarships are awarded only to students who can provide proof of an unrestricted admission to the relevant Master’s programme.
**Countries of origin:** Afghanistan; Algeria; Bangladesh; Cabo Verde; Egypt; Cote d'Ivoire; Gambia; Ghana; India; Indonesia; Kenya; Kyrgyzstan; Libya; Mauritania; Morocco; Nepal; Nigeria; Pakistan; Philippines; Rwanda; Senegal; Somalia; Sri Lanka; Sudan; South Sudan; Chad; Tunisia; Uganda; Uzbekistan; Vietnam
**Type of funding:** Funding for a Master’s programme in Austria | Support for the entire study programme
**Duration:** 24 months
**Funding:** 1,300 euro monthly scholarship rate + one-time travel allowance of up to 1,200 euro
**Age limit:** 35 years
Please note that the selection of potential scholarship recipients is carried out exclusively by the respective higher education institutions. Direct applications to the OeAD are not possible.
##  Application and selection process 
Higher education institutions apply to the OeAD for the designated scholarship places for an Erhard Busek Scholarship as part of a formal expression of interest. They confirm that the academic and organisational support of the students at the higher education institution is guaranteed. A selection committee from the BMFWF and the OeAD decides on the allocation of scholarship places (Phase I).
**Submission deadline for declaration of interest (for higher education institutions): 15.12.2024**
On the basis of the allocated quota, the higher education institutions then select the best-qualified candidates from among those students who have been admitted to a relevant Master's programme with unrestricted admission and nominate them for the Erhard Busek Grant. The students then apply to the OeAD to receive this scholarship. After a final review of the formal requirements of the applications received, the scholarships are awarded. (Phase II)
**Tuition fees are not covered by the scholarship.** Erhard Busek Grant recipients are are exempted from these fees by the admitting higher education institution.
**Submission deadline for applications (for nominated students): from February 2025**
