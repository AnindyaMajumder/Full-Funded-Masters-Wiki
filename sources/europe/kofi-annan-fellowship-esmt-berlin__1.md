# ESMT Berlin — Master's Fees & Scholarships (crawled 2026-06-18)

Source: https://esmt.berlin/master-programs/scholarships-financing

NOTE (verifier): This official ESMT master's scholarships page lists ~13 scholarships in
full detail. There is NO "Kofi Annan Fellowship" among them. The closest match to the
LDC/LLDC "emerging leader from a developing country" description is the partial-tuition
"Developing Country Scholarships LDC/LLDC" (a DIFFERENT, tuition-discount-only program),
quoted verbatim below.

---

## Cost of tuition for master's programs: 36,000 Euros

The ESMT Master's Program tuition fee covers all courses and seminars, teaching materials,
orientation, and career support. Your tuition will be paid in three installments over two
academic years.

In addition to tuition, you should budget enough money for relocation to Germany's capital
Berlin and living expenses. We recommend that you plan a budget of around €1,200 a month
for living expenses.

## Developing Country Scholarships LDC/LLDC

**Number of scholarships available**: four
**Value**: up to 50% of tuition fee
**Requirements**: Master candidates should complete the scholarship essay as part of their
ESMT online application explaining how they qualify for this scholarship.
**Eligibility**: Candidates with permanent residence in any United Nations designated LDC
or LLDC.
**Status**: Open
