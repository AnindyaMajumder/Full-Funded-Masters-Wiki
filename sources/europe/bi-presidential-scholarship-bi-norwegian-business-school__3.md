# How to apply
Are you getting ready to apply but not sure where or how to start? Here is a step-by-step guide on completing an application with information about requirements, scholarships and more. You will also find frequently asked questions on this page.
## Create an application
The application process starts on the page of the programme or course you wish to apply to. When clicking "Apply now", you will be asked to log in, fill in some information about yourself and upload the necessary documentation. Under "My applications" you can keep up-to-date on the status of your applications at all times. 
For our full-time programmes the application portal opens 1 November.
**Have you applied before or started an application?** Have you started an application and registered your email address or are you already a BI student? You can log in to check the status or complete the application. 
### Step-by-step guide
  1. **Select your programme and check the** [admission requirements](https://www.bi.no/en/programmes-and-individual-courses/admissions/admission-requirements/). Find your [bachelor programme](https://www.bi.no/en/programmes-and-individual-courses/bachelor-programmes/) or [master programme](https://www.bi.no/en/programmes-and-individual-courses/master-programmes/).
  2. **Check tuition fees and scholarships** BI Norwegian Business School is a self-owned, not-for-profit foundation whose sole focus is education and research. Any profit will be used to strengthen BI's research and learning environment in accordance with the objectives of the school. [Read more on tuition fees](https://www.bi.no/en/programmes-and-individual-courses/admissions/tuition-fees/) [Read more on scholarships](https://www.bi.no/en/programmes-and-individual-courses/scholarships/)
  3. **Collect necessary documentation**[Bachelor programme documents][Master programme documents]
  4. **Create and submit your application**


##  What happens next 
Application deadlines 
BI Norwegian Business School is a private institution and the admissions process is not handled by Samordna opptak (Norwegian Universities and Colleges Admissions Service). You can therefore apply for admission to our full-time programmes after the priority deadlines outlined below. International applicants with citizenship from outside the EU/EEA must apply by 15 June.
### Priority deadlines
  * **1 March** for international Bachelor of Business Administration, Bachelor of Data Science for Business and Bachelor of Digital Business applicants
  * **15 April** for Norwegian Bachelor of Business Administration, Bachelor of Data Science for Business and Bachelor of Digital Business applicants
  * **1 March** for Master of Science applicants (application deadline for scholarships is 1 March) 


BI offers rolling admission thereafter, based on availability. The application web for BI's Master of Science programmes closes 1 August.
If you apply for admission to one of our part-time programmes, there will be continuous admissions until the respective course or programme starts. 
Scholarships 
BI offers various scholarship programmes to full-time students as part of our commitment to academic excellence. [Read more about our scholarships, your eligibility, our deadlines, and how to apply](https://www.bi.no/en/programmes-and-individual-courses/scholarships/).
