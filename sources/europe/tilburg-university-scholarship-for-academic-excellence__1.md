## Tuition fees
When you wish to study at Tilburg University, you must pay a tuition fee.
  * **Tuition fees cover** the cost of**lectures** and**facilities provided by the university** , such as the extensive university library, Wi-Fi on campus, and symposia and other meetings.
  * Tuition fees at Tilburg University are always for **one full academic year**.


[More information on the tuition fees and rates ](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/students/administration/tuition-fees "Tuition fees and payment")
## Estimated monthly costs of living
Besides the tuition fees, you will have living costs and other expenses. You will find life in Tilburg relatively inexpensive compared to life in other Dutch university cities, such as Amsterdam and Utrecht. The approximate cost of living is **between € 1,000 and € 1,200 per month**.

## Scholarships and Waivers for Incoming International Master's Students 2026-2027
Tilburg University is committed to supporting talented and motivated students in financing their studies. 
On this page, you will find an overview of tuition-fee waivers and scholarships offered by Tilburg University, as well as selected external opportunities, for both prospective and [current Master's students](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/students/administration "Administration: Registration, Tuition Fees & Money Matters"). 
Please note that most scholarships are merit-based and limited in number. Scholarships are not available for [Pre-Master's programs](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/students/administration/tuition-fees).

    * Outstanding non-EEA students pursuing their first master's degree in the Netherlands.

This partial tuition fee waiver covers the entire two-year duration of the Research Master

Liberia 
**University of Liberia Scholarship** Scholarship (tuition waiver + budget) for a maximum of one student from the University of Liberia accepted for a one-year Master's program at Tilburg School of Law
**Scholarship coverage**
  * Tuition waiver: 50 % reduction of the institutional tuition fee
  * Living expenses: €15,000 contribution


**Selection and Availability**
Only **one scholarship** is awarded each academic year. The most competitive eligible candidate, who meets the admission criteria will be granted a scholarship
**Eligibility criteria**
  * You are a student or faculty member from the University of Liberia.
  * You have completed a relevant Bachelor’s degree.
  * You will start your studies in September (Fall intake).
  * You are directly admitted (conditionally or unconditionally) to a 1-year Master’s program at Tilburg Law School.
    * Pre-Master’s students are not eligible.
    * You meet all admission requirements of your chosen Master’s program, including English language requirements.
    * English proficiency must be proven according to the requirements on the admission website.
    * GMAT/GRE is not required for this scholarship.


_Tip: Always check the admission & application section of your chosen Master’s program for specific entry requirements and deadlines._
**Application timeline and how to apply**
  * Apply for admission to your chosen Master’s program by **April 1**.
    * Follow the steps listed on the Application and admission page of the program of your choice.
  * Submit the [University of Liberia Scholarship application form](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/form/liberian-scholarship) and a motivation letter via webform by **April 1**.
    * Both the form and the motivation letter should be submitted via this link.
  * Applicants will be informed of the outcome by **mid-June**


**Motivation letter requirements**
Please upload a letter in which you:
  1. Introduce yourself and explain why you apply for this scholarship.
  2. Answer the following:
     * What will you contribute to Tilburg University (socially, academically, culturally)?
     * Tilburg University’s motto is _“Understanding Society.”_ How do you relate to this?
     * What are your career aspirations, and how will this Master’s help you achieve them?
