# Scholarships for fee-paying students
## Scholarships for master's studies, administered by Chalmers
**Application for fall 2026 is closed.**
There is a separate application process for Chalmers-administered scholarships. You can only apply once you have submitted your programme application at [universityadmissions.se](https://www.universityadmissions.se/intl/start "University Admissions"), as you will need to provide that application number.
You can apply for a scholarship before completing your programme application with the necessary documentation.It is important to familiarise yourself with the key dates. Please note that the scholarship deadline aligns with the programme application deadline, not the deadline for submitting supporting documentation.[Key dates for master's studies](https://www.chalmers.se/en/education/application-and-admission/key-dates-for-masters-studies/ "Key dates for master's studies")
The scholarships listed below are for prospective students only. Applications must be submitted prior to the start of the first academic year at Chalmers as they are not open to current students. Scholarship offers are valid for two years of study under certain conditions. These conditions are outlined in the Scholarship Regulations.
