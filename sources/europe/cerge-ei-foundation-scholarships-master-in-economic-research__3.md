# Admissions

**Master in Economic Research. The Admissions Committee evaluates all materials received by the application deadline March 31 (or August 7 in case of 2nd admission round).** Applicants are notified when their application is complete and has been forwarded to the Committee. The Admission Committee evaluates whether the applicants' background, qualification and professional interests sufficiently meet the requirements of the program and are in accordance with the research focus of the CERGE-EI. The results are known within 2 weeks after the deadline and applicants are notified immediately. There are only two possible outcomes – direct admission or rejection. The number of admitted students is around 40 each year. The academic year starts in October.
