#  How to apply to international master's programmes

Do you want to apply to an International Master’s Programme at the University of Helsinki? This quick guide gives you a general overview of the admissions schedule and the application process.
The schedule and application instructions on this page are for the application round for the autumn 2026 intake.

## Master's admissions schedule
  * **2 January 2026 at 8.00 (UTC +2):** Application period starts and application form opens in Studyinfo.
  * **16 January 2026 at 15.00 (UTC +2):** Application period ends and application form closes. The application form cannot be modified after the deadline.
  * **23 January 2026 at 15.00 (UTC +2):** Deadline for uploading application documents to the application form.
  * **March 2026:** Eligible applications are evaluated by the master's programmes. Interviews for those programmes that use interviews as part of the admission process.
  * **7-15 April 2026:** Admissions results are published starting after the Easter holidays.
  * **29 May 2026 at 15.00 (UTC +3):** Deadline for conditionally admitted applicants to submit officially certified educational documents to the Admission Services.
  * **9 July 2026 at 15.00 (UTC +3):** Deadline for accepting the admission offer (study place).
  * **4 August 2026 at 15.00 (UTC+3):** Admission from the waiting list ends for those programmes that use a waiting list.
  * **31 August 2026 at 15.00 (UTC+3):** Deadline for paying the tuition fee for those students who are required to pay tuition fees. Deadline for registering for the academic year.
  * **August–September 2026:** Orientation week and start of your studies.

## A quick guide to the application process

**The application period for studies starting in autumn 2026 is from 2 Jan 2026 at 8:00 (UTC+2) to 16 Jan 2026 at 15:00 (UTC+2).**
The academic year always begins on 1 August.

To be eligible to apply to a master’s programme, **you must have completed a first-cycle (bachelor's) degree** or an equivalent first qualification.
If you complete your degree only after the application period ends, you can still apply as long as you fulfil the other admission criteria. If you are offered a student place, it will be conditional and you must submit proof of your graduation by the date given in the admission criteria.
You must also meet the degree programme-specific language requirements.

When applying to an international master’s programme, you have to submit all the required attachments as instructed in the application instructions and admission criteria.
During the application phase, it is enough to upload good quality copies of the required attachments to the application form. You do not need to submit officially certified documents during the application phase.
If the documents regarding your previous education are in a language other than English, Finnish or Swedish, you must also submit authorised translations into one of these languages in addition to the documents in the original language.

The application form must be submitted before the deadline. Late applications are not accepted.
You can only fill in one application form. You can apply to max three master's programmes with the same application form.
If you are liable to pay the application fee, it will be collected by the Finnish National Agency of Education in connection with filling out the application form. The fee must be paid or you must upload a document proving the exempted category. Otherwise, your application will not be processed. **There are no application fee waivers.**

**If you are liable to pay tuition fees, you can apply for a scholarship at the same time when applying to an international master’s programme. There are no separate scholarship application forms.**

You can supplement your application with the required attachments until 23 Jan 2026 at 15:00 (UTC+2) by uploading them to the application form on Studyinfo.fi. Late attachments are not accepted.

Admission results for this application round will be published at the latest on 15 Apr 2026.

NOTE (from search of studies.helsinki.fi bulletin and helsinki.fi admissions): The application fee is a one-time national fee of 100 euros for non-EU/EEA citizens, covering all applications to studies beginning in the same academic term. The next application round (autumn 2027 intake) is 5-19 January 2027.
