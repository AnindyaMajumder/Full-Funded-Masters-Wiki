# VILNIUS TECH — Tuition and Other Fees (crawl)
Source: https://vilniustech.lt/en/international-students/full-time-students/tuition-and-other-fees/
Accessed: 2026-06-20

Tuition fees are charged to all students; however, depending on your citizenship, government loans and scholarships are available (all EU students can apply for government funding), residence status, unit load, and the level of the programme enrolled in (undergraduate/postgraduate).

  * Application fee
    All applicants must pay the 100 EUR non-refundable application fee. The fee can be paid by bank transfer to the VILNIUS TECH account or by credit card in the VILNIUS TECH payment site.
  * Registration Fee
    A 200 EUR registration fee is paid with the first tuition fee after receiving the official acceptance letter.
  * Tuition Fee for Students Outside European Union
    All non-EU country first year students are sent an invoice for tuition fee payment before you start the visa procedure.

The following tuition fees for **one year** (2026-2027 academic year) are set for non-EU students:

#### Master's degree programmes:
Business Management; Economics Engineering; Finance Engineering; Transport Logistics; Business Leadership; Sustainability Management — 5450 EUR
All of the other Master's degree programmes in Engineering/IT/Technologies — 6450 EUR
5th year in the Architecture study programme — 7950 EUR

  * Money when You Arrive
    Students must open a bank account in Vilnius with at least 7500 EUR/year. A bank statement from the bank is required. All expenses related to accommodation, food, traveling, extension of Temporary Residence Permit (TRP), copying literature, re-examination, and medical services are the sole responsibility of the student.
  * Work in Lithuania
    Students who intend to work in the Republic of Lithuania can do so after obtaining a Temporary Residence Permit (TRP) in Lithuania. Bachelor's and Master's degree students can work full-time while studying in Lithuania.
  * Refund Policy for International Students
    If a student's visa/TRP is denied or he/she cannot come for studies for another important reason, we may refund tuition and registration fees. Evidence of visa/TRP denial is required.
