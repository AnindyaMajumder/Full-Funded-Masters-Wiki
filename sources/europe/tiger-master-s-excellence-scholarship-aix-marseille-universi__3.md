# AMSE international fellowships (TIGER Master's Excellence Scholarship)
Source: https://www.amse-aixmarseille.fr/en/study/amse-international-fellowships
Accessed: 2026-06-18
(Retrieved via WebFetch; crawl of univ-amu.fr main-domain pages returned 404 during this session — content corroborated across feg.univ-amu.fr and sesstim.univ-amu.fr.)

## Eligibility
- Students entering a Master's programme with no prior French higher education enrollment
- Excluded: French nationals and dual nationals
- Selection criterion: Academic excellence only

## Benefits
- Funding: up to €10,000 for one academic year
- Housing: University residence (CROUS) room reserved (subject to completing required formalities)
- Support: Accompaniment from arrival through stay duration

## Eligible Master's Programmes (AMSE)
- Master Economics
- Master Econometrics Statistics
- Master Finance

## Application Process
Step 1: Select an eligible Master's programme
Step 2: Submit application via one of three platforms based on eligibility + fill out the interest form
Step 3: Programme coordinators assess applications and select scholarship recipients
Step 4: Receive program admission letter
Step 5: Receive TIGER scholarship award letter if selected

## Application Platforms & Deadlines
- Études en France: From November 2025 (country-dependent)
- MonMaster: From February 2026
- e-Candidat: From March 2026

## Scholarship Decision
Award notifications communicated beginning June by the university's international relations department.
