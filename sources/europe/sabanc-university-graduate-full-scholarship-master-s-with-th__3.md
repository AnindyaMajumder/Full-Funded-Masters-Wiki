# Graduate Applications Requirements for 2026-2027 Academic Year — Sabancı University (excerpts)
Source: https://www.sabanciuniv.edu/en/admission-to-graduate-programs
Accessed: 2026-06-20

To apply for graduate programs at Sabancı University, candidates must meet the minimum requirements set by each Institute. Applications for graduate programs are submitted online.
Documents submitted to the University go through a screening process. The prospects meeting the minimum application criteria are called for a written exam and/or an interview. The Admission Committee then decides which students to be admitted, and the applicants are informed accordingly.

## Application Requirements For Graduate School of Engineering and Natural Sciences Thesis Master's Programs 
  * Applications for master programs are evaluated by the respective Admission Jury along with individual program entries. Admission is finalized by the approval of the related Graduate School Executive Board upon the recommendation of the Jury and announced to the applicants. 
  * Applicants may be called for written exams and interviews. All written exams and interviews are held in English. 
  * Periods for application are announced in Academic Calendar. 
  * Applications by international applicants studying abroad are valid only after the approval and recognition of the last graduated higher education institution and the course of study by the Turkish Higher Education Council. The necessary enquiry is made by the University. 

#### Application Documents and Requirements:
#### 1. Online Application Form
#### 2. Official Transcript
An official document issued by the registrar of the candidate's previous institution showing all courses taken and grades received by the candidate.
#### 3. Statement of Purpose
A summary of the candidate's reason for applying to the program, career objectives, motivations and expectations related to the program.
#### 4. Curriculum Vitae (CV)
####  5. Two Letters of Recommendation
The email addresses of the two faculty members who will send the reference letters should be entered in the 'Recommenders' section.
Reference letters uploaded to the system by the candidate will be considered invalid.
#### 6. Graduate Program Entrance Exam Results
**ALES, e-ALES** Numerical Min. Score 65
**GRE** Quantitative Min. Score 153
**GAT** Accumulative Test Score (General Test) Min. 65 (Pakistan NTS)
#### 7. English Proficiency Exam Results (*)
TOEFL IBT: Min. Score 80 (Best Score is not accepted); PTE Min. Score 69; CAE Min. B; CPE Min. C; YDS, e-YDS, YÖKDİL e-YÖKDİL Min. Score 67; e-TEP Min Score 54
(*) Applicants who do not submit a valid English Proficiency Exam score (fulfilling all other requirements) can only be admitted to a graduate study on the condition of obtaining a satisfactory score from the Sabancı University English Language Assessment Exam (ELAE) which will be run right after the final application deadline.
Those candidates who fail to attend or get a unsatisfactory grade in the ELAE exam are obliged to attend and successfully complete the English Language Preparation course(s) in order to enroll in a graduate program. Scholarships do not apply to the English Language Preparation courses.
#### 8. Degree Requirements
  * Candidates are required to have completed their previous studies by the time of enrollment at Sabancı University. 
  * Candidates applying for Master's Programs must hold an undergraduate degree or be registered in the last term of an undergraduate program.

## Application Requirements for the Graduate School of Social Sciences Master’s Programs 
**Who can apply?**
  * Admitted candidates are expected to have completed their undergraduate degrees by the time of registration for the graduate program. 
  * Applications by international applicants studying abroad are valid only after the approval and recognition of the last graduated higher education institution and the course of study by the Turkish Higher Education Council. The necessary enquiry is made by the University. 

Required documents (Thesis Programs, Table 1-A): Online Application Form (1), Official Transcript (2), Two recommendation letters (3), One passport-sized photo (4), Statement of Purpose (5), Sample work (6), Portfolio (7), Graduate Education Entrance Examination scores / ALES-GRE-GAT (8), English proficiency / ELAE (9), Research Plan min 1000 words (10), CV (11) — exact subset varies per program.

Candidates may be invited to written exams or interviews. Exams and interviews will be in English. Based on the conditions, interviews can be in-person or online.

NOTE on scholarships + prep year: "Candidates who cannot meet the English proficiency criterion will be required to complete the English preparatory year to continue their MA program. However, there are no scholarships available for the preparatory year, and thus such candidates will be required to pay the full tuition fee."
