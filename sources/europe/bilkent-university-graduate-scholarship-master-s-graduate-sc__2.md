### GRE / ALES
Don’t have your exam scores yet? You can still apply.
We encourage passionate and qualified applicants to apply, even if you have not yet received your required exam scores. You may be offered conditional admission based on your existing application materials. All official scores must be submitted by the time of registration to secure your full enrollment.
**ALES** is required from resident Turkish citizens who are applying for the graduate programs. Those applicants who are not Turkish citizens, and Turkish citizens who have completed their undergrad degrees without enrolling in a Turkish University may provide **GRE General Test** (obligatory for foreign students) scores instead of ALES. All other applicants must provide ALES to apply the graduate school. ALES and GRE scores are valid for 5 years. 
Home edition test results will not be accepted.
#### Minimum Acceptable ALES Scores
  * **M.S.: 55** (quantitative) 
  * **Ph.D.: 55** (quantitative, for those who hold an M.S. degree with thesis.) 
  * **Ph.D.: 80** (quantitative, for those who do not hold an M.S. degree with thesis )


#### Minimum Acceptable GRE Scores
  * **M.S.:** Minimum quantitative: **157**
  * **Ph.D.:** Minimum quantitative: **157**
  * (GRE ETS CODE: 0620)


### English Proficiency
Don’t have your exam scores yet? You can still apply.
We encourage passionate and qualified applicants to apply, even if you have not yet received your required exam scores. You may be offered conditional admission based on your existing application materials. All official scores must be submitted by the time of registration to secure your full enrollment.
TOEFL IBT (at least 87 before 2026 or 4.5/6 after 2026) or,IELTS Academic (at least 6.5 average and minimum 5.5 from each section) or,PAE (Bilkent Proficiency in Academic English) (at least C)
If you submit IELTS or Bilkent English Proficiency Exam (PAE) results and you are applying for a Ph.D. program, you also need to satisfy the foreign language requirement as imposed by the Inter-Universities Board (ÜAK) of Turkey. In that case, [a minimum of 55 from YDS, e-YDS, YÖKDİL, e-](http://mfbe.bilkent.edu.tr/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/esdegerlikdokuman060420211.pdf)[YÖKDİL](http://mfbe.bilkent.edu.tr/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/esdegerlikdokuman060420211.pdf) or its equivalents as listed by the Board, in a language other than the mother tongue must be submitted.
For Ph.D. applicants, citizens of Turkey who plan to submit TOEFL IBT scores must take the TOEFL IBT exam at one of the test centers in public universities (YÖK requirement).
  * Validity period for all exams: 2 years
  * TOEFL IBT “Computer-based” test results will be accepted.
  * TOEFL IBT “Online test taken at home (Home Edition)” and “my best score” test results will not be accepted.
  * IELTS Academic “Computer-based and paper-based” test results will be accepted.
  * IELTS Academic “OSR (One Skill Retake)” exam will not be accepted.
  * (TOEFL ETS CODE: 0620)
