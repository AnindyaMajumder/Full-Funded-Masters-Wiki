# Helmut Veith Stipend for Female Master’s Students in Computer Science
##  **Annual Deadline: November 30** th
Female students in the field of computer science (CS) who (plan to) pursue one of the Master‘s programme in Computer Science at TU Wien taught in English and who have (or have the interest to develop) a solid mathematical and technical background in at least one of the areas in which Austrian scientist Helmut Veith worked, are invited to apply for the annually awarded Helmut Veith Stipend.
The Helmut Veith Stipend for female master students is dedicated to the memory of Professor Helmut Veith (1971-2016)​, an outstanding computer scientist who worked in the fields of logic in computer science, computer-aided verification, software engineering, and computer security.
The Helmut Veith Stipend's fund is set up by TU Wien, the Wolfgang Pauli Institute and the colleagues and friends of the late Prof. Veith.
### **BENEFITS**
-EUR 7000 per year-Waiver of tuition fees at TU Wien
### **LOCATION OF MASTER'S PROGRAMME**
For study in Austria, at TU Wien
### **FOR FIELD OF STUDY**
Applicants must be eligible for admission to one of the Master’s Programme in Computer Science at TU Wien that are taught in English.
Currently those are:
  * Master in Data Science
  * Master in Business Informatics
  * Master in Logic and Artificial Intelligence
  * Master in Media and Human-Centered Computing
  * Master in Software Engineering
  * Master in Quantum Information Science and Technology
  * Master in Embedded Computing Systems
  * Master in Visual Computing


### **APPLICATION**
While applications for funding can be filed **before or in parallel** with the admissions process, the funding will only be awarded to applicants who have been unconditionally admitted to the Master's Programms. An application for funding does not replace the admissions process; neither does a conditional offer of funding entitle the applicant to study at TU Wien.
Your application must be submitted electronically via the [job portal, opens an external URL in a new window](https://jobs.tuwien.ac.at/Job/244375).
### **DEADLINE**
November 30th (annually)
