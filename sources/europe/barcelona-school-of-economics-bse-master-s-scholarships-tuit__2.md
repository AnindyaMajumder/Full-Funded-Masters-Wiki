# Admission Process for Master's Degrees
Application deadline for 2026-27: July 2, 2026
Programs begin in September 2026

## How to apply to BSE Master's programs
#### 1. Create an account in the BSE application system ([BSE Application System](https://apply.bse.eu/apply/login))
#### 3. Upload the required documents
The following documents will need to be uploaded before you can submit your application:
  * Undergraduate academic transcripts or mark sheets
  * Copy of your undergraduate diploma
  * Copy of your passport
  * Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  * Statement of Purpose
  * Proof of your level in English: 90 TOEFL points; 6.5 IELTS band; 120 Duolingo English points; and Cambridge Exams (Proficiency or Advanced, 180 minimum score)
  * If you are applying to the PhD Track Program, you are required to submit a GRE score

#### 2. Complete the application questions — required info:
  * Program(s) you want to apply (you have the option to chose up to 3 programs)
  * Personal data / Education / Exam grades / English language level / Work experience
  * Finance and scholarship details
  * Names and contact information for 2 referees

#### 4. Pay the corresponding fee and submit your application
Application Fee: €100. Payable by credit card or Flywire. Non-refundable.

## Application requirements — admission criteria:
  * Outstanding academic record
  * Strong quantitative background
  * Reference letter

## Gather your application materials
#### Undergraduate diploma and transcripts
If already completed undergrad: copy of diploma + official final transcript.
If not yet completed: signed declaration stating you will obtain your degree before the start of the Master's program + provisional transcript.
If your diploma or transcript is not written in English, Spanish, Catalan, French, Italian, or Portuguese, you will also have to include a sworn translation.
#### Curriculum vitae (2 pages maximum)
#### Proof of English language proficiency
Unless you are a native English speaker or have done your undergraduate degree at an English-speaking university, you must provide TOEFL, IELTS, or Cambridge scores or certificates.
Accepted certificates: IELTS Academic Test (6,5), TOEFL (90), Duolingo English Test (minimum score 120), Cambridge Exams (Proficiency or Advance, 180 minimum score B2-C2)
#### Contact two referees — Letters from academia signed by a professor are strongly preferred. The two signed letters must be uploaded directly to the online application system by your referees. Your application is not considered complete until both letters have been uploaded.
#### Copy of your passport
#### Statement of purpose — critical document.
#### Optional but highly recommended: GRE General Test scores
The only program that requires a GRE General Test score as part of the application package is the PhD Track Program. However, applicants to all other programs are encouraged to provide a GRE score. BSE institution code for GRE General Test: 2531

## After Applying — track status via application system; email decision; informed of any financial support awarded.
