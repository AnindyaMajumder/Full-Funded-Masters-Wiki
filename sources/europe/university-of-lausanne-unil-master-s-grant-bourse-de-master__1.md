The Unil Master's Scholarship is awarded for the minimum statutory period of the Master’s programme and amounts to **CHF 1,600 per month** (see the general conditions below for further details).
As this amount does not cover the full cost of living in Switzerland, the scholarship does not constitute full funding. Applicants are therefore advised to consult the information on the cost of living in Switzerland for a student, available in the [Admissions Service FAQ](https://www.unil.ch/unil/en/home/menuinst/etudier/immatriculations-et-inscriptions/faq-liens-utiles.html#futur-etudiant-doctorant "faq-liens-utiles").
Scholarship recipients are exempt from the standard course registration fees. **Only the semester fees of CHF 80 remain payable.**

**Withdrawal from the master programme will lead to the suspension of the scholarship**

## Conditions of Application
  * Hold  recognized as equivalent to a Swiss university bachelor before starting the Master’s programme at Unil.
  * Have achieved **outstanding academic results** during your studies.
  * Have a **minimum language level of C1** according to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR):
    * French for programmes taught in French
    * English for programmes taught in English
  * Not be currently enrolled at Unil.
  * Have paid the **administrative fee of CHF 200**.
## Application Procedure
  1. **Prepare your application file in the form of a single PDF**.
  2. **Online registration and submission of application:**
     * Enter your application online.
     * Upload your PDF application file.
     * Deadline for online registration: November 1
     * **Be aware:** applications sent by e-mail and incomplete applications will not be considered.
  3. **Send letters of recommendation by e-mail:**
     * Have two letters of recommendation sent by teachers familiar with the applicant's past or present studies.
     * The letters must be sent directly by the author to: mastergrants@unil.ch
     * Deadline: November 1
     * **Be aware:** only letters sent after online registration will be considered.
  4. **Send certified true copies of academic documents by post (if applicable)** This step only concerns candidates whose diplomas were obtained in a State that has not ratified the Lisbon Convention (https://www.coe.int/fr/web/conventions/full-list?module=signatures-by-treaty&treatynum=165)
     * Have the university or college certify and seal in an envelope: university diplomas, university transcripts, provisional certificates of achievement (where applicable).
     * Send these documents to: University of Lausanne Social Affairs and Student Mobility Service (SASME) Bâtiment Unicentre CH - 1015 Lausanne Switzerland
     * Deadline: 1 November (postmark date)
**Please note:** your application for the Master's Scholarship also serves as your application for the chosen Master's degree. It is therefore not necessary to apply separately to the Admissions Office.
**Incomplete applications will not be taken into account.**
## Application Contents
**The application file must include:**
  * a single PDF containing all the required documents,
  * two letters of recommendation,
  * certified true copies of academic documents (if applicable).
Single PDF
A single PDF to be downloaded during online registration with the documents in the following order:
  * A complete and up-to-date curriculum vitae (CV) with photo.
  * A covering letter (1 to 2 pages maximum).
  * A copy of the university diploma(s) (if already obtained) and of the official transcripts for all examinations taken at the university as well as authenticated translation if these documents are not in French, German, English or Italian.
  * A copy of the description of the programme for the last semester, or even the last year of the bachelor's degree, if the bachelor's degree has not yet been obtained.
  * A copy of the official language certificate in which the master at Unil is taught (if required).
  * A copy of the secondary school leaving diploma (High School) and an authenticated translation if the diploma is not in French, German, English or Italian.
  * A copy of your passport.
  * A copy of proof of payment of the administrative fee of CHF 200.
**Instructions for paying the administrative fee:** Beneficiary: Université de Lausanne - 1015 Lausanne IBAN: CH17 0900 0000 1001 3575 3 SWIFT/BIC: POFICHBEXXX. This fee is non-refundable, even if the applicant withdraws their application or is refused admission.
Letters of recommendation
  * The application must include **two letters of recommendation**.
  * The letters must be sent directly by the author from a **professional (not private) e-mail address.**
Certified true copies (if applicable)
This procedure concerns candidates whose diplomas were obtained in a State that has not ratified the Lisbon Convention.
## Selection Deadline
  * **1 November:** Application deadline.
  * **November to December:** Processing of applications by SASME and the Admissions Office (SII)
  * **December to January:** The SASME informs applicants whose application is incomplete or has not been accepted.
  * **January to February:** Complete applications are transferred to the faculties for consideration.
  * **February to March:** The SASME informs applicants whose application has not been accepted by the faculty.
  * **March:** The Selection Board for the Master's Scholarship selects the applicants who will receive scholarships.
  * **Early April:** Applicants are notified of the Selection Board's decision.
**NB: Decisions may not be appealed!**

## FAQ
Who can apply?
Students whose academic results are of a very high level (there is no list with required grades by country available).
Applicants must have graduated from a foreign university **only**: their qualification/university degree must be deemed equivalent to a Swiss university bachelor’s degree (180 ECTS credits).
**Students with a foreign university degree who have already begun a Master's programme at Unil or who are in their preparatory year are not authorized to apply.**
Is the Master's Scholarship sufficient to live in Switzerland?
The Federal and Cantonal Authorities consider that **an amount of approximately CHF 2000.- per month** is necessary to live in Lausanne. As such, the amount of the Master's Scholarship does not cover the full expenses of a student. It is thus **highly recommended to have some personal complementary financial ressources in order to supplement the monthly budget.**
Nonetheless, **Unil guarantees to all Master's scholarship holders an accommodation with the Student Housing Foundation of Lausanne (FMEL).**
Is there an age limit?
There is **no age limit** to apply for the Master's Scholarship. However, for applicants from countries subject to visa restrictions, a higher age as well as a completed first master's degree may have an influence on the obtention of a visa.
Can I apply if I already hold a degree equivalent to a Swiss master's degree?
Applicants who have already obtained a master's degree from a foreign university (or are about to obtain one at the time of application) also have the right to apply, **although priority will be given to applicants who only hold a bachelor's degree**.
What language level is required for the chosen master programme?
For a master taught in ENGLISH: For candidates whose mother tongue is not English or whose bachelor's degree was not entirely taught in English, a level of C1 minimum should be attained and a language certificate is required. Accepted: TOEFL IBT 100; Academic IELTS 7; Cambridge English Advanced (CAE) Grade B; Cambridge English Proficiency (CPE) Grade C; TOEIC 850. The diploma should not be older than 2 years.
For a master taught in FRENCH: DALF C1/C2, TCF C1+, etc.
**If the master programme chosen at Unil is taught in the mother tongue of the candidate or the bachelor studies were entirely taught in the language of the master programme chosen at Unil, no language certificate is required!**
Why do I need a proof of payment of the CHF 200 Administrative fees?
This is the fee for assessing and examining your application. **This fee must be paid by all applicants wishing to register at Unil. No fee waiver is possible.** **No refunds can be made, even where applications are withdrawn or refused.**
Who can write a recommendation letter?
Any member of the teaching staff with knowledge of your past or present studies. Any of your current or former employers may also write a letter of recommendation.
