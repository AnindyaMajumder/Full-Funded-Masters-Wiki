### Our goal
The Excellence Scholarships enable selected, outstanding Master’s students to focus fully on their studies and research – a highly persuasive argument in the international competition to attract the most talented students.
This support, however, is not just an investment in talented individuals, but advances society as whole. As the career paths of former scholarship recipients show, they have excellent prospects of holding key positions in business, science or politics, or founding their own companies. In this way, the Excellence Scholarship Programme is not only a targeted promotion of young talent, but also has a lasting positive impact on innovation in Switzerland and beyond.
The programme is fully funded by donations. Tho goal is to award at least 50-60 scholarships per year through a [highly competitive selection](https://ethz-foundation.ch/en/spotlight/magazines-talents-2023-lorenz-hurni-quality-is-everything/).
### Your support enables
  * ETH Zurich to attract highly talented students to its Master’s programmes;
  * the best possible development of potential by enabling the scholars to pursue their scientific interests, network across disciplines and motivate one another to achieve great things;
  * Switzerland, as a centre for knowledge and business, to maintain an internationally leading position in the future.

**Good to know:** around three-quarters of the foreign scholarship students remain in Switzerland after completing their degree. They work and pay taxes and insurance. This means that the amount invested in them flows back into society after an average of three years.
