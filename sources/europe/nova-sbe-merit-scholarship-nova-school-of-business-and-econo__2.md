## Application rounds & deadlines (2026/27 Fall intake)

General Application Period: November 13, 2025 – April 30, 2026

Round schedule:
- Round 1: Nov 13–27, 2025 (4-week decision time)
- Round 2: Nov 28, 2025 – Jan 6, 2026 (8-week decision time)
- Round 3: Jan 6 – Feb 12, 2026 (8-week decision time)
- Round 4: Feb 12 – Mar 23, 2026 (8-week decision time)
- Round 5: Mar 23 – Apr 30, 2026 (8-week decision time)

Note: IMF, IMM, CEMS MIM, and Double Degrees close earlier on March 23, 2026.

## Fees
Application Fee: "€51 for all candidates, per application" (non-refundable)
Enrollment Fee: €1,499 total (€1,250 non-refundable enrollment + €249 scholarship
donation, opt-out available)

## Mandatory application documents
"Portrait/ID format photo," identification scan, CV in English, official transcript with
grades, one-page motivation letter in English, and "English certificate with a minimum
level of B2."

English exemptions: Nationals of English-speaking countries, completed Bachelor's fully
in English, or completed/completing Bachelor's at Nova SBE (excludes IMF/IMM candidates).

## GMAT/GRE
GMAT/GRE is listed as "Optional documents" to enrich applications, not mandatory.

## Decision timeline
"Allow up to 8 weeks for the decision (maximum), counting from the first day of the round
in which you submitted your application."
