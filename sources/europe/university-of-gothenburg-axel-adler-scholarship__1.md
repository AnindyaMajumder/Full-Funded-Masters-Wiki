#  Axel Adler Scholarship 
Axel Adler scholarship 2026 is a tuition fee waiver and is awarded to students studying international Master’s or Bachelor’s Programs at the University of Gothenburg. The selection of scholars is primarily based on the applicant's qualifications in relation to the chosen study program. Axel Adler Scholarship is funded by Adlerbertska Scholarship Foundation, Adlerbertska Grant Foundation and the Swedish Council for Higher Education (UHR). 

##  Axel Adler Scholarship notifications 2026 
Scholarship offers for **Bachelor's and Master's program applicants** have been sent out via email to the selected recipients. Please make sure to also check your spam folder.

## Requirements
To be eligible for **Axel Adler Scholarship 2026,** you must**:**
  * Be required to pay tuition in Sweden.
  * Apply for an international campus-based program at the University of Gothenburg, as your first priority.
  * Submit your application for studies by **15 January 2026** and pay the application fee and document your eligibility for studies by**2 February 2026** through [www.universityadmissions.se](http://www.universityadmissions.se/).

## Application
Students who have applied to a program at the University of Gothenburg as their first priority through [universityadmissions.se](https://www.universityadmissions.se) and who fulfill the requirements, will receive an email with application instructions around**February 10, 2026. The invitation to apply** for Axel Adler Scholarship will be sent to the email address you have registered at universityadmissions.se.
If you are eligible and have not received an email by this date (and also checked your Spam mail folder), please let us know by sending a mail to scholarships@gu.se.

### CV and Letter of Motivation
When applying for Axel Adler Scholarship you must write a CV and a Letter of Motivation in the application system. No documents can be attached to your scholarship application. The CV and Letter of Motivation are used in the selection process. In your motivational letter, you have the possibility to highlight the merits presented in your CV. 

## Selection process
Scholarship applications are assessed and ranked by academic staff based on the CV and Letter of motivation included in the scholarship application. Your academic merits can also be taken in consideration. 
One scholarship will be awarded for each program, with the exception of programs at the Artistic Faculty, which will be awarded a total of four scholarships for all programs.
**News for 2026 selection round:** If sufficient funds are available, up to **ten additional scholarships** may be awarded to the programmes with the highest number of scholarship applicants.

## New: Axel Adler Scholarship for Sustainability
Sustainable development is a vital part of the University of Gothenburg’s vision. Therefore, we also offer the Axel Adler scholarship for Sustainability, an additional scholarship program, for students admitted to one of our sustainability focused Master's programs. 
### Eligibility
  * You need to meet the general requirements for Axel Adler Scholarship 2026 and the special requirements for Axel Adler Scholarship for Sustainability
  * If you are eligible to apply, you apply for both scholarships in the same application.
  * A minimum of 2 students and a maximum of 12 will be awarded the Axel Adler Scholarship for Sustainability. The scholarship is a tuition fee waiver. 

## Frequently asked questions
###  How large is the scholarship amount? 
The scholarship covers the tuition fee for the study program you are admitted to. It is a tuition fee waiver only; no additional financial support for living expenses or travel grants is provided. Make sure you have the finances to support yourself for the costs of living during your studies.

###  Can I combine the Axel Adler Scholarship with other scholarships? 
Please note that it is not possible to combine the Axel Adler Scholarship with another scholarship covering the tuition fee. You may apply for several tuition fee scholarships but can only be awarded one. 

###  How will the scholarship be distributed? 
The scholarship foundations will transfer the payment directly to the University of Gothenburg for your tuition fees. The scholar does not need to act in this matter.

###  If I am unable to apply in time, can I apply later? 
Late applications will not be considered.

###  What happens if I am unsuccessful with my studies? Will the scholarship be withdrawn? 
To keep your scholarship, you must complete at least 75% of the semester’s credits. Regular follow-ups are conducted.

###  Am I eligible for the Axel Adler Scholarship if I choose an online programme? 
No, you are not eligible for Axel Adler Scholarships if you choose to study an online program. The scholarships are for campus-based programs in Gothenburg only.
