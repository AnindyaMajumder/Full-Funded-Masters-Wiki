# IP Paris PhD Track — scholarships & funding (source 2)

## Scholarships page
URL: https://www.ip-paris.fr/en/education/scholarships

Verbatim (from official ip-paris.fr search snippets):

- "Institut Polytechnique de Paris awards excellence scholarships each year to PhD
  Track students, covering their Master's years as well as doctoral funding
  commitments with a monthly salary."
- "The funding awarded by IP Paris to PhD Track students is divided into three types
  of scholarships: Full Package (FP): 5-year (or 4-year) funding, including a
  Master's excellence scholarship (€10,000/year) for 2 years (or 1 year) and a
  doctoral funding commitment for 3 years (a total minimum gross funding of
  approximately €82,800 over 3 years)."
- Three types: Full Package (FP); Master's Scholarship (BM); Doctoral Package (DP),
  where the Doctoral Package provides a 3-year doctoral funding commitment covering
  only the doctoral studies.
- "All PhD Track students benefit from an attractive rate of 243€. They can also
  benefit from an excellence scholarship during their master's year(s) and then from
  a doctoral contract with a monthly salary as part of the PhD."
- Tuition subsidy: Years 1 and 2 are reduced from approximately €7,000 per year to
  €243 per year; Years 3, 4, and 5 are set at €380 per year.

## Application process
URL: https://www.ip-paris.fr/en/education/scholarships

- "No additional steps are required when applying to the PhD Track program. If you
  are admitted, your application will also be reviewed by a jury for the potential
  award of a PhD Track scholarship. You will then be contacted by the Institut
  Polytechnique de Paris; otherwise, you will be placed on a funding waiting list."

## VLS-TS visa (non-EU, applies to Bangladeshi nationals)
URL: https://www.ip-paris.fr/en/education/international/international-students

- "If you are not a national of a European Economic Area country (European Union
  countries, Iceland, Norway and Liechtenstein) or Switzerland, you must request a
  student long stay visa which is valid as a French residence permit (VLS-TS = Visa
  étudiant Long Séjour valant Titre de Séjour). This visa allows you to remain in
  France for one year."
