Brexit
Note that British students are considered Programme countries students under the Erasmus Mundus Master programmes selected before 2021. For the Masters selected from 2021, the distinction between Programme and Partner countries does not exist any longer and all students receive the same scholarships.
Data protection
Please note that when you apply for a scholarship, some data is shared with EACEA and treated according to the following privacy statements:
  * EACEA Mobility Tool Privacy Statement for the applications to a project selected before 2021.
  * Privacy Statement for the applications to a project selected as of 2021.

Scams and Frauds
We have been informed of numerous attempted frauds. Be cautious when receiving any advertisement inviting you to apply for an EU funded scholarship; before giving out any personal data, verify that the information comes from a trustworthy source. Be wary of messages telling you to pay a fee to be selected as a scholarship holder; EU scholarships are not granted for a fee.
Complaints
Applicants and scholarship holders can submit a complaint about the following:
- the way your application for a scholarship has been dealt with; - the way your mobility and/or scholarship has been managed.
