University studies in Sweden are free for Swedish and EU/EEA citizens. If you are not from one of these countries or exempt for other reasons, you will need to pay tuition fees. 
## Are you required to pay fees to study at Linköping University?
  * If you are a citizen from a country outside the European Union (EU), European Economic Area (EEA) or Switzerland, then you must pay an application fee to University Admissions and tuition fees to Linköping University (LiU).
  * You do not need to pay application and tuition fees if you are a citizen of Sweden or any country within the EU/EEA or Switzerland, or if you have certain types of residency in Sweden.

Please note that fees do not cover the price of accommodation, course literature or the general cost of living. 

##  How much are the fees? 
### Application fee
An application fee of SEK 900 (Swedish kronor) must be paid to Universityadmissions.se when applying for a programme or course to cover the processing of your application. You must pay the application fee before the deadline, for your application to be processed and considered. 
### Tuition fee
The tuition fee is paid in semester instalments. Newly admitted students are encouraged to pay their tuition fees to Linköping University as soon as possible after receiving an offer at LiU. The payment deadline varies depending on when you received your admission offer. If you received your admission offer at the end of March the deadline is 15 May and if you received your admission offer in July or later you must pay within 10 days of receiving the offer. Find specific tuition fees on the programme page or on universityadmissions.se.
#### What do I receive for paying tuition fees?
Tuition fees at Linköping University cover the following benefits: 
  * The Swedish state's insurance FAS+, including accident and property cover 
  * Study-related services such as study administration, study counselling, student health care, library access, computer labs, etc. 

Course literature, housing costs, living expenses, etc. are not included in the tuition fee. You will need approximately SEK 10,000 a month for such expenses.
