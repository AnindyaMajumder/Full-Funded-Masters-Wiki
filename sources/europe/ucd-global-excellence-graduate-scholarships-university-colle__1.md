## FAQs
###  Am I eligible?
This scholarship is ONLY open to incoming students who are classed as non-EU for tuition fee purposes. (For information regarding fee classification, please review the fees page.)
To apply for the Global Excellence Scholarship, students must first have received an offer (or a conditional offer) to study on an eligible UCD course.

###  Which programmes are eligible?
Many undergraduate and graduate taught programmes are eligible for this award however, some programmes are excluded. Please check the following document to see if your programme is listed as eligible: Eligible Programmes 2026.

###  How do I apply?
It is important to **submit your application to your programme of study as early as possible**. During peak scholarship competition windows, we experience a significant surge in applications. As a result, processing delays may occur.
While UCD strives to review programme applications and return decisions promptly, we cannot guarantee that all admissions applications will be processed prior to the scholarship application deadline.

###  What is the application deadline?
Application Deadlines for 2026/27 TBC:
  * 1st February 2026: North America Undergraduate Applications
  * 28th February 2026: South Asia Graduate Taught Applications and Middle East, Africa and Pakistan Undergraduate and Graduate Taught Applications
  * 31st March 2026: All Other Regions Applications

**Results Announcement**
Scholarship results will be shared approximately seven weeks after each regional deadline via SISWeb.
There are a limited number of 100% and 50% tuition fee scholarships for outstanding international students.

###  When will I find out if I've been successful?
Scholarship results will be shared approximately seven weeks after each regional deadline via SISWeb or no later than two weeks after the Committee has confirmed their decisions.

###  Are scholarships automatically renewed each year?
No. If you are a continuing student, please note that scholarships **are not automatically renewed**.
All continuing students will be assessed for scholarship eligibility for the next academic year in accordance with the terms and conditions of your original award. The review process takes place after the end of the academic year, and all students will be notified of the outcome between late September and October.
