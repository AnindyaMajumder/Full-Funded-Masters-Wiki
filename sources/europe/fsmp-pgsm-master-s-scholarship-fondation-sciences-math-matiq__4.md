#  PGSM Master : 2nd call for applications
PGSM second call for application is open **from February 23 rd to April 20th 2026**.
It will be open only to students who carried out a bachelor degree or a 1st year of Master in one of the universities of the FSMP network. On this call, we also accept exceptionally, students from French universities outside the FSMP network, provided that the type of program they are considering is not available in the region where they are currently studying.
**Applications will be made online, via the[online application form](https://applications.sciencesmaths-paris.fr/en/). **
