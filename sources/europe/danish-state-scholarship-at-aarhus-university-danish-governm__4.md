# Gather documentation
The documentation you need to submit depends on both your educational background and which programme(s) you apply to.

#  If you are an applicant with an international Bachelor's Degree
**You must upload:**
  * Documentation for **paid application fees** or a **valid applicable residence permit** if you are a **non-EU/EEA citizen**.
  * Your official **diploma** and official **transcript of records**. The documentation must contain your name, the course titles, grades and weight in eg. credits or ECTS. You diploma must clearly state when you completed your Bachelor’s degree. If you have not completed your degree at the time of application, you must upload your official **transcript of records** showing your examination results.
  * An official description of the **grading scale** at your foreign university. The grading scale must show the lowest passing grade and the highest grade.
  * **Course descriptions** for all courses you have passed or are currently taking. The course descriptions must be official.
  * Upload documentation that you meet the **language requirements** of the degree programme.

**If relevant in relation to your situation, you must upload:**
  * Documentation of courses you are currently taking if you have not yet completed your degree programme at the time of application.
  * Documentation of study abroad through a transcript of records and course descriptions.
  * Documentation for supplementary Bachelor’s courses you have passed or for which you are registered.
  * Documentation that you have passed or expect to pass a Master’s degree programme before commencement of studies (cf. the Master's degree rule).
  * Documentation that you have passed courses on a Master’s degree programme you did not finish (mandatory credit transfer assessment).
  * Degree programme-specific documentation.

**Appendix requested - select programmes only**
  * A number of programmes requests an _Appendix_ to be uploaded. If the programme you are interested in requests an appendix, it will be stated on the programme page under _Admission Requirements_.

**Documentation of relevant employment** If you are applying for a work-integrated programme, you must complete and upload the employment agreement form.

###  Requirements for translation of documents
Documents that were not issued in either Danish, Norwegian, Swedish or English must be translated into one of these languages.
Translations of official diplomas must be issued by the university or accompanied by the signature and stamp of an authorised translator or your home university.
