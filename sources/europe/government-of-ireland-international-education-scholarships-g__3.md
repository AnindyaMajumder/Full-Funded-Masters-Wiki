GOVERNMENT OF IRELAND INTERNATIONAL EDUCATION SCHOLARSHIPS 2026 — CALL DOCUMENT

1. Overview
The Government of Ireland International Education Scholarship (GOI-IES) programme supports high-calibre international students who wish to study at NFQ levels 9 or 10 (master's, postgraduate diploma or PhD) in Ireland. ... The programme is funded by the Government of Ireland in partnership with Irish higher education institutions (HEIs) and managed by the Higher Education Authority (HEA).

2. Details of the scholarship
GOI-IES are awarded to high-calibre international students to study full-time in Ireland for a period of one year. ... It is expected that a total of 60 scholarships will be funded under the GOI-IES programme for the 2026/27 academic year.
The scholarships will be for one year of full-time, in-person study at NFQ levels 9 or 10 (master's, postgraduate diploma or PhD) in Ireland. Scholarships will be awarded for study across all disciplines, subject to the availability of places.
The scholarships amount to €10,000 for one year of study and are awarded by the HEA to the successful applicants through the HEIs that they are attending. This funding is intended as a contribution towards student costs and living expenses. In addition, the awardee's host HEI will be required to give a full fee waiver to the awardee for the scholarship year (i.e. tuition fee and registration charge are waived). This is the minimum matched funding requirement that institutions will be required to make to the scholarship scheme.
The scholarships will not be extended beyond one academic year. However, host HEIs may decide to extend the fee waiver for courses that are three semesters long, to cover the entire duration of the course. This decision rests with the HEIs and the €10,000 amount of the stipend will remain the same.
GOI-IES scholars will be expected to commence their studies in either September or October 2026. They must also live in Ireland for the duration of their studies. An official awarding event for GOI-IES scholars will be held in spring 2027.

3. Eligibility criteria
To be eligible to apply for GOI-IES, candidates must meet all four conditions below:
   1) Have a domiciliary of origin outside the EU/EEA, Switzerland and the United Kingdom.
   2) Have received an offer of a place as an international, fee-paying student in a full-time, in-person course at an eligible Irish HEI for either:
        a. a taught master's degree programme which is at least one year long.
        b. a postgraduate diploma at NFQ level 9 which is at least one year long.
        c. a research programme (i.e. research master's or PhD) which is at least one year long.
   3) Not have previously held a GOI-IES scholarship.
   4) Not be a Russian or Belarusian citizen.

To be eligible for the GOI-IES, candidates must already have an offer of admission to a full-time, in-person master's, postgraduate diploma or PhD programme at an eligible HEI. A list of eligible HEIs is included in Appendix 1.

A candidate must have received an offer of admission before they submit their GOI-IES application. Proof of admission, i.e. an offer document, must be submitted with the GOI-IES application, in the relevant section of the GOI-IES application form. Acceptable offer documents are:
    - an offer letter issued by the HEI where the candidate has been accepted. ... The offer must be for the 2026/27 academic year.
    - an offer email from the HEI ... only acceptable if the email comes from the relevant Admissions Office and if it confirms that the candidate has been offered a place on an eligible course for the 2026/27 academic year.
    - a screenshot from the candidate's applicant portal on the HEI's website ... only acceptable if it confirms that the candidate has been offered a place on an eligible course for the 2026-27 academic year.
Both conditional and final offers are acceptable for a GOI-IES application.

Preliminary eligibility check questions:
   1. Is your domiciliary of origin outside the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland?
   2. Do you have a conditional or final offer from an eligible Irish higher education institution, as a full-time, non-EU paying student?
   3. Have you already received a Government of Ireland International Education Scholarship?

4. Candidate profile
Applicants are expected to demonstrate:
    • a record of outstanding academic achievement;
    • excellent communication skills;
    • participation in extracurricular activities, for example, humanitarian work, politics, arts or sport;
    • a strong rationale for pursuing their study in Ireland that indicates how a GOI-IES would align with their longer-term goals.

5. Evaluation criteria (marking scheme — application must cover):
    • Academic qualifications, achievements and work experience (40 marks).
    • Personal statement (45 marks) — benefit of becoming a scholar (15), how they will extend beyond their studies into Irish society (15), long-term interest in Ireland (15).
    • Two references uploaded via the online application portal. (15 marks). The HEA will not accept references submitted outside of the online application portal. Guidance for referees is available in Appendix 2.
The references must be dated at most a year prior to the 2026 call closing date (12 March 2026).
The application must be the applicant's own work. It is prohibited to have the application drafted by third persons or to include AI-generated content.

6. Assessment and award process
... Only applications that meet a minimum threshold (60 marks) will be considered for a scholarship. While there is an upper limit of five awards per HEI, the number of awards per HEI can vary ... The top 12 ranked applications will be automatically funded on the basis of excellence, as they represent the top 20% of the 60 awards to be funded.
Final GOI-IES award offers will be subject to admission to the relevant HEI. Should a candidate hold a conditional study offer and fail to meet the conditions specified in this offer, the GOI-IES award offer will be rescinded.

7. Submission of Applications
The deadline for GOI-IES 2026 applications is 5pm (Irish time) on 12 March 2026. Applications will not be accepted after this deadline.
The HEA expects to release the results of GOI-IES 2026 in early June 2026.

APPENDIX 4 — Recommendations for Successful Candidates
Visa applications ... it is possible that visas will not be approved for your family members, who will not be able to accompany you.
Accommodation and living costs: The cost of living in Ireland can be considerably higher than in other countries. The €10,000 stipend will assist with living costs, but it is unlikely to cover them completely. It is advisable to secure other sources of funding ...
Your stipend will be transferred to your host HEI in two €5,000 instalments, one in semester 1 and one in semester 2. The HEI will then transfer the stipend to you, either in the same two instalments or in smaller, monthly payments.
