## PSE EUR Scholarship for PPD Students from Middle/Low-Income Countries (verbatim extracts)

Eligibility:
"The PPD Master's Program will offer two to three scholarships to M1 students who are (exclusively) citizens of a middle or low-income country"

Application Process:
"Eligible candidates can apply to this scholarship when applying to the PPD program using the PSE application site. In the section 'About your Application,' simply mark 'yes' as the answer to the question 'Do you wish to apply to the PSE scholarship?' and upload the required documents"

Required Documents (for the scholarship):
- "A personal statement mentioning your family and you individual resources"
- "Official documents justifying those resources (paychecks, tax statements, former scholarship entitlement, etc.)"

Award Amount:
"The amount is 5,000€ for students entering M1 and 2,500€ for those going into M2."

Duration:
"The scholarship can be maintained for the duration of Master's studies (M1 and M2) under the condition that the M1 is successfully completed within a year."

Document also notes (other funding, for context):
- Eiffel Scholarship: ~1181€ per month, starting in September; PSE application deadline Nov 6th, 2025.
- French Government Grants (BGF): contact the French embassy/consulate in country of origin.

NOTE: This PSE EUR scholarship is a PARTIAL award (€5,000 M1 + €2,500 M2 = €7,500 total over two years), not a full living stipend. It tops up the already-low tuition (~€250/yr + ~€100 CVEC that all students pay). It is NOT fully funded.
