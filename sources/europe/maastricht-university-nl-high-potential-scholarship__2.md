Key verbatim facts confirmed (identical to __1.md):

- "The Maastricht University (UM) NL-High Potential Scholarship programme offers 21 full scholarships, including tuition fee waiver and monthly stipend, each academic year for highly talented students from outside the European Union (EU) who have been admitted to a master's programme at UM."

- Eligibility: "You hold a nationality in a country outside the EU/EEA, Switzerland or Surinam and meet the requirements for obtaining an entry visa and residence permit for the Netherlands."
- "You **do not** have dual nationality from an EU/EEA country or Switzerland."
- "You have applied for admission to a participating full-time master's programme at Maastricht University for the 2026-2027 academic year **before 8 December 2025**."
- "You are not older than 35 years of age on 1 September 2026."
- "You have obtained excellent results during your prior education programmes, as demonstrated by your latest grade transcript showing a GPA of 7.5 out of 10 or higher."
- "Please note, this is a very competitive scholarship, only 2% of the applicants get awarded the scholarship."

- Application deadline: "Applications are closed and will reopen in October 2026."
  "by the Scholarship deadline of 1 February 2026 you must have met all admission requirements"

- Duration: "13 months for a one-year master programme" / "25 months for a two-year master programme"

- Amount table:
  Living expenses € 20,150 (13 months) or € 38,750 (25 months) — € 1,550 per month
  Health & liability insurance € 700
  Visa application costs — At cost
  Tuition fees — At cost
  Pre-Academic Training costs — At cost
  ISAP — At cost for first block only

- "Please note that a mandatory part of the Scholarship is a room that will be allocated to you. Accepting the Scholarship means, you accept the room. The accommodation provided to Scholarship students is not suitable for partners/spouses and/or children... Refusing the room means your Scholarship will be cancelled."
- "Please note that air tickets are not included and must be paid by the candidate."
