## VILNIUS TECH Scholarships

Important Note: Please keep in mind that the application fee is paid once when you apply to a programme, including when applying for a scholarship.

### Tuition-free and Partial Scolarships for Bachelor's Degree Studies at VILNIUS TECH (Academic Year 2026-2027)
VILNIUS TECH offers both tuition-free scholarships and partial scholarships (50% and 25% of the tuition fee).

  * Tuition-Free Scholarships
    Eligibility: Best international students applying to the first year of Bachelor's degree studies at VILNIUS TECH
    Application Deadline: 20th of April, 2026

### Tuition-free and Partial Scolarships for Master's Degree Studies at VILNIUS TECH (Academic Year 2026-2027)
VILNIUS TECH offers both tuition-free scholarships and partial scholarships (50% and 25% of the tuition fee).

  * Tuition-Free Scholarships
    Eligibility: Outstanding international students applying for the first year of Master's degree studies at VILNIUS TECH
    Application Deadline: 20th of April, 2026
    **Selection Criteria:**
    * Previous academic achievements in Bachelor's degree studies (minimum 80% of the maximum GPA).
    * Motivation letter highlighting personal qualities, assets, liabilities, significant achievements, and goals at VILNIUS TECH (1000-4000 symbols).
    * 2 References or letters of recommendation from previous professors (academic).
    * Knowledge of English language (minimum level B2).
    * Extracurricular activities related to the chosen program at VILNIUS TECH.

  * Partial Scholarships
    Eligibility: International students applying for the first year of Master's degree studies at VILNIUS TECH
    Application Deadline: 20th of April, 2026
    **Selection Criteria:**
    * Previous academic achievements in Bachelor's degree studies (minimum 70% of the maximum GPA).
    * Motivation letter highlighting personal qualities, assets, liabilities, significant achievements, and goals at VILNIUS TECH (1000-4000 symbols).
    * 2 References or letters of recommendation from previous professors (academic).
    * Knowledge of English language (minimum level B2).
    * Extracurricular activities related to the chosen program at VILNIUS TECH.

  * Application Process
    * All applicants must submit their applications with education documents online https://apply.vilniustech.lt/ for the chosen study program.
    * Choose the "Scholarship" application and complete it.
    * Applicants meeting the selection criteria will be invited to record an interview after submitting the application.

  * Additional Points
    * Applicants who completed the Bachelor's degree in the last three years will receive an additional point.
    * Scholarships are awarded for the full study period if the student has no academic debts.

**Important Note:** Incomplete applications or those without an application fee payment will not be considered for a scholarship. Applications submitted after 20th of April, 2026, or without proper registration will not be considered for a scholarship at VILNIUS TECH.

### State Scholarships (Lithuanian state — Education Exchanges Support Foundation)
Call for Applications for Full-time Master's Degree Studies at Lithuanian Higher Education institutions.
Funding under the Call will be provided to the citizens of Armenia, Australia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, China (People's Republic of China and Taiwan), Georgia, Israel, Japan, Kazakhstan, Moldova, New Zealand, South Korea, Ukraine, The United States of America, Uzbekistan and foreign citizens of Lithuanian origin of non-EU and non-EFTA countries, 70 grants in total (monthly scholarships and grants covering the study cost).
A monthly scholarship is equal to 560 EUR. The grant covering the study cost is limited to and cannot be higher than the national standard study cost.
[NOTE: Bangladesh is NOT in the State Scholarship country list — this sub-program is NOT Bangladesh-eligible. Only the VILNIUS TECH university tuition scholarship is relevant for a Bangladeshi.]
