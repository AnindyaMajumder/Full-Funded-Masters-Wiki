# Tuition fees (UNIGE)
Source: https://www.unige.ch/immatriculations/en/informations/fees
Accessed: 2026-06-18

## Amount of fees
Fees amount to **CHF 500 per semester** (CHF 65 in fixed fees and CHF 435 in administrative fees), payable in a single installment.
  * Any reductions you are entitled to by your status (cf. statut de l'université, art. 77, al. 2), e.g., leave of absence, internship, doctoral studies will be automatically applied to the amount due;
  * Requests for fee relief due to financial difficulties must be submitted to the Service social et finances.
