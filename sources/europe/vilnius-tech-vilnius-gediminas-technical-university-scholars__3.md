# VILNIUS TECH — Admission Requirements (crawl)
Source: https://vilniustech.lt/en/international-students/admission-requirements/
Accessed: 2026-06-20

## For Master's degree programmes

  * Academic Qualifications
    A Bachelor's degree or its equivalent in a relevant field with a minimum CGPA of 60%. Please refer to the country-specific requirements for more detailed information.
  * Academic Transcripts
    Applications must include official, legalized English translations of all academic transcripts if they are not issued in English. Unofficial or self-translated copies are not accepted.
  * Document Legalization
    All applications must include official, legalized English translations of academic transcripts if they are not issued in English. Unofficial or self-translated documents will not be accepted.
    Your documents must be:
      * Legalized by a consular office – if they were issued in a country not part of the 1961 Hague Convention.
      * Certified with an Apostille – if they were issued in a country that is part of the 1961 Hague Convention.
  * English Language Proficiency
    International applicants whose native language is not English must provide proof of their English language proficiency. Exceptions are made only for applicants who have completed their previous studies fully in English.
    IELTS academic — 5.5 (no section below 5.5)
    TOEFL — Paper 510 / iBT 70 / CBT 180 (code 6529)
    Duolingo English Test (online) — Minimum score: 95
    (others: FCE/CAE/CPE, LanguageCert B2, TOEIC, PTE 48)
  * Faculty Evaluation
    All applicants must receive acceptance from the Dean's Office of their chosen faculty. This includes a recorded interview, submitted after the application, which is evaluated by the director of the selected study programme.
  * Study Gap
    The gap since the last completed degree should not exceed three years, unless the applicant provides credible certificates of relevant professional work experience.

## For Bachelor's degree programmes (Bangladesh explicitly named — confirms open admission country)
  * Entrance Examinations
    Applicants from secondary or vocational schools in Pakistan, Bangladesh, or Nepal must take the OMPT Mathematics entrance exam. The exam grade replaces the school mathematics grade in the Entrance score and requires at least 60% for university Bachelor's programmes or 50% for professional Bachelor's programmes.
    [NOTE: This Bachelor-level rule explicitly lists Bangladesh among recognized applicant countries, confirming Bangladeshi nationals are admissible as fee-paying international students. The Master's scholarship is open to all such international fee-paying students.]
