# IP Paris PhD Track — application docs & deadlines (source 3)

## Required supporting documents
URL: https://www.ip-paris.fr/sites/default/files/pages/documents/Masters/PhD%20Track%20-%20Application%20Supporting%20documents.pdf
Also: https://www.ip-paris.fr/en/education/useful-information/admissions

Verbatim (from official ip-paris.fr search snippets):

- "Supporting documents (résumé and supporting statement) must be written
  specifically for each application."
- Transcripts and Diplomas: "To register to a M1/PhD Track Year 1 program: upload
  your Bachelor diploma or a certificate of completion or all of your Bachelor
  transcripts or an equivalent diploma (180 ECTS completed). For non-European
  diploma: please also provide a certificate from your university who indicate the
  equivalence of your credits in ECTS (European credits)."
- "An official document explaining the grading and ranking system signed by
  university."
- "Please join a GRE certificate to your application."
- "All supporting documents must be translated either in French or English."
- "A Bachelor's degree (Bac+3 and 180 ECTS) or an international Bachelor's degree is
  required to apply for the PhD Track."
- English proficiency certificate (B2): TOEIC, IELTS, TOEFL, or Cambridge ESOL —
  except native speakers / those who previously studied in English.
- Two referees' e-mail addresses (recommendation letters not mandatory).
- Contact: phd-track@ip-paris.fr

## Deadlines / cycle
URL: https://www.ip-paris.fr/en/education/graduate-programs/phd-track
Application platform: https://admission.ip-paris.fr/

- "For the 2026–2027 academic year, the application period for the PhD Track Program
  runs from October 14, 2025 to January 20, 2026, via the IP Paris online
  application platform."
- Registration deadlines (after admission): "Students enrolled in the engineering
  program at one of the member schools of IP Paris: July 8, 2026. All other admitted
  candidates: July 31, 2026."
