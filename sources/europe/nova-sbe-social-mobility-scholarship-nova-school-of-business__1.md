## Eligibility (verbatim)
"All candidates can apply for this scholarship, regardless of their country of origin."

Need-based: requires demonstrated financial need verified through household income / household tax-return documentation.

## Coverage (verbatim)
"This scholarship can cover 20 %, 50 %, or 100 % of the tuition fee value of the Master's program."

Tuition-fee reduction ONLY — no living stipend, monthly allowance, travel, or accommodation.

Excluded costs (verbatim): "application fee, the first installment of tuition paid as the enrollment fee, the CEMS program, the Double Degree Program, the amount value associated with additional semesters, exchange programs, or Executive Master's programs"

## Combining with other scholarships (verbatim)
"When a student is granted different scholarships within Nova SBE, the funding cannot exceed 70 % of the total amount of the tuition fee for the Master's program, except in cases where the funding comes entirely from a 100 % social mobility scholarship."

## Application timing (verbatim)
"Applications are available for the Fall and Spring Intakes. It is not possible to consider applications for scholarships after submitting the application to the Master's program."

"Scholarships are awarded on a rolling basis to applicants, which means that the budget available for scholarships may be used before the end of the Master's admission cycle."

"we strongly recommend that you apply as early as possible" (limited funding).

## Required documents (verbatim / paraphrase)
Submitted as a single PDF:
- For Portuguese income: "IRS Model 3 concerning the previous fiscal year available at the time of the application, complete and fully identified"
- For other countries/territories: "a household tax return concerning the most recent fiscal year" issued by the official tax authority
- "All documents must be in Portuguese or English" (otherwise certified translation)
- Tax returns from all household members, unless the applicant demonstrates financial independence
