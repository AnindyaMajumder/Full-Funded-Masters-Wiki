# Master's scholarships
## Scholarships for international master's students
Wageningen University aims to attract talented international students to its international classroom by offering several scholarship opportunities. Below you will find an overview of the scholarships available for prospective international MSc students.
  * The scholarship programmes offered by Wageningen University (ABF, ASP, Excellence) are for prospective students only. WU does not offer scholarship programmes for students who are currently enrolled at Wageningen University.
  * The Wageningen University Scholarship programmes are for the MSc September start only. 


## Scholarships based on invitation (academic year 2026-2027)
### Excellence programme
The Excellence Programme is initiated by Wageningen University & Research to attract excellent students for a Master’s programme, and who might have the potential to do a PhD after their MSc graduation in Wageningen or elsewhere.
##### For who
International MSc students from outside the European Economic Area (including Switzerland) starting an MSc programme in September 2026 who have unconditional admission.
##### Components of the scholarship
A tuition fee waiver for the duration of the programme (24 months)
##### Requirements
  1. You are citizen of a country outside the European Economic Area (and Switzerland)
  2. You have submitted a complete application for one of the MSc programmes at Wageningen University & Research before the given deadline. Please see: [How can I apply for the master’s programme?](https://www.wur.nl/en/education/master/application-admission-masters/apply-masters-programme "Apply for a master's programme")
  3. You are unconditionally admitted to this MSc programme
  4. You have a high GPA of 80% or higher. A First Class honours BSc graduation or equivalent is required.


##### MSc application deadline 
1 February 2026. All MSc applications that have been submitted before this deadline will be assessed by the selection committee.
##### Can I apply for this scholarship? 
The selection committee will coordinate the selection and will inform the invited candidates about the next steps in the selection. On 1 April 2026, students selected by the selection committee who are eligible for the Excellence September 2026 scholarship will be notified by email. From this selection, a number of students will be chosen by lot; they will either be offered the scholarship or be placed on the waiting list.
If you do not receive an email on 1 April, you are no longer in consideration for the Excellence September 2026 scholarship.
On 7 May 2026, the result of the selection process will be communicated to all invited candidates. 
### Africa Scholarship Programme (ASP)
The Africa Scholarship Programme (ASP) offers talented and motivated students from Africa the unique opportunity to pursue a Master’s degree at Wageningen University and to support capacity building in Africa.
##### Components of the scholarship
  * Travel to and from The Netherlands
  * Living allowance for the duration of the programme (24 months)
  * Tuition fee coverage for the duration of the programme (24 months)
  * Visa costs and costs for health insurance


##### Requirements
  1. You are a citizen of an African country.
  2. You have submitted a complete application for one of the master’s programmes of Wageningen University & Research before the given deadline.
  3. You are unconditionally admitted to this MSc programme.
  4. You are an excellent student with a First class honours degree and a GPA of 80% or higher in a bachelor's degree.
  5. Preference is given to students who have not obtained a master’s degree yet.


### The Anne van den Ban Fund (ABF)
The Anne van den Ban Fund allows talented students from developing countries to study at Wageningen University. The scholarships are assigned to students in close cooperation with the university and are combined with the WU Fellowship Programme.
##### For who
International students from developing countries starting an MSc programme in September 2026 who have unconditional admission.
##### Scholarship amount 
Full and partial scholarships.
##### MSc application deadline 
1 February 2026.

### NL Scholarship
The NL Scholarship is financed by the Dutch Ministry of Education, Culture and Science and several Dutch research universities and universities of applied sciences. This scholarship is meant for international students from one of the selected countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA) who wish to pursue a master’s degree in the Netherlands.
Available scholarships: 8 Scholarships.
##### Components of the scholarship
If you are selected as a beneficiary of the NL Scholarship you will receive a one-time payment of € 5.000 in the first year of your studies.
Requirements
  1. You are an excellent student with a GPA of 80% or higher
  2. You have one of the following nationalities: Argentina, Australia, Brazil, Canada, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Cuba, Ecuador, Guatemala, Honduras, India, Japan, Malaysia, Mexico, New Zealand, Peru, Singapore, South Africa, South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand, Turkey, United Kingdom, United States of America or Vietnam.
