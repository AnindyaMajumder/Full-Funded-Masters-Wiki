**Call for application 2026 is open.**

The Eiffel Excellence Scholarship Program was established by the French Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs to enable French higher education institutions to attract top foreign students to enroll in their masters and PhD programs.

It gives opportunity to the future foreign decision-makers of the private and public sectors, in priority areas of study, and encourages applicants from foreign countries up to 29 years old at master's level, and applicants up to 35 years old at PhD level.

## 2026 Campaign Timeline
  * Opening of the call for applications: **October 1, 2025**
  * Deadline for the reception of applications by Campus France: **January 8, 2026 included**.
  * Publication of results: **from the 30th of March, 2026**.

# Call for France Excellence Eiffel applications 2026

Source: https://www.campusfrance.org/en/actu/call-for-france-excellence-eiffel-applications-2026
Accessed: 2026-06-06
Published on: 01/10/2025

The call for applications for the 2026 campaign of the France Excellence Eiffel scholarship program is open until January 8, 2026.

The call for applications for the France Excellence Eiffel Scholarship program is open until January 8, 2026, and is exclusively for French higher education institutions.

## Fields of Study for Master's and Doctoral Levels
Two major disciplinary fields are covered by the France Excellence Eiffel scholarships, encompassing the following 7 fields of study for both the Master's and Doctoral components.

### For Science and Technology
  * Biology and Health
  * Ecological Transition
  * Mathematics and Digital Sciences
  * Engineering Sciences

### For Humanities and Social Sciences
  * History, French Language and Civilization
  * Law and Political Science
  * Economics and Management

> Applications must be submitted solely by the higher education institutions, based on the excellence of the international students they wish to welcome into their programs.

The France Excellence Eiffel Scholarship program has been developed by the French Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs to enable French higher education institutions to attract the best international students to master's and doctoral degree programs.

The management of the France Excellence Eiffel Scholarship program is entrusted to Campus France.
