ART. 1 – NATURE, AMOUNT AND DURATION
Ca’ Foscari University of Venice establishes the award of up to 28 scholarships for high-achieving international
students enrolling in bachelor’s and master’s degree programmes in the 2026/2027 academic year:

    For the candidates of the first and second admission rounds:1
    20 scholarships of 10,000 € per year for up to two years for students enrolling in a master’s degree
    programme;
    8 scholarships of 10,000 € per year for up to three years for students enrolling in a bachelor’s degree
    programme.

All amounts indicated are subject to taxation by the Italian state.
Awardees are not exempt from paying the tuition fees they owe upon enrolment.

Should the number of suitable applicants be fewer than the total amount of scholarships, the university
reserves the right to assign fewer than twenty-eight (28) scholarships.
The term “international students” describes all applicants who, regardless of their nationality, possess valid
academic qualifications granting access to a bachelor’s or master’s degree programme in the Italian university
system obtained from either a non-Italian school/university or an Italian institution located abroad.
Scholarships cannot be assigned to students enrolling in joint or double bachelor’s or master’s degree
programmes for which the tuition fee is due to a university other than Ca’ Foscari University of Venice.



ART. 2 – INCOMPATIBILITY
The scholarship is incompatible with any other scholarship awarded by the Italian Government or by Italian
public institutions, including the need-based scholarship for student financial aid awarded by the Veneto
Region. Candidates are required to ascertain any incompatibility between this and other scholarships by
contacting the International Office / Outreach & Recruitment Unit at prospective.students@unive.it.

ART. 8 – PAYMENT OF THE AWARD
The awarding of scholarships requires that a scholarship recipient obtain his or her entry qualification before
the end of the enrolment period of the 2026/2027 academic year.
The scholarships are awarded for enrolment in the degree programme indicated by the successful applicant
on his or her application and, if required, in the video presentation.
Awardees will retain their scholarship in the event that they modify their chosen degree programme during
the enrolment phase (bachelor’s and master’s degrees at Ca’ Foscari University of Venice only).

The scholarship will be accredited in two instalments per year for each year of the chosen degree programme.
Each instalment is equal to half of the annual amount of the awarded scholarship package and will be
accredited exclusively to the scholarship recipient’s CartaConto (the multi-function card issued to all newly
enrolled students that provides access to all of the University’s services and is usable as a pre-paid card).

The first instalment will be paid out after the scholarship recipient successfully completes the enrolment
procedure and no later than 28 February 2027. Scholarship recipients must carry out all of the procedures that
are necessary to receive the scholarship instalment(s) on their CartaConto by 31 January 2027, under penalty
of losing their right to receive the scholarship.
The second instalment will be paid out only if the scholarship recipient has earned at least 24 ECTS associated
with the study plan of the first year (officially appearing on his/her transcript of records) AND has a minimum
weighted average of 26 out of 30 by 10 August 2027. If the student fails in either respect, the second
instalment will not be paid out.

ART. 9 – REVOCATION OF THE SCHOLARSHIP
The scholarship will be revoked if:
    • the student withdraws from his/her bachelor’s or master’s degree studies at Ca’ Foscari;
    • the student chooses to transfer to another university;
    • the student cannot meet the requirements to receive the second instalment of the scholarship during
       the first year;
    • a bachelor’s student fails to enrol in the second or the third year of his or her studies;
    • a master’s degree student fails to enrol in the second year of his or her studies.

The students whose scholarship has been revoked must return the instalment(s) already received.
Until they do so, their academic career will be blocked, and they will be unable to take or register exams, fill
out their yearly study plan, transfer to another degree programme or to another university, etc.



ART. 10 – RENEWAL OF THE SCHOLARSHIP

                                       BACHELOR’S DEGREE STUDENTS
The scholarship will be automatically renewed if awardees successfully enrol in the 2027/2028 academic year
and have obtained at least 48 ECTS associated with the study plan of the first year by 30 September 2027 with
a weighted average of at least 26 out of 30.
The scholarship will be disbursed in two equal instalments:
    • the first one will be transferred upon confirmation of the renewal by no later than 31 October 2027;
    • the second one will be transferred upon registration of at least 24 ECTS associated with the study plan
        of the second year by 10 August 2028 at the latest with a weighted average of at least 26 out of 30.

The scholarship will be automatically renewed for the third year of study if the recipient enrols to the
2028/2029 academic year and has officially earned at least 96 ECTS pertaining to the courses of the first two
years of the relevant study plan by 30 September 2028 with a weighted average of at least 26 out of 30.

    • The first one will be transferred upon confirmation of the renewal by no later than 31 October 2028;

ART. 11 – RENOUNCEMENT
A successful applicant’s renouncement of the scholarship he or she has been awarded must be communicated
via email by writing to prospective.students@unive.it. The scholarship will then be assigned to the next

absence of the funds provided by the scholarship.

For the purposes mentioned above, in addition to specifically authorized employees and University collaborators,
