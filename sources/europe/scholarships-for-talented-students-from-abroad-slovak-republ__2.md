# Scholarships for Talented Students from Abroad — application portal & deadline notes
Accessed: 2026-06-20

## Official application portal
https://scholarships.portalvs.sk/  — this is the dedicated official application portal named on the
SAIA studyinslovakia page. When crawled (2026-06-20) it returned a BitNinja CAPTCHA / IP-block page
(bot protection), so its inner text could not be machine-extracted. WebFetch also returned HTTP 403.
crawlOk=false for this specific URL. The portal's existence and role are confirmed verbatim on the
official SAIA page (source __1): "The website through which to apply for the scholarship ... :
https://scholarships.portalvs.sk/" and "More information available at: www.scholarships.portalvs.sk".

## Deadline / cycle status (official)
On the official SAIA page (studyinslovakia.saia.sk, source __1, accessed 2026-06-20):
- "The scholarship programme is designed for international students who plan to pursue higher
  education in Slovakia in the academic year 2026/2027."
- "The Ministry of Education, Research, Development and Youth of the Slovak Republic will soon launch
  the application process for a scholarship scheme for international students."
- "We recommend regularly checking our website, where the launch of the application process will be
  announced soon."
- "The criterion for selection will be the results in the SAT test. The latest possible dates for
  taking the SAT test are 2 May 2026 and 6 June 2026, while registration for the SAT test must be
  completed via the official website within the deadlines set by the test provider."

=> As of 2026-06-20 the official SAIA page does NOT publish an explicit application open/close date for
the 2026/2027 cohort of THIS scheme; it states the process "will soon launch" and gives only the SAT
test sit-by dates (2 May 2026, 6 June 2026). Third-party aggregators report an application window of
"23 March – 25 May 2026", but this is NOT stated on the official SAIA page and is therefore not used
as a verified official deadline.

## NOT this program (disambiguation)
The same official page separately lists "Scholarships of the Government of the Slovak Republic for full
Bachelor and Master and PhD degree programmes" (deadline 25 May, via www.vladnestipendia.sk), which is
restricted to an Official Development Assistance (ODA) country list that does NOT include Bangladesh.
That ODA-restricted government scheme is a DIFFERENT program and is not the one documented here.
The National Scholarship Programme (NSP, www.scholarships.sk) is yet another, separate program that
funds only short-term mobility stays (1–2 semesters), NOT full-degree master's studies, and explicitly
does NOT support "international students ... accepted for full degree higher education ... in Slovakia."
