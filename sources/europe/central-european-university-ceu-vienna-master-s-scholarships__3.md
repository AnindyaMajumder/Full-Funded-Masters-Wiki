## Eligibility Requirements for Master's Programs at CEU
You must have a bachelor's degree (or equivalent) from a recognized university or institution of higher education or provide documentation indicating that you will earn such a degree before enrollment in a CEU master's program.
Applicants applying for two-year master's programs must have collected a minimum of 180 ECTS credits (or equivalent) during their undergraduate studies, while applicants for one-year master's programs, a minimum of 240 ECTS credits (or equivalent).

## Master's Degree at CEU - Application Deadlines
Admission to CEU's master's degree programs for the academic year 2026/2027 is organized into four rounds.
- Institutional (CEU) financial aid awards will be made in the first two rounds only.
- candidates from visa-sensitive countries (= countries with a very long visa/residence permit application process) can apply in the first round only, while self-financing EU candidates can do so in all four rounds.

### Admission Round 1
- Deadline: October 15, 2025 (23:59, Central European Time)
- Admission decisions: December 1, 2025 – January 15, 2026
- Application for CEU financial aid: open
- Applicable to: all applicants

### Admission Round 2
- Deadline: February 4, 2026 (23:59, Central European Time)
- Admission decisions: April 1 - 30, 2026
- Application for CEU financial aid: open
- Applicable to: all applicants except those from visa-sensitive countries

### Admission Round 3
- Deadline: April 15, 2026. Application for CEU financial aid: closed.

### Admission Round 4
- Deadline: July 15, 2026. Application for CEU financial aid: closed. Applicable to all applicants with visa-free entry to Austria, incl. EU/EEA.

## General Admission Process
The final admissions decisions are made by the departmental/program selection committees. In addition to assessing the submitted application documents, most departments interview pre-selected candidates prior to making the final decision.

## Documents you need to submit
1. Academic Records — complete official bachelor's transcript and bachelor's diploma in English.
2. Proof of English Proficiency — TOEFL/IELTS etc. Exemption if first language is English, or if you obtained/will obtain a Bachelor's/Master's taught exclusively in English (upload official letter from university confirming English-medium instruction).
3. Standard Test Scores and Other Supporting Documents — some Economics programs require GRE or GMAT.
4. Curriculum Vitae or Resume.
5. Academic Writing Submission(s) — statement of purpose, research proposal, academic essay, writing sample, etc.
6. Letters of Recommendation — two confidential letters of recommendation submitted by a faculty member or job supervisor, written in English and sent via the online application system by the recommenders themselves.

NOTE: Applicants are expected to prepare all written materials independently, without the use of generative AI tools.

## Application fee
CEU charges an application fee of EUR 30 for each candidate irrespective of the number of programs applied to in the given admissions cycle. The application fee is non-refundable and cannot be waived. Applicants to Erasmus Mundus programs (MESPOM, Mundus MAPP) do not have to pay the application fee unless they also apply for other CEU programs.
You may apply to up to 5 programs.
