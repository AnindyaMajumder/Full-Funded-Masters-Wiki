# Key dates for master's applications and enrolment (Chalmers)
## Submit your application
### 16 October 2025 - 15 January 2026
The application period for master's degree programmes is open for national and international students. Create your account and choose programmes at universityadmissions.se or antagning.se (application deadline at midnight CET).

## Apply for a scholarship
### Late November 2025 - Mid January 2026
An application for scholarships administered by Chalmers is a separate application process. Take note, the Swedish Institute (SI) scholarships for Global Professionals are not administered by Chalmers.

## Deadline for paying the application fee or prove fee-exempt status
### 2 February 2026
The deadline for paying the application fee or to document your citizenship of an EU/EEA country. If required, pay the application fee to universityadmissions.se. This is a non-refundable administrative fee. Note that one or the other is required in order for your application to be processed.

## Deadline for all supporting documentation, to complete your application
### 2 February 2026
No documents should be sent directly to Chalmers University of Technology. The list of required documents is specific to Chalmers and applies to all applicants including those with a Swedish personal identity number and/or Swedish citizenship.

## Publication of the official Notification of selection results
### 26 March 2026

## Scholarship award notifications
### Mid-March to late April 2026
The Scholarship Award Notifications will be sent out (by e-mail) to all grant recipients. In order to keep your scholarship offer, you have to confirm that you accept the offered scholarship. All applicants will receive an email with information regardless of whether or not they have been awarded a scholarship. This applies to scholarships administered by Chalmers University of Technology only (the SI scholarships are NOT administered by Chalmers).

## The deadline for paying the first semester's tuition fees (for fee-paying students)
### 1 May 2026
These must have been received by Chalmers by this date (payment for the first out of four semesters).

## First day of the autumn semester
### 31 August 2026
