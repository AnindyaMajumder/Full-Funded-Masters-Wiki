# Scholarships for prospective Master’s students
Every year, Uppsala University offers a limited number of scholarships to international fee-paying Master’s students. These scholarships cover the full cost of tuition, but not living expenses, and are awarded on a competitive basis.

### Autumn 2026 Scholarship Results
The scholarship results for the autumn semester 2026 have now been announced. All applicants have received an email with their results. If you have applied but cannot find the email, please check your spam folder.
