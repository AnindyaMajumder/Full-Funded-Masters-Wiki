# International students: Application for Scholarship of excellence 2025-2026
Source: https://www.ens-lyon.fr/en/article/studies/international-students-application-scholarship-excellence-2025-2026
Accessed: 2026-06-18

Mon, 02/12/2024
Tag : Call for applications

ENS de Lyon offers international students masters excellence scholarships. **The call is open from November 12, 2024 to January 31, 2025.**

ENS de Lyon and its partners offer scholarships for excellent international students to enrol in its Master's programs. in the Exact Sciences, the Arts, and the Human & Social Sciences.

**The Ampère Scholarships of Excellence** of the ENS de Lyon provide students with the opportunity to enrol in one of the ENS de Lyon Masters programs in the Exact Sciences, the Arts or the Human and Social Sciences.

The financial support is 1,000 Euros per month for 12 months.

ENS de Lyon offers a GRADUATE + Scholarship for the best international students selected for a Master’s degree in some Graduate initiatives (CORE, DynLifeSys, MathInfi, PACE) and Master’s programmes, either in M1 or M2

The financial support is 1,000 Euros per month for 10 months (or in addition to another scholarship to reach a maximum of 1000 €/month).

**> >> Closing date for application is January 31 2025 (23:59 CET)**
