Title line: "World Bank Group Classified Low and Lower-Middle Income Countries FY 26"

Bangladesh appears in the eligible-country list (line 5, first column):
" Bangladesh                         Mauritania"

This is the Trinity College Dublin HEI-specific eligibility list for GOI-IES. Bangladesh
is explicitly included, confirming Bangladesh-eligibility even under Trinity's stricter
World Bank income-classification gate. (The program-wide HEA criterion is broader — any
domicile of origin outside the EU/EEA, Switzerland and the UK, excluding only Russian and
Belarusian citizens — so Bangladesh qualifies program-wide regardless of HEI.)
