# Aalborg University — Finance and fees (Danish Government Scholarship)
Source: https://www.en.aau.dk/education/apply/master/finance-and-fees
Accessed: 2026-06-18

## Application fees
You must pay the application fee if you are a NON EU/EEA citizen, unless you hold a residence permit that exempts you from paying application fee. See the exemptions explained below.
Please note that EU/EEA citizens are **not** required to pay application fee.

### Payment instructions
The application fee is 150 EUR (1150 DKK). One application fee covers all applications (max 3) to Aalborg University for the specific intake.
We will refund the application fee if you are admitted and begin your studies at Aalborg University.

### Tuition fees
Students from outside EU/EEA countries are required to pay tuition fees in order to study at Aalborg University unless you hold one of the residence permits mentioned below.
Please note that students from EU/EEA countries are **not** required to pay tuition fees.

**Tuition fee rates for one semester 2026** range from 27.800 DKK / 3725 EUR (humanities/business) to 57.850 DKK / 7745 EUR (engineering/science) per semester.

## Scholarships
**Who can get a scholarship?** Each September intake, Aalborg University grants a limited number of scholarships to individually selected applicants from non-EU/EEA countries based on strategic considerations and academic qualifications. Academic qualifications transcend national borders and are essentially global. The scholarships are financed by the Danish Government and awarded by Aalborg University.

All non-EU applicants are automatically considered when applying to a master's programme. Therefore, it is not possible to apply for a scholarship separately.

Please note, Aalborg University only grants a limited number of scholarships - **approximately 15 each year**. We do not advise you to rely solely on receiving a scholarship when applying for a master's degree at Aalborg University.

**What is included in a scholarship?** A scholarship consists of a tuition fee waiver and a monthly stipend aimed at covering living expenses during the four semesters of your master's programme. The stipend rate for new students in 2026 is **11,225 DKK / 1503 EUR per month**. Please note that you might be required to pay tax.

Be aware that the scholarship might not cover all your living expenses in Denmark. You may need to supplement your monthly stipend with your own savings, a student loan from your home country, or a student job.

The stipend is paid for four semesters, which is the standard duration of a master's degree. If your studies are prolonged, you will not receive further stipends beyond the first two years of study.

**When will the scholarships be granted?** We will inform all applicants about the scholarship decision by the beginning of July at the latest, though it may be earlier. If you receive a scholarship, you will be informed about the details in a separate letter in the Application Portal.

We cannot guarantee that the scholarship decision will be made before the deadline for paying tuition fees. If you are required to pay a tuition fee and are subsequently granted a scholarship, your tuition fee will be refunded.

Please note, if you are granted a scholarship, you might not receive the stipend from the 1 September, as you must have a Danish CPR-number, a NemKonto, and be registered with the Danish tax authorities before we can pay the stipend. In that case, the stipend will be paid retroactively.
