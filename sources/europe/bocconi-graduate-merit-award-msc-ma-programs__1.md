#  Bocconi Graduate merit Awards a.y. 2026-27

|  **Key information**  |
| --- |
|  **100% waiver** on the Bocconi ordinary academic tuition and fees  |

Bocconi University is committed to making our superior level of higher education available to outstanding students. For the provision of these Awards, Bocconi University commits its own resources as well as generous donations from private **donors**, Alumni, individuals and foundations. Merit Awards will be made available for first year Graduate applicants for the 2026-2027 academic year based on their academic standing.

The Merit Award includes a **full tuition and fees waiver**.

The provisions below constitute official publication of the regulations a.y. 2026-2027 of the "Bocconi Graduate Merit Award" benefit for students enrolled in the first year of a MSc program in the a.y. 2026-2027.

**1. Eligible students**

Academic profiles considered to be amongst the top applicants (International, Italian and Bocconi) will be considered for a Merit Award based on the admission's application submitted to Bocconi University. **A separate application will not be required**.

The Bocconi Merit Award evaluation will primarily be based on the candidate's academic achievement. In addition to academic achievement, the complete dossier provided to the Admissions office will be evaluated.

The Selection of awardees will be carried out by a Committee, nominated by the Rector, including the Dean of the Graduate School and the Dean for International Affairs, whose decisions about numbers, amounts and recipients is final and unquestionable.

The number of awards to applicants will not be equally divided amongst the different rounds. The majority of awards will be allocated - for each category of students (International, Italian, and Bocconi candidates) - in the first relevant rounds: the final percentage of awarding for each round is at the discretion of the Committee.

2. Awarding

The assignment of an award will be **communicated** to awarded students **together with admission results.**

Please note that the assignment of the Merit Award is tied to the admission round and the program of admission with which it was communicated.

The Merit Award will not be maintained in the following cases:
  * **Change of round for the same program** ...
  * **Change of program** ...

**ATTENTION!** Only in case you are admitted to two different programs in two different rounds, both with a Merit Award, you will be able to choose **one** program (and the related scholarship)...

3. Cyber Partnership scholarship

Students admitted to the MSc in Cyber Risk Strategy and Governance, Joint Degree with Politecnico di Milano, may be awarded the **Cyber Partnership Scholarship**, which is equivalent to a Bocconi Graduate Merit Award.

4. Acceptance

Awarded students will need to follow the procedure indicated in the admission results and therefore **complete the online scholarship acceptance procedure within the enrollment deadline.**

Students will have to upload a copy of their updated CV that might be shared with the Fundraising Organizational Unit... During the academic year, the Merit Award, indeed, might be converted (by the end of September 2026) into a named scholarship.

It is specified that the assigned benefit will be revoked to students enrolled to the first year who are still enrolled with **reserve** by and no later than September 30, 2026 (with the exception of Bocconi and Italian Candidates who enroll for the first year of the a.y. 2026-2027, who will have to graduate by October 30, 2026).

5. Renewal

The Merit Award will be automatically renewed for the entire legal duration of the regular course of study if the following requirements are met:
— **enrollment requirements**: completion of the enrollment in each academic year within the deadlines foreseen by Academic Services;
— **merit requirements**: the second year (AY 2027-2028) provided the awardee earns **at least 50 credits as of 10 September 2027**.

The Merit Award cannot be renewed for fuori corso year/s.

The renewal of the award is also subject to the payment of tuition and fees at Bocconi. ... students enrolled in the LSE-Bocconi Double Degree program (PPA), Sciences Po-Bocconi Double Degree program (PPA) and Yale-Bocconi Double Degree program, awardees during their first year a.y. 2026-2027, will not renew the Merit Award for their second year (a.y. 2027-2028).

Please note that bonus credits ARE NOT applicable to the Merit Award.

6. revocation

The award will be revoked in the following situations:
• acceptance and/or award of an equal or larger benefit in Bocconi for the same academic year...
• verifications carried out by the Fees, Funding and Housing Office revealing **false declarations**...
• **disciplinary sanctions** imposed by Bocconi University...
• failure to lift the **reserve** on enrollment for the first year of the a.y. 2026-2027 by the final deadline of September 30th, 2026...

Moreover, students who withdraw from studies or transfer to another university during the course of the academic year will forfeit their Merit Award benefit...
