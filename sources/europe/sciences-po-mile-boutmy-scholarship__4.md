# Tuition fees for Academic year 2026-2027

Excluding specific programmes under agreements and One Year Master Programmes that have fixed tuition fees, tuition fees charged depend on the tax residence on which the student depends. Nationality is not taken into consideration in the tuition fees calculation.

## Graduate students — First enrolment

### Fiscal residence in the European Economic Area (European Union Country + Lichtenstein, Norway, Iceland)
Tuition fees are calculated according to the resources of the tax household on which the student depends and from €0 to €20,640.

### Fiscal residence outside the European Economic Area
Tuition fees are €20,640 per academic year.

(Note: The Émile Boutmy Master's scholarship grants a €18,500 exemption per academic year for each of the two years. Full non-EEA Master's tuition is €20,640/year for 2026-2027, so the maximum Boutmy award does not cover the full tuition and provides no living stipend.)

## Undergraduate students — First enrolment
### Fiscal residence outside the European Economic Area
Tuition fees are €14,900 per academic year.
