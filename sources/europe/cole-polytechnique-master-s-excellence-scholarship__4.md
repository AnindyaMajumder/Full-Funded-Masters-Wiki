Master of Science & Technology
# Language & Academic Requirements

## Language Requirements
### English
**Applicants must demonstrate full English-language proficiency.** The tests that we accept are the International English Language Testing System (IELTS), the Internet-based Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL ibt), and the following Cambridge General English Tests: First (FCE), Advanced (CAE), and Proficiency (CPE). Test scores must be less than two years old at the time of application deadline.
The minimum scores required are:
TOEFL iBT ......................................min. 95 overall score
IELTS (Academic)…………….……min. 7.0 overall score
Cambridge Advanced Exam (CAE) .………………..C1 Level (no expiry)
You must upload a copy of your IELTS, TOEFL, or Cambridge test results in the online application system. Do not send your scores electronically through ETS or the British Council.
You can submit your application without your English language test result. However, please note that any offer will be conditional on your providing your English language test results at the required level.
Important: The English test may be waived only if: English is your native language (Passport or National Identity Card required) or if you have earned your last degree from an institution in which ALL your instruction was conducted in English. Your last degree certificate must state the official language of the degree program, or it must be mentioned in the transcript of records or its supplement (official attestation from your university).

### French
**No level of French is required to be admitted in the Master of Science and Technology Programs.**

## Academic Requirements
Our MSc&T programs are open to high-level candidates applying for the first (M1) or second year (M2) of a master's degree:
### M1 Candidate Profile:
  * Hold a bachelor's degree of at least three years, having earned 180 ECTS with a strong background in science and/or engineering.
  * Candidates can apply in the same year they obtain their degree.
  * English proficiency: IELTS (academic) 7, TOEFL iBT 95, Cambridge Advanced Exam C1.
  * French proficiency is not required.
### M2 Candidate Profile:
  * Hold a master's degree or an M1 with a strong background in science and/or engineering.
  * Candidates can apply in the same year they obtain their degree.
  * English proficiency: IELTS (academic) 7, TOEFL iBT 95, Cambridge Advanced Exam C1.
  * French proficiency is not required.
  * Entry into the second year of the Master's program is not available for programs in partnership with HEC.
