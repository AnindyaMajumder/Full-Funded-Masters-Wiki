# MSc & PhD
The Fisheries Training Programme offers scholarships to former fellows.
Fellows must have completed the six-month training programme in Iceland and have been accepted into a postgraduate programme at an Icelandic university.
To date the Fisheries Training Programme has provided over 40 former fellows with an opportunity to advance their education at Master's (21) and Doctoral (23) study levels.
For further information see our list of scholarship recipients.
