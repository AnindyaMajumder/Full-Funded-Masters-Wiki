# Application and Scholarship
With the TUM Deutschlandstipendium, you receive 300 euros per month as well as contact to
industry and cooperation partners of TUM. The decisive factor is your individual contribution -
and not just your academic performance.

TUM awards around 800 Deutschlandstipendium scholarships each year.

**Application deadlines for the Germany Scholarship in the 2026/2027 academic year:
For university students and those who got their university entrance qualification before 2026:
May 18-31, 2026 – _expired_
For high school graduates of the class of 2026: July 13-26, 2026**

If you are accepted into the Deutschlandstipendien program, you will receive 300 euros per
month. Half of the amount is financed by the federal government, the other half of is financed
by a sponsor from industry or the private sector.

### Who can apply?
If you are enrolled in a degree program at TUM and have a TUM matriculation number, you can
apply. You can also apply for the Deutschlandstipendium as soon as you have submitted your
application for a degree program at TUM and have received an applicant number.
You **cannot** be accepted into the program if you have exceeded the standard period of study,
are already receiving performance-based funding of €30 or more per month or are doing a
doctorate at TUM. Also excluded are exchange students who are enrolled at their home university.

### Are international students who are new to TUM also eligible to apply?
Yes, the prerequisite is admission or enrollment at TUM. The basis for selection is the grade of
your last degree.
If you receive an offer for a Deutschlandstipendium from us and would like to accept it, you
must be a **german resident** and have a **german bank account**. If you are unable to provide
proof of these requirements, the offer of funding will lapse. Therefore, please take care of
these requirements and, if necessary, a visa in good time.

### Minimum grade
First-year students: Minimum university entrance qualification grade of 2.40
Enrolled students: Grade point average of at least 2.40

### What should be considered regarding all documents?
  * All documents must be translated into either German or English.
  * Please upload the documents as PDFs.
  * Only upload the evidence relevant to each point.

### The cover letter
  * Length: max. one to one and a half pages
  * Content: Explain why you should receive the scholarship.
  * Language: German or English

### The curriculum vitae
  * Curriculum vitae in tabular form, 1 to max. 2 pages in German or English
  * chronological order

### Proof of performance
Bachelor applicants enter university entrance qualification grade (or uni-assist grade if
obtained abroad). Master applicants state Bachelor's grade (or current transcript GPA / uni-assist
grade if not from EU/EEA/Switzerland).

### Documents for special circumstances (optional, with proof)
Confirmation of voluntary work/commitment; medical certificates; child rearing; care of a
relative; orphanhood; experience of war or flight; migration background (passport/ID/birth
certificate); employment contracts; prizes/awards.

### Evaluation
Academic performance (60%), commitment (20%), special circumstances (15%), employment and
awards (5%). The selection committee reserves the right to invite individual applicants to
personal interviews. Selection committee meets in September; offers of admission sent from
October; first payment by mid-December at the latest. To accept, you must provide your tax ID
("Steuer-ID").

### Tax / combining
The Deutschlandstipendium is tax-free. You can receive BAföG and the Deutschlandstipendium at
the same time. You may not receive other talent/performance-based funding averaging €30/month
or more. Funding for a doctorate is not possible with a Deutschlandstipendium.
