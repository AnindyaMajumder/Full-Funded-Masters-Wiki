## Graduate Scholarship
Admission to the graduate school guarantees automatic registration to scholarship without any additional process
**Government Scholarship**
A student receiving scholarship benefits funded by the Korean government.
### Available Departments
  * Physics and Chemistry
  * Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
  * Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering
  * Energy Science and Engineering
  * Brain Sciences
  * New Biology
  * Artificial Intelligence Major in Interdisciplinary Studies
  * Artificial Intelligence
  * Biomedical Science&Engineering
  * Quantum Information Science


### Full tuition coverage
Please confirm with the department, as tuition fees vary by department and major.
### Stipend provided
  * Master’s program : A minimum of **9.6 million KRW** annually plus additional incentives
  * PhD program : A minimum of **14.04 million KRW** annually plus additional incentives


The amount of additional incentives will vary depending on research performance
### Housing
  * On-campus housing (Biseul Village) is available for DGIST international students (single room, double room and married student apartment)
  * Single room : 220,000 KRW per month, double room: 170,000 KRW per month, married student apartment : 290,000 KRW per month


The above price is subject to change depending on the academic year
**DGIST Scholarship**
A student receiving scholarship benefits funded by DGIST funds, external donations, or research grants.
Please check with the department or (prospective) academic supervisor whether you are eligible for the DGIST Scholarship.
### Available Departments
  * Interdisciplinary Engineering Major in Interdisciplinary Studies
  * Artificial Intelligence
  * Biomedical Science&Engineering
  * Quantum Information Science
  * Graduate School of Technology and Innovation Management Department of Technology and Innovation Management


### Tuition payment required
Please confirm with the department, as tuition fees vary by department and major.
### Stipend provided
An additional stipend equal to the tuition fee will be provided.
  * Master’s program : A minimum of **15 million KRW** annually plus additional incentives
  * PhD program : A minimum of **22 million KRW** annually plus additional incentives


The amount of additional incentives will vary depending on research performance
### Housing
  * On-campus housing (Biseul Village) is available for DGIST international students (single room, double room and married student apartment)
  * Single room : 220,000 KRW per month, double room: 170,000 KRW per month, married student apartment : 290,000 KRW per month


The above price is subject to change depending on the academic year
**General Scholarship (Currently Employed)**
A student planning to study at DGIST while employed by an organization (or a company)
### Available Departments
  * Physics and Chemistry
  * Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
  * Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering
  * Energy Science and Engineering
  * Brain Sciences
  * New Biology
  * Artificial Intelligence Major in Interdisciplinary Studies
  * Artificial Intelligence
  * Biomedical Science&Engineering
  * Quantum Information Science
  * Graduate School of Engineering Pratice, Advanced Technology
  * Graduate School of Technology and Innovation Management Department of Technology and Innovation Management


### The applicant, or his/her affiliated organization (or company), is required to pay the tuition fee.
Please confirm with the department, as tuition fees vary by department and major.
### Non-provision of student stipend
### Housing
  * On-campus housing (Biseul Village) is available for DGIST international students (single room, double room and married student apartment)
  * Single room : 220,000 KRW per month, double room: 170,000 KRW per month, married student apartment : 290,000 KRW per month


The above price is subject to change depending on the academic year
