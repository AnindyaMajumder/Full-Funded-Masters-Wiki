#  Graduate Scholarship for Excellent Foreign Students (GSFS)
![](https://oia.snu.ac.kr/theme/basic/img/scholaship.jpg)
### Graduate Scholarship for Excellent Foreign Students (GSFS)
#### Eligibility
Newly-admitted graduates from universities in East, Southeast, and Central Asia(i.e., students in China and Japan are not eligible) and it is aimed at students who applied designated departments of SNU.
#### Details of the Award
  * Application Period : During the admission application period for international students every semester
  * Number of Recipients : 20 new graduate students
  * Amount and Duration of Award

  
GSFS  
| Tuition  | 4 semesters maximumnote  | Full Tuition Fee  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| Living Expenses  | 500,000 KRWnote monthly  |  
Please note that the amount of living expenses and the duration of award may vary depending on the individual colleges.
The scholarship is renewable up to a maximum four(4) semesters, contingent upon the recipient maintaining a minimum term GPA of 3.0.
Scholarship recipients must have a cumulative 3.0 GPA for graduates at the end of each semester to continue eligibility.
#### Required Documents
An applicant should apply **online** during the designated application period. 
#### Contact Information
  * Tel: 82-2-880-2519
  * E-mail: intlscholarship@snu.ac.kr
