#### Types of Scholarships and Eligibility Criteria
##### 1. Tuition Support Scholarships
**National Scholarship**  
National Scholarship  
| Scholarship Types  | Support Details  | Eligibility Requirements  | Additional Information  |  
| --- | --- | --- | --- |  
| Presidential Science Scholarship National Excellence Scholarship (STEM)  | Full tuition  | 
  * Completion of at least 12 credits
  * Cumulative percentile of 87 (3.0 GPA) or higher

 | Academic incentive funds and living expenses are separate.  |  
| National Scholarship Type I  | Full or partial tuition  | 
  * Within the 8th income bracket for financial aid
  * Completion of at least 12 credits
  * Cumulative percentile of 80 (2.3 GPA) or higher

 | Support is tiered based on the income bracket.  |  
| National Scholarship Type II  | University's own effort linkage  | Full or partial tuition  | Based on the university’s own criteria  |   
 |  
| Regional Talent Scholarship  | 
  * Within the 8th income bracket for financial aid

##### 3. External Scholarships
External scholarships vary by foundation/organization in terms of eligibility, support amount, selection, and payment criteria, and they may change each year. Please contact the relevant department for more information.
  * Open Selection: External scholarship selection guidelines are posted on the POVIS → Community → Student Notice Board & Scholarship Board.
  * School-Nominated Selection: When external scholarship organizations request the university to nominate students, the Student Affairs Team selects the scholarship recipients based on the organization's criteria.


##### 4. Honorary Scholarship Program
The Honorary Scholarship Program is a system where students receiving tuition support from the University Funds donate their scholarships to assist other financially struggling students. For information on how to apply and eligibility, please refer to the POVIS Scholarship Board.
