# Chinese Government Scholarship & Shanghai Government Scholarship (SJTU) — pages 60 & 61

## Chinese Government Scholarship — https://global.sjtu.edu.cn/en/study-sjtu/prospective/scholarships/60
Official Website: https://www.campuschina.org/scholarships/index.html

Application Methods — Graduate Programs:
Incoming international graduate students have two options for Chinese Government Scholarship applications: through Shanghai Jiao Tong University (Type B) or through Chinese Embassies/Consulates in their home country (Type A). Applicants may choose only one application channel, as submitting duplicate applications through multiple channels is not permitted.

- Apply for Chinese Government Scholarship through SJTU: When applying at http://apply.sjtu.edu.cn/, applicants should select "Apply for Scholarship through SJTU". Applicants may register on the CSC website (https://studyinchina.csc.edu.cn/#/register), ... please make sure to choose Scholarship Type B, as well as fill in SJTU's agency number "10248" and upload the pre-admission notice of SJTU and other required materials.
- Apply for Scholarships through Chinese Embassies/Consulates in the Home Country: The application schedule varies by country (usually from November to April).

Regarding accommodation, SJTU does not provide free dormitories; however, the university offers an accommodation subsidy based on your scholarship coverage.

## Shanghai Government Scholarship — https://global.sjtu.edu.cn/en/study-sjtu/prospective/scholarships/61
Application Methods — Graduate Programs:
International graduate students who intend to apply for the Shanghai Government Scholarship do not need to apply separately. The scholarship application has been combined with the program application. Please select "Apply for scholarships through SJTU" in the Apply Scholarship section when submitting the "Foreign Students Apply (http://apply.sjtu.edu.cn/)".
