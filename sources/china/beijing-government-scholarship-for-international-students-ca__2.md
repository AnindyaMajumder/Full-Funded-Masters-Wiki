## Eligibility
- non-Chinese citizens holding a foreign passport
- New international students admitted to Beijing Normal University, enrolling in their admission year
- Age: Bachelor's <25, Master's <35, Doctoral <40, Non-degree <45
- in good health both mentally and physically
- Cannot simultaneously receive other Chinese government or organizational scholarships

## Coverage
- A: Tuition, accommodation (dorms or subsidies), living allowances, medical insurance
- B: Tuition, accommodation (dorms or subsidies), medical insurance
- C: Tuition, medical insurance

## Duration
- Undergraduate/Master's/Doctoral: shall not exceed the standard duration of study
- Non-degree: shall not exceed one academic year

## Annual Assessment
- Recipients must participate in the annual assessment each year to maintain eligibility.

## Application Steps
1. Log into the "Beijing Normal University Online Application System" and select this scholarship
2. Mail required hardcopy materials
3. University conducts scholarship review after admission
4. Check results through the online system
