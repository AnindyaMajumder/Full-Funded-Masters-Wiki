2026 Chinese Government Scholarship (University Program) for
International Students at Fudan University

Eligibility
1. Applicants should be non-Chinese citizens and in good health.
2. Applicants for master’s programs should be under the age of 35; applicants for doctoral
   programs should be under the age of 40.
3. Applicants should not be holding any other scholarship provided by Chinese government.

Coverage
Exemption from tuition fee, providing free on-campus accommodation or providing
accommodation subsidy, Comprehensive Medical Insurance
Living stipend (Master student: 3000RMB/month, Doctoral student: 3500RMB/month)

Application Period
From October 13, 2025 to December 10, 2025

How to Apply
Step One:
Visit http://www.campuschina.org to complete the application online. Print and sign the
application form. (Program Category: Type B, Agency No.10246 for Fudan University)
Step Two:
Visit https://istudent.fudan.edu.cn/apply to complete the application for study program. Please
choose “Self-support” as the “fund source”. When filling in the application form, choose the
option of “Chinese Government Scholarship” and upload the scholarship application form.

●Applicants who apply for the scholarships have to upload documents below.
Completely filled Foreigner Physical Examination Form.
The form can be downloaded from DOCUMENTS at https://iso.fudan.edu.cn/isoenglish/, and
should be filled out in Chinese or English，the report is valid for six months.
Applicants currently residing in China may submit Residence Permit or Foreign Permanent
Resident ID Card in substitute for Foreigner Physical Examination Form.

●According to the Chinese Government scholarship application requirements, applicants for
Category B scholarships can only choose one school. Please be sure to select "Fudan University"
in the CSC system to submit the application, and then upload the CSC application form in the
application system of our university.

Result Check
The applicant may check the result by visiting the column “Notices” on ISO website
(https://iso.fudan.edu.cn/isoenglish/).

Notes
1. New scholarship students are required to register at Fudan University on the date specified on
   admission notice. Students who arrive late without approval in advance will have their
   Scholarship automatically cancelled.
2. New scholarship students must participate in annual evaluation. Students who fail to meet the
   requirements will have their scholarship cancelled.
3. New scholarship students are not be allowed to change the major or study period.
4. Scholarships awarded by the Chinese government shall not be subsidized by other
   scholarships (excluding all kinds of one-time awards) established by the Chinese
   governments at all levels and the admitted university at the same time. Once found, the
   Chinese Government Scholarship qualification will be cancelled and the Chinese
   Government Scholarship received should be returned. Those who deliberately conceal their
   financial support are not allowed to apply for Chinese Government Scholarships within three
   years besides disqualification.
