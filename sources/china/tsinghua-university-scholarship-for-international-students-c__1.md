- 2026 Chinese Government Scholarship
  In order to strengthen mutual understanding and friendship between the Chinese and people around the world and promote China’s exchange and cooperation with other countries in various fields, the Chinese government sets up scholarships for talented students and scholars to pursue higher education or conduct research in Chinese universities.

  Link: https://yz.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/info/1027/1477.htm

- 2024 The Atomic Energy Scholarship Program of China (AES), jointly initiated by CAEA and MOE.

- Full Scholarships for Certain Programs
  Programs that provide full scholarships include Schwarzman Scholars Program, International Master's Program in Nuclear Engineering and Management, International Master's Program in Public Health, Master of Public Administration in International Development and Governance Program, International Master of Public Administration Program, International Master of Public Administration in the Belt and Road Initiative Program, International Master of Public Administration for Future Leaders Program, etc.
  Link: https://yz.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/info/1027/1119.htm

- 2024 Tuition Scholarships
  Tuition Scholarships, for example, the Beijing Government Scholarship for International Students (BGS) and the Tsinghua University Tuition Scholarship, cover full or partial tuition fees with the duration of one academic year. Applicants may apply for Tuition Scholarships annually (competitive).
  Link: https://yz.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/info/1027/1118.htm
