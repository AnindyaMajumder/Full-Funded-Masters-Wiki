Peking University Scholarship for International Students
Peking University Scholarship for International Students is a financial aid scholarship established by Peking University in 2012. It aims to attract more outstanding international students to pursue undergraduate, master's, and Ph.D. degrees at Peking University.
I. Scope of the Scholarship
It covers tuition, a living stipend and medical insurance.
II. Scholarship Duration
Undergraduate degree: 4 years
Master’s degree: 2 - 3 years
Doctoral degree: 4 years
III. Eligibility
1. Meet the pertinent admission requirements for international students of Peking University.
2. Have successfully submitted an application for full-time self-funded programs for international students of Peking University.
3. Have not received any other forms of scholarship funding.
IV. Application Time
Generally in January and March each year.
V. Selection Process
1. Applicants submit an online scholarship application at http://www.studyatpku.com .
2. Following the evaluation of the applications by the scholarship review committee of Peking University, notifications will be sent by email to the successful recipients.
Note: New applicants for undergraduate programs do not need to submit a separate scholarship application. Peking University will award merit-based scholarships to the admitted students. Please keep an open communication channel to ensure timely delivery of incoming email notifications.
VI. Annual Review
Scholarship is renewable on an annual basis provided scholarship recipients have participated in and passed the annual scholarship review.
