  * Students applying for SJTU scholarships can click the option “Apply for scholarship” when completing the online application form. Excellent students will be awarded the scholarships in the following form.

  
| Classes  | Scholarship  | Criteria  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| First-Class Scholarship  | Period：4 years Covers tuition, living allowance (2,500 CNY per month), accommodation subsidy, insurance  | Applicants should be officially admitted by SJTU and not be awardees of other scholarships at the same time Scholarship decision is based on the comprehensive ranking of applicants’ both academic and interview performance. Scholarship results will be announced together with the admission results.SJTU reserves the right of final explanation.  |  
| Second-Class Scholarship  | Period：4 years Covers tuition, accommodation subsidy (1,100 CNY per month), insurance  |  
| Third-Class Scholarship  | Period：4 years Covers accommodation subsidy (1,100 CNY per month), insurance  |

Graduate Programs 
SJTU scholarships are available for international doctoral programs, including Monthly stipend, tuition waiver, group comprehensive insurance in China, and accommodation subsidy (covering partial accommodation expenses).
The International Master's program offers Master’s SJTU scholarship and tuition waiver scholarship. Master’s SJTU Scholarship includes Monthly stipend, standard tuition waiver, group comprehensive insurance in China, and accommodation subsidy (covering partial accommodation expenses). Tuition Waiver Scholarships include standard tuition waiver and group comprehensive insurance in China. In addition, some schools have school/college scholarships, please refer to the respective school/college prospectus or contact the school/college for details.
For detailed information, please refer to the scholarship instruction when filling in the "Foreign Students Apply (http://apply.sjtu.edu.cn/)", or, contact us through email ( gs.admission@sjtu.edu.cn) .
