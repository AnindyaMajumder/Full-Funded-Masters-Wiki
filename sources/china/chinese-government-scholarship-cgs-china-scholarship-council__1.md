# Application Guide for Chinese Government Scholarship
Source: https://www.campuschina.org/content/details3_74776.html
Date:2018.01.01 From:China Scholarship Council

In order to strengthen mutual understanding and friendship between the Chinese and people around the world and promote China’s exchange and cooperation with other countries in various fields, the Chinese government sets up scholarships for talented students and scholars to pursue higher education or conduct research in Chinese universities.

The China Scholarship Council (hereinafter referred to as “CSC”), entrusted by China’s Ministry of Education, is responsible for the admission, enrollment and administration of Chinese Government Scholarship recipients.

**I. Scholarship Categories and Application**
**(I) Bilateral Program** — open to undergraduates, postgraduates, general and senior scholars. An applicant shall file an application with the dispatching department of their home countries.
**(II) University Program** — designed to finance talented international students directly recommended by designated Chinese universities. An applicant shall file an application with Chinese universities undertaking this program.
**(III) Designated Scholarship Programs** — set up per cooperation agreements between China and international organizations.
**(IV) Student Exchange Program**

**III. Scholarship Coverage**
1. Tuition fees;
2. Free university dormitory or accommodation subsidy;
3. Stipend:
Undergraduates: CNY 2,500 per month;
Master’s students/general scholars: CNY 3,000 per month;
Doctoral students/senior scholars: CNY 3,500 per month.
4. Comprehensive medical insurance:
CNY 800 per person per year;
CNY 400 per person (for programs lasting less than six months)

**IV. Eligibility**
1. be a citizen of a country other than the PRC and in good health both mentally and physically;
3. be an academically excellent holder of a Bachelor’s degree or its equivalent under the age of 35 when applying for a Master’s program;
10. have passed the HSK (Level 4) when applying for a Chinese-taught master's or doctoral program;
11. meet the admission requirements of the university of preference in terms of language proficiency and academic ability, among others, when applying for a program taught in a language other than Chinese.

**V. Application Process**
1. Identify the preferred scholarship program, designated university and major;
2. Reach the designated university for admission documents;
3. Reach an application receiving agency for a recommendation;
4. Fill in and submit the Application Form for Chinese Government Scholarship online via the Chinese Government Scholarship Information System;
5. The application receiving agency shall submit the recommended candidates list and their application documents;
6. The CSC will organize designated universities and experts to examine applicants’ eligibility and academic performance before finalizing the short-list candidates;
7. The CSC will notify the admission results to the application receiving agency.

**VI. Application Documents**
1. Application Form for Chinese Government Scholarship (filled in online);
2. Clear scanned copy of the information page of ordinary passport (validity ≥ 12 months from expected start date of study);
3. Diploma and certificate of highest education (notarized photocopy). Current students submit certificate of expected graduation or student status;
4. Academic records (notarized Chinese/English translations if in other languages);
5. Language qualification certificates. Master's applicants taught in Chinese must submit a valid HSK score report; programs taught in other languages require relevant language proficiency certificates such as IELTS or TOEFL per university requirements;
6. CSCA Score Report (bachelor's degree applicants only);
7. Pre-Admission Documents — all applicants must provide a pre-admission document issued by the universities applying (pre-admission notice or invitation letter from schools or professors);
8. A study or research plan (no less than 1000 words/characters for postgraduates);
9. Recommendation letters — applicant for a postgraduate program shall submit two recommendation letters in Chinese or English from professors or associate professors;
11. Applicant under the age of 18 shall submit legal documents of legal guardian(s) in China;
12. Applicant to study in China for more than six months shall submit a photocopy of the Foreigner Physical Examination Form;
13. A valid Certificate of Non-Criminal Record (or Police Clearance Certificate) issued by local public security, usually within six months prior to submission.
