Application Guide to the Shanghai Government Scholarship for International Students (2026 Intake)
Release Time: 2025-12-22 / 2025-12-10

The Shanghai Municipal People's Government established the Shanghai Government Scholarship for International Students (SGS) in 2006.

I. Scholarship Categories and Coverage
The Shanghai Government Scholarship is divided into Type A and Type B.
Type A (Full Scholarship): Type A covers tuition, accommodation, living allowance, and comprehensive medical insurance for the approved duration of study. It is designed to support outstanding international students applying for undergraduate, master's, and doctoral degree programs.
Type B (Partial Scholarship): Type B covers tuition and comprehensive medical insurance for the approved duration of study. It is intended for excellent international students applying for undergraduate, master's, and doctoral degree programs.

II. Eligibility Criteria
Nationality and Health: Applicants must be non-Chinese citizens and be in good physical and mental health.
Age and Educational Background:
- Undergraduate programs: high school diploma or above and be under 25 years old.
- Master's programs: Applicants must hold a bachelor's degree or above and be under 35 years old.
- Doctoral programs: master's degree or above, strong academic research ability, under 40 years old.
- Pre-college programs: high school diploma, under 23 years old, hold a pre-admission letter.
Academic Performance: Applicants must demonstrate outstanding academic performance and strong study potential.
Scholarship Status: Applicants who are currently receiving any other scholarships funded by the Chinese government or other organizations are not eligible to apply for SGS.

III. Application Procedures
Application Period: December 1, 2025 – April 30, 2026
Application Process:
- Applicants shall complete the Shanghai Government Scholarship Application Form for International Students.
- The completed and signed form must be uploaded to the online application system when submitting the application through Shanghai Maritime University's international student application portal: https://gjy.shmtu.edu.cn/login

IV. Scholarship Management and Evaluation
Shanghai Maritime University will conduct an annual comprehensive evaluation of scholarship recipients based on their academic performance, learning attitude, attendance, and overall conduct. Students who pass the evaluation will continue to receive the scholarship for the following academic year. Students who fail the evaluation may have their scholarship suspended or terminated for the subsequent year.
