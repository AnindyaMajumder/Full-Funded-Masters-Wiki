# The Belt and Road School Scholarship — Beijing Normal University

Your location： Home - English - Scholarship - BNU Scholarships - The Belt and Road School Scholarship

The Belt and Road School scholarship are divided into full scholarship and partial scholarship. Full scholarship includes tuition, accommodation allowance, living stipend and comprehensive medical insurance. Partial scholarship includes tuition, living stipend and comprehensive medical insurance.

**Attention: The Belt and Road School scholarship is only for prospective students of The Belt and Road School. For details about the scholarship, please contact The Belt and Road School.**
