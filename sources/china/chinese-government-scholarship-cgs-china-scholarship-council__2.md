# 2026/2027 Chinese Government Scholarship (CGS) Application Now Open
Source: https://fm.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/xwdt/202510/t20251021_11737164.htm
(Ministry of Foreign Affairs / Chinese Embassy announcement, dated 2025-10-21)

Title: "2026/2027 Chinese Government Scholarship（CGS）Application Now Open！"

Application Deadline: "Please submit online no later than February 24, 2026."

Academic Year/Cycle: "2026/2027 Chinese Government Scholarship"

Application platform: applicants apply online via the Chinese Government Scholarship
Information System at http://www.campuschina.org / https://studyinchina.csc.edu.cn/

Note: CGS deadlines are set by each application receiving agency (embassy / dispatching
authority) and by each designated university and therefore vary by country and institution.
The CSC online system typically opens December–April each year. The February 24, 2026
date above is the deadline published in this embassy announcement for the Bilateral Program
route for the 2026/2027 cycle. Other agencies publish earlier dates (e.g. Indonesia
Feb 8, 2026; some universities Dec 31, 2025 / Feb 15, 2026).
