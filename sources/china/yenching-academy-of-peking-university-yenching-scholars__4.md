#### Application Instructions
##### Application Instructions for International Applicants, and Applicants from the Chinese Regions of Hong Kong, Macao & Taiwan (HMT)
发布时间：2023-11-27 10:02

# Timeline
We accept applications for the 2024 cohort of Yenching Scholars starting **August 15**, 2023. The general application deadline for international applicants is at **12:00 noon on December 3**, 2023 (Beijing time). For applicants from the Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan regions, the application deadline is at **5 pm on December 29**, 2023 (Beijing time).
Candidates must submit all materials, including letters of recommendation, before 12:00 noon (Beijing time) on the deadline in order for the application to be considered as valid.

## Eligibility
Applicants should have:
■ Minimum of a Bachelor's degree in any field, or be currently enrolled in a Bachelor degree program and be on track to graduate no later than August 31 of the year in which they wish to enroll*;
■ An outstanding academic record;
■ Strong interest in interdisciplinary study of China;
■ A record of extracurricular achievement, community engagement, social responsibility, and leadership potential;
■ English proficiency.
We do not have a strict age cut-off for applicants, but in general, no person over the age of 28 has been admitted to the program. The average age of students entering the program is 23.

## Partner and Cooperating Universities
The Yenching Academy works with **Partner Universities** that conduct internal application processes and nominate candidates for consideration. **All** candidates who are students or alumni of Partner Universities are required to apply first through the university's internal process; nominees should then apply through the Yenching Academy admissions portal. Note that we grant Partner University nominees an additional week to complete their online applications (deadline at 12:00 noon Beijing time on **December 10**, 2023).

## Application Components
Each applicant must submit the following materials in English:
■ Completed online application form
■ Personal statement (750 words or less)
■ Statement of Research Interest (maximum 1500 words, excluding citations)
■ Curriculum vitae (maximum two pages)
■ Official transcript(s)
■ Diploma(s), Certificate(s) of Enrollment, and Overseas Credential Evaluation
- An Overseas Credential Evaluation are required only for offer holders, and are only required for undergraduate degree programs.
■ Two academic letters of recommendation (English or Chinese)
- Peking University will only accept recommendation letters written by **full professors or associate professors** (or equivalent titles).
■ English proficiency test score:
- IELTS (Academic): Overall 7.0
- TOEFL (iBT): Overall 100
- Cambridge English Scale (Advanced): Overall 185
- CET 6: Overall 600
- TEM 4: Pass level of "良好" or above
International applicants whose native language is English, or have graduated from an English-taught degree program (undergraduate or above), are not required to provide an English proficiency test report.

## Health History
According to Peking University admission guidelines, candidates with certain medical conditions are not eligible for admissions. During the application process, applicants are not required to inform us with your health status. However, please kindly be informed that you will be asked to provide a health history form after you have been offered a place at the Yenching Academy.

## Application Review
International finalists can be interviewed online or in person at Peking University. Chinese and HMT candidates must attend interviews in person.
Shortlisted candidates will be invited for an interview from December to March.

## Acceptance Procedures
Candidates who receive an offer from the Yenching Academy need to go through the following procedures before they are officially admitted to Peking University:
Step 1: Completion of an online registration with the Peking University Office of International Relations (for international candidates only), and final round of application revision by the Graduate School of Peking University.
Step 2: Upon approval from the Graduate School, public announcement of admission (公示) need to be conducted for 10 working days for public supervision. This will be available on the Yenching Academy website around late May.
Step 3: If there is no objection against admission from the public, the official admission letter from Peking University will be sent out by the end of June of the enrollment year.
