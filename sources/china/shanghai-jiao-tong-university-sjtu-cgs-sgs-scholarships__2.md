2026 SJTU International Graduate Admissions Guide

SJTU ... currently ranked 47th globally in the QS World University Rankings 2026.

The typical duration of master's programs is two to three years, while doctoral programs generally take three to four years to complete.

## ELIGIBILITY
- Applicants should be non-Chinese citizens with a valid private ordinary passport.
- Applicants should be in good physical and mental health, and meet the medical examination requirements of both the country and the university. Applicants should not have criminal record and maintain a friendly attitude towards China.
- Master's degree program applicants should have a Bachelor's degree or an equivalent degree (or above) accepted in China.
- The applicant's Chinese or English proficiency must satisfy the relevant requirements specified in the Language Requirement for SJTU International Graduate Programs.

## MATERIALS TO PREPARE
1. Degree Certificates: scanned copies of degree certificates. Applicants for master's programs must submit a bachelor's degree certificate. ... All admitted students must obtain a valid degree or an official Certificate of Accreditation and Evaluation issued by the CSCSE before online registration.
2. Transcripts: scanned copies of your transcripts. Applicants for the Master's program are required to provide their undergraduate transcripts.
3. Proof of language proficiency.
4. Scanned copy of passport: clear scanned copy of the photo page of their private ordinary passports. If the validity period is less than 12 months, renew the passport.
5. ID Photo: JPG format, less than 200 KB, with a minimum resolution of 295x413.
6. Personal Statement and Study Plan.
7. Two Letters of Academic Recommendation: from associate professors or above, or senior professionals in a related academic field.
8. Supervisor Acceptance Letter (for specific schools/programs): Applicants to listed schools must contact a potential supervisor in advance and obtain an acceptance letter. Master's Programs of: School of Mechanical Engineering, School of Computer Science, School of Integrated Circuits, School of Materials Science and Engineering, School of Mathematical Sciences, School of Life Sciences and Biotechnology, School of Biomedical Engineering, School of Agriculture and Biology, School of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Tsung-Dao Lee Institute, and SJTU ParisTech Elite Institute of Technology.

## SCHOLARSHIPS
SJTU offers a range of scholarship opportunities for outstanding international students:
Doctoral Programs: SJTU Fellowship, Chinese Government Scholarship (CGS), Shanghai Government Scholarship (SGS), and SJTU Scholarship.
Master's Programs: CGS, SGS, SJTU Scholarship, SJTU Tuition Waiver Scholarship, and School/College Scholarships.

### Eligibility Requirements
- Applicants must meet the general eligibility criteria for SJTU international graduate programs.
- Applicants must not have received any other forms of scholarship support.
- For doctoral degree applicants: must be under 40 years old.
- For master's degree applicants: must be under 35 years old.

### How to Apply
Applicants may choose to apply through one of two channels: via SJTU or via Chinese Embassies/Consulates. Eligible applicants can only select one of these channels.
1. Apply for Scholarships through SJTU (Type B). The agency code for SJTU is 10248. If an applicant is not recommended for the CGS Scholarship, they will be automatically considered for other available scholarships, including SGS, SJTU, or School/College Scholarships — there is no need to make multiple selections in the system.
2. Apply for Scholarships through Chinese Embassies/Consulates in the Home Country (Type A).

### Scholarship Notice
Applicants can check the scholarship results via the Study@SJTU system. The university will not issue separate written notices for scholarship results.

### Important Notes
- In general, the duration of scholarships cannot be extended. All scholarship recipients are required to participate in the annual scholarship review.
- The SJTU Scholarship, the SJTU Tuition Waiver Scholarship, as well as the School/College Scholarships, may cover either full or Partial tuition fees.
- Regarding accommodation, students may choose to reserve on-campus accommodation or live off-campus. SJTU offers an accommodation subsidy based on your scholarship coverage.
