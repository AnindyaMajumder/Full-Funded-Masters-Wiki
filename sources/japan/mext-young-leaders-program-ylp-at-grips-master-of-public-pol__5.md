### Scholarships / Tuition and Fees
#### Scholarships
We have a number of scholarships for which intending GRIPS international students may apply, and full scholarships are available for outstanding applicants.

#### Scholarships open to application through the sponsoring organizations
##### Master's programs
| Program | Scholarship |
| --- | --- |
| Young Leaders Program (YLP) | Japanese Government – YLP-MEXT Scholarship |

Notes:
  * Applicants who are interested in this type of scholarship program must obtain the application information from the sponsoring organization and submit an application with supporting documents during the set application periods.
  * Contact the relevant sponsoring organization for further details.

### For self-financed applicants
#### Tuition and fees
| Fee type | Cost | Frequency / Due date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Application Fee | 30,000 yen | Once / When requested by GRIPS |
| Admission Fee | 282,000 yen | Once / At the time of enrollment |
| Tuition | 642,960 yen | Yearly / In two installments, due October 31 and April 30 |
Note: tuition and fees are subject to change.

(For YLP MEXT scholars these education fees — entrance examination, matriculation/admission, and tuition — are waived per the MEXT Application Guidelines.)
