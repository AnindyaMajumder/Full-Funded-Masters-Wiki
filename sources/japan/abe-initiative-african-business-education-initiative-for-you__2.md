# Master's Degree and Internship Program of African Business Education Initiative for Youth (ABE Initiative)

Welcome to the portal site of ABE Initiative for Tunisian candidates! This page provides key details about the 2026 (13th Batch) ABE Initiative program (mostly departing in September 2026)

### 1. What is ABE Initiative?
A scholarship program for _young African professionals_ to pursue a Master's degree in Japan and gain internship experience with Japanese companies, aiming to support industrial development in Africa.
