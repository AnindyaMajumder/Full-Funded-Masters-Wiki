## Programme Overview
"The WCO established the Japan-WCO Human Resource Development Programme (Scholarship Programme) in 2000, funded by Japan Customs."

## Public Finance Track (GRIPS)
"The Programme provides Customs officials from developing countries with an opportunity to pursue Master's level studies and training in Customs related fields in the Public Finance programme."

Host: GRIPS (National Graduate Institute for Policy Studies), Tokyo — Master's degree in Public Finance.
Academic portion: "economics-oriented training aimed at developing a broad understanding of the theoretical, empirical and institutional aspects of Customs policy."
Practical training conducted with Japan Customs and the Japan Customs Training Institute.

## Strategic Management and IPR Track (AGU)
"The Programme provides Customs officials from developing countries with an opportunity to pursue Master's level studies and training in Customs related fields in Strategic Management and Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)."
Host: AGU (Aoyama Gakuin University), Tokyo.

## Participation Statistics
"221 Customs officials from 57 Members in all the WCO regions have been awarded the scholarship" (GRIPS Public Finance, by 2021/2022).
"114 Customs officials from 50 Members" participated in the AGU programme (by 2022/2023).
