## Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship Applicant Qualifying Examinations - Subject Test Questions
2014 - 2019 examinations are published as samples of exam questions.

For Research Students, the qualifying examinations published are Japanese exam and English exam (own MEXT-administered written tests). No external IELTS/TOEFL test is listed as required by this program; the embassy-recommendation screening uses MEXT's own Japanese and English examinations.
