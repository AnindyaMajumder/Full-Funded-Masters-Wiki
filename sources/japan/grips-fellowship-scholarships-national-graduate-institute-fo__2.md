### How to Apply
#### Before You Apply
For the details of each program, go to .
For information about funding including scholarships, go to [Scholarships / Tuition and Fees](https://www.grips.ac.jp/en/admissions/expenses/). 
For the details of each scholarship provider, see [Scholarship Eligibility and Details chart](https://www.grips.ac.jp/uploads/admissions/2025/07/GRIPS_Scholarships_2026.pdf).
For deadlines, see [Application Deadlines by Program](https://www.grips.ac.jp/uploads/admissions/2025/07/GRIPS_Application_Deadlines_2026.pdf). 
#### Application Eligibility
**Master's Programs and Five-year Ph.D. Programs**
A bachelor's degree or equivalent
**Three-year Ph.D. Programs**
A master's degree or equivalent
#### Online Application (directly to GRIPS)
**Applications for admission to GRIPS for 2026-2027 have been closed.**
**Applications for admission to GRIPS for 2027-2028 will open in August 2026.**
To apply for any of the programs listed below, download our application guide and forms via the links below.
**Master's Programs**
  * One-year Master's Program of Public Policy (MP1) 
  * Two-year Master's Program of Public Policy (MP2)
  * Macroeconomic Policy Program (MEP) (One year Program or Two year Program) Note: for externally funded or self-financed applicants only
  * Public Finance Program (PF)
  * Economics, Planning and Public Policy Program (EPP)
  * Maritime Safety and Security Policy Program (MSP) Note: for externally funded or self-financed international applicants only


**Ph.D. Programs**
**Five-year Ph.D. Programs**
  * GRIPS Global Governance Program (G-cube)
  * Policy Analysis Program (PA)


**Three-year Ph.D. Program**
  * Science, Technology and Innovation Policy Program (STI) 


**Step 0 Application Guide **
Download our Application Guide.
Read the Application Guide carefully before starting your application.

Notes:
  * If you are a candidate for MP1/2, MEP, PF(Tax), G-cube, PA or STI, you must be instructed by JICA to apply to GRIPS. Please begin your process by contacting the department in your organization responsible for overseas study to confirm that employees from your organization are eligible to apply for the JICA Scholarship. Note: please do not contact the JICA office in your area directly.
  * If you are a candidate for EPP, you must be instructed by JICA to apply to GRIPS. Please begin your process by contacting the person in charge of the Linkage Master's Program at your university in Indonesia for details. Note: please do not contact the JICA office in your area directly.
