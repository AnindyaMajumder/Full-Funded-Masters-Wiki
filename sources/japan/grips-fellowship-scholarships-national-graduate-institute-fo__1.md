#### Scholarships
We have a number of scholarships for which intending GRIPS international students may apply, and full scholarships are available for outstanding applicants. Below is a simplified list of the scholarships that applicants may be matched with. Please review the information below and consult the links provided to learn more about these funding opportunities.

##### Master’s programs  
|  **Program**  |  **Scholarship**  |  
| --- | --- |

|  Public Finance Program (PF) (Customs)  |  Japan-WCO Human Resource Development Programme (Scholarship Programme)  |

Notes:
  * Specific scholarship allocations will be determined by GRIPS, based on the eligibility requirements stipulated by the scholarship providers (see [Scholarship Eligibility and Details chart](https://www.grips.ac.jp/uploads/admissions/2025/07/GRIPS_Scholarships_2026.pdf)). 
  * If you wish to be considered for one of these GRIPS-allocated scholarships, please simply apply to our program. (see ).

  * If you are a candidate for MP1/2, MEP, PF(Tax), G-cube, PA or STI, you must be instructed by JICA to apply to GRIPS. Please begin your process by contacting the department in your organization responsible for overseas study to confirm that employees from your organization are eligible to apply for the JICA Scholarship. Note: please do not contact the JICA office in your area directly.
  * If you are a candidate for EPP, you must be instructed by JICA to apply to GRIPS. Please begin your process by contacting the person in charge of the Linkage Master’s Program at your university in Indonesia for details. Note: please do not contact the JICA office in your area directly.
