# The University of Tokyo Fellowship
Source: https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/prospective-students/fellowship.html
Accessed: 2026-06-06

## 1. Overview
This research grant-in-aid, established in 2004, aims to support outstanding, self-funded international students pursuing degrees at the University and to promote the enrollment of exceptional international students.
Recipients are awarded a monthly research grant-in-aid of 200,000 yen for the standard period of study in their respective graduate programs (up to one year for research students). In the academic year 2025, 44 new recipients were selected, bringing the total number of graduate students receiving the grants to 110. As of March 2026, a total of 952 individuals have been awarded the research grant-in-aid since the inception of the fellowship.
Application Guidelines for The University of Tokyo Fellowship (AY 2026): https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/content/400281411.pdf

## 2. Application
Applications must be submitted through the applicant's affiliated or prospective Graduate School. Applicants should contact the relevant office (e.g., the international office) at their affiliated or prospective Graduate School for detailed application procedures.

# AY 2026 Application Guidelines for The University of Tokyo Fellowship (PDF, extracted)
Source: https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/content/400281411.pdf
Accessed: 2026-06-06

1. Purpose — Research grant-in-aid will be awarded to outstanding, self-financed international students to focus on their academic research obtaining the degree.

2. Definition of Self-financed Overseas Students — students receiving Foreign Students Scholarships and students dispatched by foreign governments are not eligible.

3. Eligibility — Self-financed international students with outstanding performance to be admitted to the graduate schools at UTokyo are eligible. Applicants for exemption of tuition fee will not be excluded. However, if accepted for the grant-in-aid, it is expected to withdraw the application for exemption of tuition fee. It is also not possible to receive the other scholarships designated by the Executive Vice President.

4. Applications — Submit the documents by the specified period to the relevant section of one's affiliated (proposed) Graduate School.

5. Number of Recipients — Confirmed annually. If applicants who hold American or British citizenship ... there are possibilities of being the recipient of KOGA Shinsuke Scholarship.

6. Submission Date of the Documents — The specified period designated by the relevant section of one's affiliated (proposed) Graduate School.

7. Grant-in-Aid — 200,000 yen per month

8. Period of Payment — Until the end of the standard program duration ... (For Research Students, the period of payment is the same as the period of research (maximum 1 year)).

9. Payment of Grants-in-Aid — paid directly into the bank account of the recipient once every three months.

11. Termination — refund all or part of grant-in-aid; (3) when failing to report the outcome of the research at the end of one's academic year.

14. Submission of Reports — The recipient must submit a research progress report, an academic transcript and an advisor's assessment to the relevant section of one's affiliated Graduate School at the end of one's academic year.
