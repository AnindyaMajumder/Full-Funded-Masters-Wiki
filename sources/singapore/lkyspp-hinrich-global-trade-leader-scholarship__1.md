# LKYSPP Master in International Affairs 
In partnership with Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy, we provide two full-tuition scholarships for postgraduate study in international affairs. The aim is to educate current and future generations of leaders to improve the level of governance and prosperity in Asia.

## About the scholarship - Academic Year 2026
In partnership with Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy, we provide two full-tuition scholarships for postgraduate study in international affairs. The aim is to educate current and future generations of leaders to improve the level of governance and prosperity in Asia.
### WHAT
Two full-tuition scholarships
### WHERE
Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy, National University of Singapore, Singapore
### WHEN
Classes begin in August 2026
### ELIGIBILITY
Open to applicants from all countries enrolled in or applying to the program and who:
  * Have at least 3-years’ work experience and display a strong interest in working in global trade
  * Have demonstrated an interest in working in Asia after graduation


### APPLICATION DEADLINE
15 December 2025
Scholarship recipients will get an opportunity to engage with the [Hinrich Foundation Alumni Association (HFAA)](https://www.hinrichfoundation.com/education/alumni) for mentorship, networking and possible career opportunities.
Prospective scholars need to apply directly with the LKYSPP Graduate Programmes Office. Learn more about the program and the admission process [at the university's website.](https://lkyspp.nus.edu.sg/graduate-admissions/application-guide/how-to-apply)
