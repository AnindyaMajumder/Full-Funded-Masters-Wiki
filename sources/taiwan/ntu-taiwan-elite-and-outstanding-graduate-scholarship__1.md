## Important Notes for Receiving Scholarships
First Time Receiving 
  * Recipients have to upload the following documents to the [International Degree Current Students System](https://oiasystem.ntu.edu.tw/ntu_oia_int_student/student/auth):


  1.     1. Personal Information Maintenance:  

      1. Alien Resident(ARC) ,(purpose of residence: study at NTU)
      2. Taiwanese Bank Information (for those who have Scholarship) Postal bank account passbook cover
    2. Scholarship Renewal:  

      1. Tuition payment receipt for the current semester
      2. Taiwan Scholarship Certificate


Steps for Scholarship Extension 
  * The scholarship stipend can only be remitted after the recipient completes the registration procedure before the start of each semester( February and September).
  * Recipients have to upload/update the following documents to the [International Degree Current Students System](https://oiasystem.ntu.edu.tw/ntu_oia_int_student/student/auth):


  1.     1. Personal Information Maintenance:  

      1. Valid Alien Resident(ARC) ,(purpose of residence: study at NTU)
      2. Check your Taiwanese Bank Information/ Postal bank account passbook cover
    2. Scholarship Renewal:  

      1. Previous semester’s transcript. **For graduate students who are working on their thesis and not taking courses for the semester, please submit the [Advisor Evaluation Form](https://oia.ntu.edu.tw/cm/paragraph_file/1/413/20150912101338.doc).
      2. Tuition payment receipt for the new semester.


You may refer to the instructions of how to upload the documents in the [International Degree Current Students System](https://oiasystem.ntu.edu.tw/ntu_oia_int_student/student/auth) [here](https://isss.ntu.edu.tw/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Steps-for-renewal-the-scholarship.pdf).
## Taiwan Government Scholarships
MOE Taiwan Scholarship 
  * The stipend will be remitted by the middle of each month.
  * *Any remaining fees the scholarship recipients must pay themselves.


**【Scholarship Details】**
  * Tuition and miscellaneous fees waived up to NTD 40,000 (exclusive of insurance, internet, and accommodation fees).
  * Monthly Stipend 
    * Graduate: NTD 20,000/month
    * Undergraduate: NTD 15,000/month


**【GPA Standard】**
  * Undergraduate: 2.44
  * Graduate: 3.38 
    * If the recipient fails to pass the grade point standard in the fall semester, the scholarship stipend will be suspended for the following **April**.
    * If the recipient fails to pass the grade point standard in the spring semester, the scholarship stipend will be suspended for the following **November**.
    * If the recipient fails to pass the grade point standard for two consecutive semesters, the scholarship will be revoked.


**【Note】**
  * The scholarship will be revoked if the student pursues studies abroad as an exchange or dual-degree student. Remaining stipends may not be retained or deferred. The student’s scholarship will be suspended if they depart Taiwan.
  * A student who departs Taiwan after the fifteenth day of a month is not required to return the stipend for that month.
