  * **Eligibility:** Full-time PhD students in years 1-4 of their program
  * **Scholarship offer:** Waiver of full tuition including basic credit fee, a monthly stipend of maximum NT$25,000.
  * Nominees do not need to submit any application documents for this scholarship. Potential recipients are to be nominated and to be selected as award recipients by the departments.


  
**Applicable to newly admitted students in the 2024-2025 & 2025-2026 Academic Years**

  * **Eligibility:** Full-time PhD students in years 1-4 of their program
  * **Scholarship offer:** Waiver of full tuition including basic credit fee, a monthly stipend of NT$25,000, and advisor's fund of NT$8,000. 
  * Nominees do not need to submit any application documents for this scholarship. Potential recipients are to be nominated and to be selected as award recipients by the departments.


[**2. NCKU Distinguished International Student Scholarship** ](javascript:void\(0\);)
**Applicable to newly admitted students from the 2026-2027 Academic Year onward**
The NCKU Distinguished International Student Scholarship is offer in accordance with the[National Cheng Kung University Implementation Guidelines for International Student Scholarships (Applicable to new students starting from the 2026-2027 Academic Year onward)](https://www.cc.ncku.edu.tw/rule/content.php?sn=2995).
- Undergraduate Students: Tuition fees waiver, and monthly stipend with amount determined by respective college. 
- Master’s and Doctoral Students: Waiver of full tuition including basic credit fee, and monthly stipend with amount determined by respective college.
**How to apply:** The University International Student Admissions Committee will review the application documents to evaluate the scholarship opportunity for the first academic year. Applicants do not need to submit any additional application documents for this scholarship, and the award information will be mailed along with the admission letter. For renewal applicants, the students have to follow [National Cheng Kung University Implementation Guidelines for International Student Scholarships (Applicable to new students starting from the 2026-2027 Academic Year onward)](https://www.cc.ncku.edu.tw/rule/content.php?sn=2995) and apply again.
**Note:**
1. Students with an employment status on their ARC or a work visa are not eligible to receive any of the above scholarships.
2. Recipients who defer admission will be disqualified from the granted scholarship and reviewed again on the returning semester.
3. Master's and Doctoral Departments applicants: Departmental evaluations must include an interview either in person or via video conferencing.
5. Further regulations about the scholarship, please refer to[ National Cheng Kung University Implementation Guidelines for International Student Scholarships (Applicable to new students starting from the 2026-2027 Academic Year onward)](https://www.cc.ncku.edu.tw/rule/content.php?sn=2995).
**Applicable to newly admitted students in the 2024-2025 & 2025-2026 Academic Years**
The NCKU Distinguished International Student Scholarship follows by the[](https://www.cc.ncku.edu.tw/rule/content.php?sn=2781)[National Cheng Kung University Implementation Guidelines for International Student Scholarships](https://www.cc.ncku.edu.tw/rule/content.php?sn=2781)[, (applicable for enrolling in the 2024 fall semester and after)](https://www.cc.ncku.edu.tw/rule/content.php?sn=2781).
_**For Undergraduate:**_
(1) Tuition fee waive, exclude miscellaneous fees and enrollment fees, and a monthly scholarship of NT$8,000.
(2) Tuition fee waive, exclude miscellaneous fees and enrollment fees
_**For Master's:**_
(1) Basic tuition fee waive, exclude credit fees and enrollment fees, and a monthly scholarship of NT$10,000.
(2) Basic tuition fee waive, exclude credit fees and enrollment fees.
 _**For Doctoral:**_
(1) Basic tuition fee waive, exclude credit fees and enrollment fees, a monthly scholarship of NT$16,000, and NT$4,000 from the recipients’ advisors.
(2) Basic tuition fee waive, exclude credit fees and enrollment fees, and NT$2,000 from the recipients’ advisors.
**How to apply:** The University International Student Admissions Committee will review the application documents to evaluate the scholarship opportunity for the first academic year. Applicants do not need to submit any additional application documents for this scholarship, and the award information will be mailed along with the admission letter. For renewal applicants, the students have to follow [National Cheng Kung University Implementation Guidelines for International Student Scholarships ](https://www.cc.ncku.edu.tw/rule/content.php?sn=2781) and apply again.
**Note:**
1. Students with an employment status on their ARC or a work visa are not eligible to receive any of the above scholarships.
2. Recipients who defer admission will be disqualified from the granted scholarship and reviewed again on the returning semester.
3. Undergraduate scholarship applicants: Departmental evaluations must include a video interview.
4. Graduate and Doctoral Departments applicants: Departmental evaluations must include a video interview. Applicants must have already secured a thesis/dissertation advisor. If, due to special circumstances, a scholarship recipient has not secured an advisor upon enrollment, they must do so no later than the beginning of their second semester to remain eligible for the award.
5. About scholarship termination/loss of eligibility, please refer to[National Cheng Kung University Implementation Guidelines for International Student Scholarships](https://www.cc.ncku.edu.tw/rule/content.php?sn=2781)
[**3. International Merit Students in Mathematics and Science, and Business Achievement Scholarship (Bachelor program applicants only)** ](javascript:void\(0\);)
  1. Based on the NCKU Guidelines for [**NCKU Guidelines for Implementing the International Merit Students in Mathematics and Science, and Business Achievement Scholarship**.](https://www.cc.ncku.edu.tw/rule/content.php?sn=2325)
  2. This applied to foreign students and overseas compatriot students who won awards from an NCKU-designated "overseas mathematics and science, and Business competition" within three years prior to their admission into an NCKU undergraduate program.
  3. The Scholarship program will begin from the month when the recipient enrolls in NCKU and end when the recipient graduates, withdraws from NCKU, is suspended or expelled. The maximum period that any recipient may hold the Scholarship is based on the regulated study years for undergraduate programs. Those who study during their extended years of study are not eligible to apply for the Scholarship.
  4. Scholarship Categories and Amounts for new students, each recipient will receive a monthly scholarship for 12 months with an approved exemption of tuition and miscellaneous fees. Scholarship amounts vary as follows:


**Mathematics and Science**
  * Level 1 (ranked in 1-15): a monthly scholarship of NTD$12,000.
  * Level 2 (ranked in 16-50): a monthly scholarship of NTD$10,000.
  * Level 3 (ranked in 51-75): a monthly scholarship of NTD$8,000.
  * Level 4 (school representative in competition): a montly scholarship of NT$5,000


**Business**
  * Level 1 (ranked in 1-15): a monthly scholarship of NTD$12,000.
  * Level 2 (ranked in 16-30): a monthly scholarship of NTD$10,000.
  * Level 4 (school representative in competition): a montly scholarship of NT$5,000


Note: About continuing student application, scholarship termination/loss of eligibility, please refer to**[NCKU Guidelines for Implementing the International Merit Students in Mathematics and Science, and Business Achievement Scholarship](https://www.cc.ncku.edu.tw/rule/content.php?sn=2325) ([https://shorturl.at/xdpu0](https://www.cc.ncku.edu.tw/rule/content.php?sn=2325))**

[**9. MOFA Taiwan-Europe Semiconductor Scholarship** ](javascript:void\(0\);)
_**1. Eligibility:**_
  * Nationality: Czech Republic, Slovakia, Poland, Lithuania, Kosovo, and Albania. (Note: Applicants who also hold nationality from China, Hong Kong, or Macao are not eligible for this scholarship.)
  * Student Status: Exchange student/visiting student, or degree student. 
  * Departments: Semiconductor-related fields, bachelor year or above.


_**2. Scholarship Content:**_
  * Tuition, Administrative and insurance fee & Monthly Stipend:

  
|  **Recipients**  |  **Tuition, Administrative and insurance fees**  |  **Monthly Stipend**  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
|  Exchange/Visiting Student  |  Up to NTD60,000 /per semester   |  NTD30,000   |  
|  Degree Student (Master)  |  Up to NTD120,000 /per year   |  NTD30,000   |  
|  Degree Student (Ph.D.)  |  Up to NTD120,000 /per year   |  NTD40,000  |  
  * Economy-class airline tickets for each recipient up to NT$100,000 (up to NT$50,000 each way).
  * Scholarship duration: 


(1) Exchange/Visiting Student: For 1 or 2 semesters.   
(2) Master student: up to 2 years.  
(3) Doctoral student: up to 4 years.
  * Other: Scholarship recipients from eligible countries under this program, who are covered by an Executive Yuan-approved project or fall under related agreements with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA), may apply for internships in Taiwan. If approved, they may receive a living allowance for up to 3 months, starting from the month the internship begins, within 6 months after completing their academic program. Monthly living allowance: NT$30,000 for bachelor's and master's students; NT$40,000 for Ph.D. students.


_**3. How to Apply:**_
  * Degree-seeking students must complete the Admission application. Qualified applicants will automatically be nominated by the NCKU Office of International Affairs to MOFA. No further procedures are required. (Contact Email: overseas@ncku.edu.tw) 
  * For Exchange/Visiting Student: Please contact NCKU Office of International Affairs-Study Abroad Division. (Contact Email: em50961@email.ncku.edu.tw) 
  * Scholarship announcements and disbursement schedules are subject to the Office of International Affairs' notifications.
  * Application deadline: open to students enrolling through the **2027 Fall Semester** intake.
