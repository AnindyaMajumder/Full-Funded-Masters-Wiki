# United States of America
## The Rhodes Scholarships for the United States
**The Rhodes Scholarships for the United States cover the 50 states of the US, the District of Columbia, and U.S. territories including Puerto Rico, Virgin Islands (U.S.), American Samoa, Guam and Northern Mariana Islands.**
The Scholarships in the United States are administered by 16 regional selection committees as detailed below, each awarding two Scholarships:
  * **District 1 –** Maine, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Vermont
  * **District 2 –** Connecticut, New Hampshire, New Jersey
  * **District 3 –** New York–South
  * **District 4 –** New York–North, Pennsylvania
  * **District 5 –** Delaware, Maryland/DC
  * **District 6 –** Georgia, North Carolina, South Carolina
  * **District 7 –** Alabama, Florida, Mississippi, Puerto Rico, Virgin Islands (U.S.)
  * **District 8 –** Oklahoma, Texas
  * **District 9 –** Indiana, Kentucky, Virginia
  * **District 10 –** Illinois, Ohio
  * **District 11 –** Michigan, Minnesota, Wisconsin, West Virginia
  * **District 12 –** Arkansas, Iowa, Kansas, Missouri, Tennessee
  * **District 13 –** Colorado, Louisiana, Nebraska, New Mexico, North Dakota, South Dakota, Utah, Wyoming
  * **District 14 –** Alaska, Idaho, Montana, Oregon, Washington
  * **District 15 –** Arizona, California–North, Hawaii, Nevada
  * **District 16 –** American Samoa, California–South, Guam, Northern Mariana Islands


**Opening date: 1 July 2026****Closing date: 23:59, Eastern Time, 7 October 2026**
For full information on the application process for the Rhodes Scholarships for the United States of America, including detailed eligibility criteria, please visit the **[Rhodes Trust Office of the American Secretary website](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/office-of-the-american-secretary/ "Office of the American Secretary").**
As the Rhodes Scholarships are place-based, the eligibility criteria, application requirements and selection process vary slightly depending on which constituency you are applying for. To ensure that you have all of the information you need to apply, please read the [**Information for Candidates**](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/files/usainformationforcandidates/ "USAInformationforCandidates") carefully. You should also check the [**Conditions of Tenure**](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/files/conditions-of-tenure/ "Conditions of Tenure") to ensure that your proposed course of study is covered by the Rhodes Scholarship and share the [**Guidance for Referees**](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/files/usarefereeguidance/ "USARefereeGuidance") with your referees. These documents can all be accessed below.
  * [ Information for Candidates - USA ](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/files/usainformationforcandidates/)
  * [ Guidance for Referees - USA ](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/files/usarefereeguidance/)
  * [ Guidance for Institutional Endorsements ](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/files/usaendorsementguidance/)
  * [ Courses covered by the Rhodes Scholarship ](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/files/conditions-of-tenure/)
