# Scholarship FAQs
Commonly asked questions about the Skoll Scholarship.

## Frequently asked questions

What is the scholarship application timeline?

Apply directly to **Saïd Business School for the Oxford MBA** (https://www.sbs.ox.ac.uk/programmes/mbas/oxford-mba) during stages 1, 2, 3, or 4 of the admissions process. Application deadlines are:
  * **Stage 1:** Monday 1 September 2025
  * **Stage 2:** Wednesday 1 October 2025
  * **Stage 3:** Monday 3 November 2025
  * **Stage 4:** Wednesday 7 January 2026

What does the scholarship cover?

The scholarship is intended to cover the Oxford Saïd MBA course fees. Additional funds are provided as a contribution towards living expenses. In rare cases, partial scholarships may be offered.

How many scholarships are offered every year?

The Skoll Centre typically awards up to four scholarships every year.

Who funds the scholarship?

The Skoll Scholarship is funded by a generous grant from the Skoll Foundation (https://skoll.org/) and is administered by the Skoll Centre, which is based at Saïd Business School, University of Oxford.

Is the scholarship only for the full-time MBA programme?

Yes, it is only for the full-time, one-year MBA programme. It does not provide funding for the part-time EMBA programme or other postgraduate programmes.

What is expected from scholarship recipients?

Upon being offered a Skoll Scholarship, successful candidates will receive a letter of agreement which outlines what is expected during and after the MBA. Examples include acting as ambassadors for the Skoll Centre, supporting classmates with entrepreneurship projects, recruiting future Skoll Scholars, serving on the Skoll Scholar Alumni Board, and responding to media enquiries.

If I do not receive a scholarship, can I still focus my MBA on entrepreneurship for social change?

Of course! Typically, only four students receive scholarships, but countless students are interested in pursuing careers that bring together business and social goals. The Skoll Centre also offers co-curricular programming to support students interested in growing as impact leaders. The only difference between the Skoll Scholars' opportunities and other MBA opportunities is the funding and access to the Skoll Scholar alumni network.

## Eligibility

Am I eligible for a Skoll Scholarship?

To be eligible for the Skoll Scholarship, you must meet the selection criteria (https://www.skollcentre.org/skoll-scholarship).

What do you count as a social venture/initiative?

We define a social venture or initiative as one that combines innovation, resourcefulness, and opportunity to address a critical social and/or environmental challenge. You may have set up a venture as a for-profit or not-for-profit organisation, or you may have initiated and led a programme/project within a larger organisation – in either case, your primary objective is to create sustainable systems change, and you have measured your success towards that objective.

What do you count as an 'impact career professional'?

An impact career professional is someone who has used entrepreneurial approaches to address the same social or environmental issue and can demonstrate they have a clear core thread that unites their work throughout their career. These professionals demonstrate a commitment to social change in a particular area through this thread of work.

Do I need to have three years' experience working on a social venture/initiative or impact career by the time I apply or by the time I start my MBA?

To be eligible for the Skoll Scholarship, candidates must have a minimum of three years of experience building and leading a social venture, with demonstrated evidence of significant social impact.

I'm applying for the 1+1 programme; can I be a Skoll Scholar?

We welcome applicants who intend to join the 1+1 programme (which consists of a one-year Master's degree followed by an MBA). However, please note that the Skoll Scholarship only covers funding for the MBA year of the programme. You may want to consider other scholarships specifically designed for 1+1 students, such as the Pershing Square Scholarship.

Are there any age restrictions to apply for the scholarship?

No, there are no age restrictions to apply for the scholarship.

If you have any further questions which are not addressed here, please contact us at skollscholarship@sbs.ox.ac.uk.
