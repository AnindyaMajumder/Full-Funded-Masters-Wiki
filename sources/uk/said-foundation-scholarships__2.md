# Applying for a Saïd Foundation scholarship
**Applications for 2026/27 Saïd Foundation scholarships are now closed.**
If you have applied for a scholarship you can find our selection timetable [here](https://saidfoundation.org/our-timetable/). You can log on to the [online application system](https://saidfoundation.fullfabric.com/login) to upload evidence of having applied to your chosen courses or any university offers received after the application deadline.
For an overview of our scholarships programme please go to [About our scholarships](https://saidfoundation.org/scholarships/).
In this section you can find guidance on applying for a Saïd Foundation scholarship, including information on:
  * [Eligibility criteria](https://saidfoundation.org/the-eligibility-criteria/)
  * [English language requirement](https://saidfoundation.org/english-language-requirements/)
  * [Required documents](https://saidfoundation.org/required-documents/)


Please read through this information carefully before starting an application to find out if you are eligible and to help you prepare your application. If you have questions after reading through this guidance, you may find the answer in our [Frequently Asked Questions](https://saidfoundation.org/scholarship-faqs/).
