#  Scholarships 
The OICSD offers a range of scholarships associated with various Master’s and DPhil degrees to support graduate students who work on the complex challenges of sustainable development in India.
There is no separate application process for most of these scholarships. Please submit your [application for graduate study](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/applying-to-oxford) by the relevant funding deadline for your course and you will automatically be considered. Please see the [standard scholarship selection terms](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/fees-funding-and-scholarship-search/standard-scholarship-selection-terms) for more information about the application and selection process. You can also use the [fees, funding and scholarship search](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/fees-funding-and-scholarship-search/search) to check for awards offered by the university and other colleges.
If you have any queries, please email student.funding@some.ox.ac.uk
