The University of Kent is offering GREAT Scholarships for the 2026-27 academic year, each worth **£10,000**.
Scholarships are available for students from:
  * **Ghana**
  * **India**
  * **Kenya**
  * **Malaysia**
  * **Nigeria**
  * **Thailand**
  * **Turkey**


The scholarships are available for all full-time postgraduate taught master's programmes.

### Key subjects showcase
  * Kent Business School
Based in the award-winning Sibson building, complete with Bloomberg suite, [Kent Business School](https://www.kent.ac.uk/kbs/) is a ‘Triple Crown’ accredited business school placing them in the top one per cent of business schools globally to be accredited by AMBA, EQUIS and AACSB. Our students learn from our expert teaching staff, many of who appear in the top two per cent of researchers worldwide. Students have the opportunity to transfer their ideas into a business through the Business Start-Up Journey with ASPIRE, and have access to employability support up to three years after graduation at the historic Canterbury Cathedral.
  * School of Psychology
The School of Psychology is in the top 100 Psychology departments in the QS World University Rankings 2026.
The School represents a dynamic and diverse community of psychologists committed to equipping students with the skills, knowledge, and experience to excel in diverse careers. Through cutting-edge research, hands-on learning, and a focus on real-world applications, we prepare graduates to address complex challenges in mental health, human behaviour, and beyond. 
  * Kent Law School
Kent Law School has been ranked 15th in the UK in the Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2026, and 2nd in the UK for research quality in the Complete University Guide 2026. Our postgraduate students come from all over the world to study an LLM at [Kent Law School](https://www.kent.ac.uk/law/postgraduate), a leading UK law school with recognised excellence in teaching, world-class research and [scholarships for international students](https://www.kent.ac.uk/law/postgraduate/postgraduate-funding). Our taught Master’s in Law degrees share an international and contemporary focus. An open choice of modules and pathways enables you to tailor your LLM study to suit your needs and interests.
