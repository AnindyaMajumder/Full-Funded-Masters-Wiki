#  Apply for a scholarship
Application guidance for anyone interested in applying for an on-campus or online with Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at the University of Edinburgh.
###  [ On-campus postgraduate scholarships  ](https://edinburgh-global.ed.ac.uk/mastercard-foundation-scholars-program/apply-for-a-scholarship/on-campus-postgraduate-scholarships)
Aimed at exceptional young leaders who have overcome educational barriers and are committed to on-campus studies with us.
###  [ Climate Leadership programme  ](https://edinburgh-global.ed.ac.uk/mastercard-foundation-scholars-program/apply-for-a-scholarship/climate-leadership-programme)
All Mastercard Foundation Scholars at the University of Edinburgh will participate in a Climate Leadership Programme.
###  [ Online postgraduate scholarships  ](https://edinburgh-global.ed.ac.uk/mastercard-foundation-scholars-program/apply-for-a-scholarship/online-postgraduate-scholarships)
Aimed at exceptional young leaders who have overcome educational barriers and are committed to online studies with us.
###  [ Our scholar community  ](https://edinburgh-global.ed.ac.uk/mastercard-foundation-scholars-program/apply-for-a-scholarship/our-mastercard-foundation-scholar)
We create a network to bring together our scholars past and present to share knowledge, expertise and experiences, seek advice and engage in meaningful conversations with their peers.
###  [ Application timelines  ](https://edinburgh-global.ed.ac.uk/mastercard-foundation-scholars-program/apply-for-a-scholarship/application-timelines)
Application deadlines and timelines for our Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program postgraduate scholarships.
Answers to the most common questions about our programmes, scholarship details and application process.
