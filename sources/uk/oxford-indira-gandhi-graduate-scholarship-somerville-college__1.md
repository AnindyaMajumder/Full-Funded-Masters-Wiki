#  Oxford Indira Gandhi Graduate Scholarship 
This scholarship has been established by Somerville College and the University of Oxford with the generous support of the Government of India.
**Please note that this scholarship has now been awarded for 2026 entry.**
### Eligibility
Candidates must be ordinarily resident in India or have previously studied at an Indian higher education institution. All full-time DPhils are eligible, but the proposed area of study must be relevant to the Oxford India Centre for Sustainable Development’s agenda of interdisciplinary research on sustainable development and India. The core research areas are:
  * Sustainable cities
  * Climate change and clean energy
  * Healthcare innovation
  * Environmental sustainability
  * Food and water security
  * Law, governance and Human Rights


Law, governance and human rights are cross-cutting themes. Independent proposals on any India-related theme which fits within the overall aims of the Oxford India Centre for Sustainable Development will be considered.
### Other criteria
Scholarships will be awarded on the basis of academic merit, relevance of studies to the Oxford India Centre for Sustainable Development’s interdisciplinary aims, understanding of the development challenges faced by India, and leadership and entrepreneurial potential.
### Award levels
The scholarship covers course fees and a grant for living costs. Awards are made for the duration of fee liability for the course.
### Number of awards
Two scholarships are available for 2026 entry.
### Selection process
This scholarship is only tenable at Somerville. All eligible candidates who have applied for graduate study by the relevant funding deadline will be considered, regardless of which college preference is included in the graduate application. Successful candidates will be transferred to Somerville in order to take up the scholarship. Shortlisting and interviews are anticipated to take place in April 2026. 
All DPhil candidates who meet the residency criteria will be emailed at the end of February 2026. They will be invited to complete a short online form to confirm they would like to be considered for the scholarship and that their studies are relevant to the Oxford India Centre for Sustainable Development.
