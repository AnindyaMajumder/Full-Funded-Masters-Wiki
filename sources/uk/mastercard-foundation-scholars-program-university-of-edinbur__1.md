A sustainable future is worth learning for 
From 2023 to 2030, Phase Two of the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at the University of Edinburgh will nurture a community of skilled, entrepreneurial, and justice-oriented climate leaders from across Africa.

###  [ About our partnership with the Mastercard Foundation  ](https://edinburgh-global.ed.ac.uk/mastercard-foundation/about-us/the-mastercard-foundation)
The University of Edinburgh and Mastercard Foundation have been working in partnership since 2016. 
From 2023 to 2030, it will offer 850 postgraduate scholarships for African students.

### 
Impact Report 2016-2023
video 
Phase one of the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program ran from 2016-2023. During that time we:
  * benefited from receiving 240 scholars 
  * our scholars came from 26 African countries 
  * they studied in 13 of our schools


Watch the video to hear our team and scholars talk about their experiences.
