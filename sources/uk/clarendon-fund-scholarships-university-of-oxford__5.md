#  About the Clarendon Fund
Learn about the rich history of Clarendon and how we support over 700 on-course scholars from around 70 different nations.
###  [ History of the Clarendon Fund  ](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/funding/clarendon/about/history)
Clarendon advances the University’s objective and passion for furthering excellence in research, scholarship and education at Oxford. 
###  [ The Fund's purpose  ](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/funding/clarendon/about/purpose)
The Clarendon Fund’s purpose is simple: to assist academically outstanding graduate students through their studies at the University of Oxford.
###  [ Partnership Awards  ](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/funding/clarendon/about/partnership-awards)
Clarendon has many valued partners throughout Oxford and beyond, who through their generous support, enable us to fund approximately 85 additional scholarships each year.
