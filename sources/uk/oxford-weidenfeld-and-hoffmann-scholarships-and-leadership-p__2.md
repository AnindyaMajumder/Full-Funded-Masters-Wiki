### Current Scholarships
In addition to the Oxford-Weidenfeld and Hoffmann Scholarships and Leadership Programme, the Weidenfeld-Hoffmann Trust also has partnerships with **Chevening (funded by the UK Foreign Office)**. The scholarships also benefit from partnerships with multiple colleges where the college contributes a portion of the funding for the Leadership Programme. Successful candidates are transferred to a partner college when taking up the scholarship. Applicants may be considered for more than one scholarship programme. 
A new initiative from 2023-24 -**the Hope Scholarships & Leadership Programme - **will support refugees and those who have been displaced due to conflict, persecution, or other serious human rights violations or deprivations, whether within or outside their country. This scholarship will be subject to the same eligibility criteria as the Oxford-Weidenfeld and Hoffmann Scholarships and Leadership Programme.
### How to Apply?
See the University of Oxford website for full details on eligibility and the application process. Application for the scholarship is made alongside your graduate course application via the University of Oxford. [Please visit the University of Oxford website for full details](http://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/fees-funding-and-scholarship-search/weidenfeld-hoffmann-scholarships-and-leadership-programme/oxford-weidenfeld-and-hoffmann-scholarships-and-leadership-programme).

### Annual Application Cycle
#### September
Applications are open for postgraduate entry to the University of Oxford and for the Weidenfeld-Hoffmann Scholarships. Applications for the scholarship should be submitted online through the [University of Oxford’s website](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/fees-funding-and-scholarship-search/weidenfeld-hoffmann-scholarships-and-leadership-programme). 
#### **November to January**
Applications must be submitted by the January deadline to the University of Oxford for the course to be considered for the Weidenfeld-Hoffmann scholarships. Deadlines vary according to the course - please check your course webpage for details [here](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/courses-a-z-listing?wssl=1). All applications are assessed by the University of Oxford and decisions are made by each department. Only candidates offered a place at the University of Oxford are eligible to be considered for a Weidenfeld-Hoffmann Scholarships and Leadership Programme.
#### March to June
Candidates for the Scholarships and Leadership Programme are contacted by the Weidenfeld-Hoffmann Trust and virtual interviews for shortlisted candidates take place. Assessment is based on the selection criteria [here](https://www.whtrust.org/scholarship/selection). The interview Selection Panel is made up of University academics from partner Colleges and Departments, and WHT staff/advisors and alums. In May, successful applicants are informed by the University of Oxford. Please note Chevening Scholarships are often not awarded until June.
WHT School of Geography Scholars share their advice on applying for a Weidenfeld-Hoffmann Scholarships & Leadership Programme at Oxford University. Apply to join them now!
#### September/October
Shortly before the start of the academic year, the Weidenfeld-Hoffmann Scholars come together as a cohort in Oxford for an intensive few days as an Introduction to the Leadership Programme. This is a compulsory part of the Scholarship award and accommodation/expenses should be covered by the stipend (living allowance).
