## Additional funding for current scholars
If you are a current student receiving support from the Trust, you might be eligible for additional funding for certain specific reasons. This may include:
  * extension funding
  * exceptional funding
  * a grant to contribute towards participation in a conference.

The following are out of scope of additional funding:
  * childcare and dependant costs
  * direct research costs such as consumables.

### Extension funding
Trust PhD scholarships typically cover 3 years of funding support unless otherwise stated.
If you are in receipt of a full Trust scholarship (full fees and maintenance) you are eligible to apply for up to 6 additional months of maintenance funding to complete your writing-up...

### Conference funding
During the tenure of a fully funded PhD award, scholars can apply for funding towards attendance at one conference up to a maximum of £500.

## Changes to your status
**I have an award from another source. What should I do?**
If you receive another scholarship which meets the full costs of study at Cambridge, such as the University Composition Fee, approved College fees (where applicable) and maintenance sufficient for a single student, you will be given the opportunity to choose which award you will accept.

If you receive a full-cost scholarship from any source other than Gates Cambridge or the Harding Distinguished Postgraduate Scholarship programme, the offer of an award from the Trust will lapse and you will be offered the status of Honorary Trust Scholar.

## Visa and immigration issues
**Am I entitled to a free visa as a Cambridge Trust scholar?**
Most scholars will need to pay for their visa and immigration health surcharge. If your scholarship covers this cost, it will be specified in your offer letter.

**I need to apply for ATAS clearance...**
If you are applying for a PhD/Masters-level course in certain science or technology subject areas and you will be studying in the UK on a visa, you will need to apply for ATAS (Academic Technology Approval Scheme) clearance.

## Coming to Cambridge from overseas
**Can the Trust help me with my travel to the UK?**
If your award covers the cost of travel to Cambridge this will be specified in your award letter... If your award does not provide funding for travel, it is your responsibility to make and pay for all necessary arrangements for travel to the UK.

**My spouse/dependants will accompany me to Cambridge. Does the Trust offer a family allowance?**
The Trust’s awards generally do not cover allowances for a spouse, children or other dependents. You are responsible for obtaining the necessary permission from the University, College and immigration to allow them to join you in Cambridge.

**Can the Trust help me to find accommodation?**
Scholars are responsible for arranging their own accommodation.

## Deferral
Your scholarship must be taken up in the academic year for which it was awarded. Scholarships cannot usually be deferred to the next academic year.
