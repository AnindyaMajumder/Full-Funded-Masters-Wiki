#  Information for applicants
If you apply for a full-time or part-time Master’s or DPhil at Oxford by the relevant December for January funding deadline for your course, you will be automatically considered for a Clarendon scholarship.
For further information on the deadlines for your chosen course, please see the [relevant course page](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/find-your-course).
###  [ Scholarship timeline  ](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/funding/clarendon/applicants/timeline)
Find out more about the application and decision timeline for the Clarendon Scholarships.
###  [ Full-time applicants  ](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/funding/clarendon/applicants/full-time)
Our scholarships are open to all subject areas and to all candidates who apply for a new Master's or DPhil course by the December or January admissions deadline, whichever is relevant to your course.
###  [ Part-time applicants  ](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/funding/clarendon/applicants/part-time)
Clarendon offers a competitive scholarship package for part-time students undertaking a range of courses.
###  [ Selection criteria  ](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/funding/clarendon/applicants/selection-criteria)
Clarendon selection criteria vary slightly depending on the subject area and whether applicants apply for a taught or research degree.
